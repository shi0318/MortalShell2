## Short answer

**Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods** is an optional boss in the retail game, fought inside a **Corrupted Gate**. Players report the gate sits near the **Mushroom Village Gate Beacon**, in the top section of the map, on the eastern side. Beating her unlocks the hidden achievement **Wheelie Good Time**, which **60.2%** of owners have — the highest of any Corrupted Gate boss, so she is the one most players clear first.

She has a **charge attack**. That is not a guess: Cold Symmetry's own Week 1 Update notes list her among the bosses that were fixed, with the line that she "no longer rotates incorrectly during her charge".

The Corrupted Gates are a set of six optional boss arenas, each attached to a named gate beacon. Magdalena's is the most-cleared of the six; the others are [The Lost Child](/bosses/) at 51.6%, The Nameless Captive at 44.0%, Hexapod at 31.7%, Droeg the Conquerer at 31.3% and Sir Isaac the Scholar-Prince at 28.1%. The full breakdown with sources is in [all 53 achievements](/mortal-shell-2-achievements/).

## What changed in the Week 1 Update

The 29 August 2026 patch did not rebalance her damage or her health. The only Magdalena line in the notes is the rotation fix on her charge. That is worth knowing for two reasons:

1. If you fought her **before 29 August 2026**, her charge could track you in ways it was never meant to. Advice written in that first week may describe behaviour that no longer exists.
2. It confirms the charge is a real, distinct attack rather than a general lunge — and given the achievement is called *Wheelie Good Time*, the charge is plausibly the signature move the whole fight is built around. The pun is Valve-published text; the inference about the fight's structure is this site's, and is labelled as such.

## How she was first announced

Magdalena was the endpoint of the **Open Beta** route. The Beta store page told players to explore the early region, cleanse beacons, clear optional dungeons, then press onward toward Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods. In the retail game she is not a story gate at all — she is one of six optional Corrupted Gate bosses. That is a real change in how she sits in the game, and it is why earlier versions of this page, written from Beta copy, described her as an early-path objective.

The Beta itself is closed; the [Open Beta record](/open-beta/) keeps what it granted.
