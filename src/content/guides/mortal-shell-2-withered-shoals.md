The **Withered Shoals** is the waterlogged gate south-west of the Outskirts of Mammon. Giant snails, eels and bear traps line the way to **Hexapod**, and the gate hides one of the three glands you need for the final boss.

![A huge long-legged creature looming over a torch-lit forest path](/images/mortalshell2-gloom-spider-creature.webp)

## Overview

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Entry beacon | Gate of Mammon |
| Gate beacons | Ossinite Falls, Nameless Pass, Hexapod's Burrow |
| Minibosses | Infested Miner; Strange Grisha (optional) |
| Main boss | Hexapod |
| Key loot | Wretchcaller's Stone, Torpor Stone, Dorsalite, Charged Stone, Depleted Gland, Hexapod Core |

## 1. Gate of Mammon – Infested Miner

From the Outskirts of Mammon go south-west through a Bone Portal to the coffin-enemy village and the **Gate of Mammon Beacon**. Inside the **Burrowkin Dwelling** the lift has no lever – go deeper, kill the warden, drop the cliff and check the **hut**: the lever is in a cart of logs. The **Infested Miner** drops rocks in straight lines with its pickaxe; keep moving and chip it down. Shooting the explosive barrels by the broken wheelbarrow opens a gap. Reward: **Wretchcaller's Stone**, Glimpse and smithing materials.

## 2. The Shoals

- An eel comes first, then wooden platforms that summon weak enemies as you cross.
- **Large snails**: parry or block when the mouth opens, stagger them several times, then attack.
- The right-hand path leads to the optional **Strange Grisha** (Grisha Remnant, Strange Remnant) – hit it until it falls over, then unload.
- Brigands and **bear traps** fill the next area; walk, do not roll, through it.

## 3. Ossinite Falls

Clear snails and eels and cleanse the beacon. Walk into the **waterfall** just past it for the **Torpor Stone**. Take the cliff path on the left, then squeeze through the crack in the wall.

## 4. Nameless Pass

Its shell-like beacon looks like a giant snail. Take the **Dorsalite** from the throne, follow the path behind it into the Odious Crevice, and loot the **Charged Stone** from the chest beside the Ova. Back on the surface, before you jump into the hollowed-out tree, land on the underside of its lower trunk and turn around for the **Depleted Gland**. The Proxima support Shell summon is nearby.

## 5. Hexapod

Drop into the fog. Stay behind it, dodge left on the icons, and use the large skeletons as cover from the phase-two projectiles. Reward: **Hexapod Core**; search the fog for the **Hexapod's Burrow** beacon. Full fight: [Hexapod](/bosses/hexapod/).
