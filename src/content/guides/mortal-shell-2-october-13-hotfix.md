Cold Symmetry pushed a new hotfix for **Mortal Shell 2** on **13 October 2026**. The headline for most players is the final boss: **Zmey's HP is lower, your Cosmic Disease resistance is doubled and its grab has more wind-up.** The rest of the list is a long tail of Tarstone, summon and dungeon fixes that matter if you are deep into New Game Plus.

![A gaunt figure stands behind a long table in a dim Mortal Shell 2 hall](/images/ms2-ss-11.webp)

## The final boss changes

| Change | What it means in the fight |
|---|---|
| Final boss HP reduced | Fewer cycles of the phase-two wave attacks |
| Player Cosmic Disease resistance doubled | Breath and wave chip no longer fills the bar as fast – Seedbearer's Scripture is still worth equipping |
| Grab attack has more anticipation | The tail grab is easier to read; dodge forward or right |

Combined with the September 24 tweak to its fire ring attacks, Zmey is now meaningfully easier than at launch. Our [Zmey guide](/bosses/zmey/) has the full strategy.

## Other boss and enemy fixes

- Fixed issues around the main bosses' phase change.
- **Droeg, the Conqueror:** improved hitboxes and a new explosion version.
- **Bor, the Deluded Monarch:** fixed lock-on location and Biosampler interaction.
- **The Monolith:** works better with Proxima's Biosampler.
- **Orrem, the Golem:** triple slam adjusted.
- Necrite run attack timing tweaked.

## Player and gameplay

- **Tiltshot** projectile now spins and moves forward.
- Barrage + Solnir Shard Tarstone no longer damages allies.
- Fixed the **Parasitic Stone** exploit when switching between sheep and Shell form.
- Unequipping **Egon's Stone** can no longer prevent Gloom loss.
- Tarstones now grant EXP for kills by your summons.
- **Black Needle** throw and infused stone can aim and interact again.
- Several Slayer Seal / Punch issues fixed.
- Cancelling **Proxima's** Shell ability no longer stops weapons re-equipping.
- **Gragu** can be possessed again where a bug blocked it.
- Astral Clones attack when summoned near an edge.
- Genessa's Lost Clotstone clones no longer get stuck forever with the Obsidian Hammer.
- **Cursed Child** and Grisha summons are no longer hit by Biosampler or targeted by sidearms, and summons no longer launch big bosses through collision.
- Force feedback tweaks; cleaned-up melee attack logic.

## Dungeons, UI, audio and visuals

- **NG+:** collect three extra Ova in the final slice dungeon.
- Fixed the enemy count on **The Last Siege**.
- Fixed slow-streaming dungeons with "seamless dungeon streaming" enabled, and double dungeon loading.
- **Mether's Breath** now shows multiple warnings; backing out of the second warning no longer soft-locks.
- Audio fixes for Lucian's dodge and Magdalena's unparryable slam.
- New VFX for **Tiel's Shadow Clones** and fixes for Poison Shadow Clones; Giant Spider FX pass; a new Shell summon handshake animation.
- Camera collision improvements, environment fixes, optimisation for castle stairs, mine pillars and tomb floors, and rebuilt shader (PSO) caches and HLODs.

## Where the game stands

On 15 October 2026 Steam shows **17,341 positive of 21,920** reviews across all languages (Mostly Positive) and **8,379 of 10,093** English reviews (Very Positive). The standard edition is $49.99 and there are 53 achievements.
