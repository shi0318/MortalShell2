If your **Mortal Shell 2 Beta save is missing** in the full game, the official rule points to a reset rather than a normal save corruption: Open Beta currency, weapons, Shells, collectibles and other inventory progress did **not** carry over to the retail launch. The Steam community also has a discussion with 18 replies from players asking about the same situation, but that thread does not create a recovery method.

## Short answer: the full Beta save was designed to reset

The Open Beta was an in-development build. Its official Steam description separated two launch rewards from ordinary progress:

- Playing the Beta could unlock the **Flayed Harbinger** cosmetic.
- Progressing beyond **Marrow Keep** could unlock an optional **Prologue skip** in the full game.

The rest of the Beta run was not presented as a full-save transfer. Currency, weapons, Shells, collectibles and other items found in the Beta reset at launch. Therefore, seeing a new retail save is expected under the published rule; it is not, by itself, proof that your Beta files were damaged.

For the original wording and scope, see the [Mortal Shell 2 Open Beta archive](/open-beta/) and the detailed [Beta save transfer guide](/beta-progress/).

## What should carry over?

The official materials identify limited rewards rather than a copied inventory:

| Beta activity | Retail benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Play the Open Beta | Flayed Harbinger cosmetic unlock. |
| Progress beyond Marrow Keep | Optional Prologue skip in the full game. |
| Collect Gloom, Gold, weapons, Shells or collectibles | Reset at the retail launch rather than carrying over as ordinary inventory. |

The Prologue skip is a convenience option, not a transfer of your Beta character's level, map completion, equipment or currency. If you start the retail game without the option, first confirm that you used the same platform account and reached the required Beta milestone; do not expect the Beta inventory itself to appear.

## Why players are seeing a “missing” save question

The Steam discussion that prompted this page is titled **“Completed the Demo version, but in full game don't have the save file.”** That is a real launch question, but a player report cannot override the official reset rule. It also cannot tell us whether a specific account, platform or cloud configuration has a separate technical problem.

Use this distinction:

1. **No Beta inventory in the retail game:** consistent with the official reset rule.
2. **No Prologue-skip option after the required Beta progress:** check the platform account, Beta milestone and current retail build before reporting it.
3. **A retail save disappears after you create it:** that is a different issue and should be documented with platform, save time and any error message.

The retail Steam listing marks the game as supporting Steam Cloud, but cloud support does not turn Beta inventory into transferable retail progress. It also does not promise cross-save between Steam, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## What to check before contacting support

If the missing item is a limited reward rather than ordinary Beta progress, use a short, safe checklist:

- Launch the **full Mortal Shell II** app, not the archived Open Beta app.
- Confirm that the platform account is the one used for the Beta.
- Check that the retail game is updated to the current platform build.
- Confirm whether the item is the Flayed Harbinger cosmetic or the Prologue-skip option; neither is the same as a copied Beta save.
- Record your platform, account region, Beta milestone and retail version before contacting official support.

Do not download a third-party “Beta save converter” or replace a retail save with an unknown file. The official source does not publish a converter, and an unverified file can overwrite progress or create a separate account problem.

## Bottom line

The answer to **“does Mortal Shell 2 Beta progress carry over?”** is **no for the ordinary Beta save**. The official materials describe the Flayed Harbinger cosmetic and the Marrow Keep Prologue skip as limited launch rewards, while Beta inventory and progression reset. For current retail progression, start with the [Mortal Shell 2 beginner guide](/beginner-guide/) and treat the Beta walkthrough as historical context.
