**Mortal Shell 2** launched on **20 August 2026** on **Steam, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S** at the same time. Devout Edition owners on every platform got 72 hours of early access from 17 August.

![A Shell with a curved blade faces long-legged creatures in a pale forest](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-1.webp)

## Platforms and prices

| Platform | Editions | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Steam (PC) | Standard $49.99, Devout $59.99 | 53 achievements; Steam Cloud and controller support |
| PlayStation 5 | Standard, Devout, physical **Revered Edition** ($69.99) | Trophies; system pop-ups no longer pause the game (6 September) |
| Xbox Series X\|S | Standard, Devout; a physical disc was also announced | Achievements |

There is no last-gen (PS4 / Xbox One) or Switch version.

## Do patches arrive at the same time?

Yes. Cold Symmetry ships hotfixes "on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series" together – the 14 September, 24 September and 13 October hotfixes all list every platform. That includes the 13 October final-boss nerf.

## Console-specific notes

- **Performance reports** in Steam reviews are PC reports. Console players mainly mention boss difficulty and the map, not stutter.
- **PS5 screenshots** work properly since the 6 September update stopped system pop-ups pausing the game.
- **Cross-save** between platforms has not been announced.

## Reception

On 15 October 2026 the Steam page shows 17,341 positive reviews out of 21,920 across all languages (Mostly Positive), and 8,379 of 10,093 English reviews (Very Positive).
