**Mortal Shell 2** released **20 August 2026** on **Steam**, **PlayStation 5**, and **Xbox Series X|S**. Steam's standard price is **$49.99**; Devout Edition is **$59.99**.

![Mortal Shell 2 key art](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-1.webp)

## What that means for buyers

Console players get the same launch date as PC. Family Sharing, Steam Cloud, and Steam Achievements are PC store flags; they do not describe PlayStation trophies or Xbox achievements.

If a review talks about stutter, it is almost always a PC report. Console performance is not in the Steam corpus.
