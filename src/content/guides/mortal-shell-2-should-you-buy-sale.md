Mortal Shell 2 costs **$49.99** on Steam (Devout Edition $59.99). Two months after launch, the question is whether to pay full price or wait for a discount.

![An old woman holding an accordion in a firelit room](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-2.webp)

## Where it stands now

On **15 October 2026** Steam shows **8,379 positive of 10,093 English reviews (Very Positive, 83%)** and **17,341 of 21,920 across all languages (Mostly Positive, 79%)**. The game costs **$49.99** (Devout Edition $59.99) and has **53 achievements**.

## Buy now if…

- You liked the first Mortal Shell. This is a bigger version of it: Harden, Shells, dense dungeons, and 30–45 hours to finish.
- You want the patched game. Since launch it has had a Week 1 update, Balance Patch 1, a 6 September content update (Adaptive Difficulty) and three hotfixes – the last on 13 October lowered the final boss's HP and doubled Cosmic Disease resistance.
- You play on console: patches land on PS5 and Xbox at the same time.

## Wait for a sale if…

- You are sensitive to performance hitches on PC. They are better after the 13 October shader-cache rebuild, but traversal stutter is still the most common technical complaint.
- The endgame worries you. Reviews still split on the last stretch; the first half is what earns the recommendation.
- You only want the Devout Edition for cosmetics – the extra $10 is just the Obsidian Skin Set now that early access is over.

## Value per hour

At $49.99 and roughly 35 hours for a first clear, you pay about $1.40 per hour. NG+ and Night Mode extend it considerably for completionists (53 achievements, some requiring NG+).
