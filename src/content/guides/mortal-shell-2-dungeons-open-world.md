## Should you skip optional dungeons?

Not automatically. The official store description places useful progression rewards away from the golden path, so an optional dungeon may be relevant even when it is not required for the next story objective. If you are stuck, explore one nearby branch before assuming the answer is a higher damage number or a harder boss attempt.

## A practical route-planning loop

1. Mark the next main objective on the [Mortal Shell 2 map](/map/).
2. Clear the nearest safe side path and note any Shell, weapon, upgrade, or shortcut.
3. Return to the main route only after you know whether the detour changed your build.
4. Keep a screenshot or written landmark for the way back to a dungeon entrance.

This approach matters because the world is interconnected rather than a sequence of isolated levels. A route that feels like a dead end may become useful after you find a Shell or upgrade elsewhere.

## What Steam does not specify

The store listing does not provide a complete dungeon-by-dungeon map, reward table, or recommended order. This page therefore explains the confirmed structure without inventing names or locations.
