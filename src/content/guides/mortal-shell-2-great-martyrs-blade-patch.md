The **Mortal Shell 2 Great Martyr's Blade** discussion became active around launch because players were asking whether the weapon was underperforming. The official **Balance Patch 1** answer is precise: **Great Martyr's Blade attack damage increased by 20%**. That is a real patch value, but it is not a complete location guide, damage table or proof that the Blade is the best weapon for every Shell.

## What the official patch confirms

Balance Patch 1 lists this weapon change under **Weapons**:

> Great Martyr's Blade Attack Damage **+20%**.

The note does not state a new base-damage number, scaling table, reach value, attack-speed change or upgrade-material cost. “+20% attack damage” should therefore be read as the published damage adjustment, not as a promise of 20% more total build DPS in every situation.

The same patch also reduced the cost of smelting a weapon at the Tarforge by **75% Gloom**. That makes it less expensive to test a different weapon setup, but it does not change the Blade's location or upgrade requirements. For the broader system, see the [Mortal Shell 2 weapons hub](/weapons/) and [Mortal Shell 2 weapon upgrade guide](/mortal-shell-2-weapon-upgrade-guide/).

## Why players were asking if the Blade was bad

The Steam General Discussions thread titled **“Great Martyr's Blade - am I missing something or is it just bad?”** had 29 replies when checked on August 21, 2026. That is enough to establish a real player question and a useful long-tail topic, not enough to turn every opinion in the thread into a universal balance conclusion.

The early debate also shows why a weapon should not be judged from one number alone:

- A slower weapon may feel worse when an attack animation leaves you exposed.
- A weapon's practical result can change with Shell abilities, Tarstones, enemy posture and the current patch.
- Review comments are personal experiences, not a reproducible retail damage test.
- The patch's +20% value is official; claims about “best weapon” remain build- and player-dependent.

## A safe way to retest Great Martyr's Blade after Patch 1

Use a controlled comparison rather than a launch-day impression:

1. Confirm that the game has installed Balance Patch 1 on your platform.
2. Use the same Shell, Tarstones, enemy type and attack input for each comparison.
3. Record ordinary hit damage separately from posture damage, criticals and execution effects.
4. Test both a safe opening and a punish window; a high-damage swing that misses or leaves you trapped is not equivalent to a reliable hit.
5. Keep the result tied to the current build instead of presenting one run as a universal weapon ranking.

The 75% Tarforge cost reduction makes this kind of experiment less punishing than the launch economy described in early discussions. It still does not justify copying an unverified “best build” or a guessed upgrade route.

## What this guide does not claim yet

The checked official sources do **not** establish:

- the exact Great Martyr's Blade acquisition location;
- the complete upgrade-material list or Tarforge cost table;
- a full moveset, scaling sheet or posture-damage table;
- whether the Blade is stronger than every fast or ranged option;
- a universal Shell or Tarstone pairing.

Those details belong on a full weapon-location or build page only after the current retail build verifies them. Until then, the reliable answer to **“did Mortal Shell 2 buff Great Martyr's Blade?”** is **yes: Balance Patch 1 lists a 20% attack-damage increase**.
