**The Wandering Shepherd** is a puzzle boss in the **Glutted Mire**. You do not win by hitting him. You win by hitting his flock – the fight teaches that the moment you swing at the Shepherd himself.

![A hooded villager stands among barrels in a Mortal Shell 2 village](/images/mortalshell2-village-accordion-npc.webp)

## Location

Cross the wooden bridge next to the **Sunken Village Beacon** and follow the path to the farm compound. Some guides list the same encounter as "The Tarblighted Shepherd".

## How the fight works

- Sheep roam the arena. Some turn **black** and attack you.
- Attacking the Shepherd directly makes him teleport away – or **turns you into a sheep**, which leaves you helpless until it wears off.
- Look for sheep that **glow red**. Hitting them fills the Shepherd's Break bar.
- When the bar is full he staggers. Run to him and riposte.
- Repeat. The fight is short once you stop chasing him.

## Rewards and what is next to the arena

- **Sheephead Totem** and Gloom.
- Squeeze through the gap past the arena for the **Etching Needles**, which you need to upgrade Tarstones.
- Ruk and a support Shell summon wait nearby before Magdalena.

## Patch notes involving sheep

- **24 September 2026:** fixed players continuing sheep attacks after transforming back into their Shell.
- **13 October 2026:** fixed the Parasitic Stone exploit when switching between sheep and Shell form.
