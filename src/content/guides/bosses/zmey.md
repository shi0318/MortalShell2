**Zmey, the Unbidden** is the final boss of Mortal Shell 2. It waits beyond the Hidden Nave on The Unfound Path and mixes three status effects – **Burn, Stasis and Cosmic Disease** – into one long fight. If Cosmic Disease fills, you lose your Shell, items, Tarstones and weapon and finish the fight as a frail Harbinger, which is why preparation matters more here than anywhere else.

![A Mortal Shell 2 Shell walking toward a moonlit fortress at night](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-4.webp)

## Before you enter: Seedbearer's Scripture

Seedbearer's Scripture is the only item that resists Cosmic Disease (about 80% resistance in the passive slot). Without it, the phase-two waves are close to unsurvivable.

| Gland | Where it is |
|---|---|
| Depleted Gland | Trunk in Withered Shoals, just before the Hexapod arena (land on the underside of the hollow tree) |
| Thawed Gland | Great hall of the Conquered Temple gate; backtrack from Winterglass Lake Beacon |
| Faded Gland | Chest guarded by dual-wielding dancers near the Citadel Annex Beacon (Faded Citadel) |

Offer the Gloombound Flame at the lantern on top of Marrow Keep, talk to Thestus to call the night, then go to the **Forgotten Tower near Sester's Refuge** and give the three glands to the creature trapped in stone. **Paradoxical Scripture** (dropped by the Prophet of Profane Infinities) cures Stasis and is the other passive worth carrying.

## Reaching the arena

Beat Malborn Offspring, The Monolith and Orrem and sever their Corrupted Ova. The Hidden Nave beacon then cleanses from outside. Take the torch at the campfire, open the chest with the **Endless Core** (it removes the +16 weapon cap at the Tarforge), and offer all of your Gloom at the gate. Spend your Gloom on levels first – you lose it either way.

## Phase one: Stasis

- **Downward sword slashes** leave Stasis zones, often twice in a row. Step out of the zone before you attack.
- **Long-range slash** sends a Stasis shockwave across the arena and can be parried from any distance.
- **Ground slam** (unblockable icon) – dodge backward.
- **Triple stomp** leaves Stasis puddles and has an odd hitbox; Harden the first stomp, then roll out.
- **Tail grab** – the most dangerous attack. Watch the tail shift slightly forward and dodge **forward or to the right**.
- Standing between its legs avoids most sword swings and gives the safest damage.

## Phase two: Cosmic fire

Around half health the blade catches fire and the damage turns Cosmic.

- It opens with **four wave attacks**. Run away from the first set; later sets have to be dodged through.
- Breath attacks build Cosmic Disease even if you parry, so stay out of them entirely.
- A new slide-and-swing combo and the sword plunge (fire waves around the blade) punish anyone standing close at the wrong moment.

## Best Shells

**Tiel** is the community favourite: Shadow Clones and Lingering Shadow pull Zmey off you. Note that its unblockable icons can fail to show when it gets stuck on a clone, so dodge on the animation, not the icon. Vatra's Seal (from the prologue) gives Harden and helps against chip build-up.

## Patch history

- **Week 1:** the tail grab no longer removes Shell and Harbinger at once (still intentional in Night Mode).
- **24 September 2026:** fire ring attacks tweaked.
- **13 October 2026:** Zmey's HP reduced, player Cosmic Disease resistance doubled, and the grab given more anticipation.

Defeating Zmey rolls the ending and unlocks New Game Plus through the siphoning machine in Marrow Keep.
