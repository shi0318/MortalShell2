The **Subjugated Guardian** is the miniboss at the end of **Sentry's Grave**, a dungeon inside the **Lonesome Spire** in the Ruins of Mammon. It drops the **Ballistazooka**, which hits harder than any other sidearm – worth the detour.

![A Shell aims a firearm at a hulking enemy in a stone town](/images/ms2-ss-10.webp)

## Route

From the **Gate of Mammon** beacon go north-west up the first staircase, follow the first level clockwise and take the stairs down into Sentry's Grave.

## How to beat it

- **Ballista Heads** along the route fire at range. Break line of sight between pushes instead of sprinting through.
- The Guardian fires **explosive volleys** at distance. Its projectiles explode on impact, so standing back is the worst place to be.
- **Close in.** Up close it switches to slow, readable **head slams** – block or parry them.
- The surrounding enemies are hard to clear first; focus the boss and keep moving.

## Rewards

| Drop | Notes |
|---|---|
| Ballistazooka | Explosive sidearm; best single-shot damage in its slot |
| Gloom | About 7,000 |
| Coins | — |

Upgrade it at the Tarforge once you have the **Obsidian Lathe** from Martyr's Tomb (south of Widow's Overlook).
