The **Tar Golem** ends the prologue of Mortal Shell 2. It is a scripted fight: whether you drain its health or it drains yours, the story reaches the same point – Harros sacrifices himself and you wake up as the Harbinger. What changes is whether you earn the trophy **"No, You Still Can't Win"**.

![A giant armoured figure swinging two burning flails in a dark arena](/images/ms2-ss-09.webp)

## Location

**Disciple's Grotto**, the prologue's final door. From the Grotto beacon, the path drops to the right, climbs an air shaft on the far side and passes Ruk on the way to a teleport pad. The pad leads to a doorway; interact with the torch at the far end to start the fight. Enter as Harros – if you walk in as the bare Harbinger without reviving Harros, you have to redo it.

## Prepare first

Pick up the **Burnt Effigy** from the optional [Tainted Vestige](/bosses/tainted-vestige/) near the Disciple's Grotto beacon. It cuts the Golem's fire damage. Back up your save (or quit to menu to reset) if you want several attempts at the trophy.

## Attacks

| Attack | Tell | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Four-hit spinning string | Evenly spaced swings | Guard it – it builds Break and Resolve |
| Slow melee swings | Long wind-ups | Parry, then riposte when staggered |
| Shoulder-height double swing / fire charge / slam wave | A glowing mark at its body centre | Roll sideways from the two that travel, backstep the one that lands where it stands |
| Jumping ground smash (plunge) | Plants both axes and leaps | Unblockable – step off the landing spot and punish the recovery |

## Getting the trophy

Keep attacking – stalling only gives it more plunges. Parry normal swings to stagger it, riposte for big damage, and spend Resolve on Harros' petrify skill to lock it in place while you build Break. When its bar reaches zero it drives both axes into the floor, the arena catches fire, and the trophy unlocks even though you "lose".

## Reward

Interact with Harros' stone form afterward for **Vatra's Seal**, which grants Harden – a defence that still works after you commit to a swing. The same Golem returns at the end of the game as [Orrem](/bosses/orrem-the-reclaimed/).
