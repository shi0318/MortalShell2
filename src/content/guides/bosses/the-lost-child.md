## Short answer

**The Lost Child** is a named boss encounter in the Mortal Shell II retail game. Shipped with a placeholder walk animation, which the Week 1 Update replaced. Both post-launch patches touched it, making it the most-adjusted early boss.

![Player fighting a multi-limbed spider-like boss among candles in Mortal Shell II](/images/mortalshell2-spider-boss-fight.webp)

## What the official record confirms

- Has a bite attack whose anticipation was retimed in the Week 1 Update.
- Balance Patch 1: base damage −10%, maximum health −15%.
- Before Week 1 it could launch you or itself across the arena — a bug, now fixed.
- It shipped using a placeholder walk animation; the finished animation was added in Week 1.
- Riposte wobble and an endless blood effect were fixed.
