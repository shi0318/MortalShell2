**The Lost Child** is the main boss of the **Sanguine Caverns** Corrupted Gate in northern Fainweald. For many players it is the first real wall: a huge, bloated creature with enormous jaws, fast bites and a ranged spit. Both post-launch patches softened it, but the second half is still where most runs end.

![A bloated, toad-like creature with bloody jaws facing a Shell in a cavern](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-5.webp)

## Route to the arena

1. Cleanse the **Stonebled Gate Beacon** first – corruption blocks the gate until you beat **Lucian, the Thirsting Knight** inside (stand in the central light so he cannot turn invisible).
2. Cross the collapsing bridges to **Pulsing Caves** (chest: Blackblood Stone) and drop through the hole ringed with crawling stone enemies.
3. Shoot the three cocoons that block the path, cleanse **Sanguine Shoreline**, then use the bone teleporter to the miniboss **Tarblighted Stoner**.
4. Jump onto the white spot after the Stoner to enter the arena. Summon Tiel's support Shell in the cave beforehand if you want help.

## Phase one

- **Left arm swipe** – slow wind-up, easy parry. This is your main damage source.
- **Unblockable bites** – telegraphed; dodge to the side rather than backwards.
- **Retreat and barrage** – when it turns its back it may hop away and spit a volley of projectiles or goop. Dash repeatedly left and right.

## Phase two (below about 60% health)

- **Triple bite** – three snaps that can take you to zero if you block late. All three can be parried or dodged; commit to one answer.
- **Instant hand strike** – the arm swipe loses its wind-up. Harden is safer than a parry here.
- **Jaw inhale** – when the jaws open and it breathes in, leave the direction it is facing to avoid the projectile stream.
- Avoid grabs at all costs.

## Rewards

| Reward | Notes |
|---|---|
| Lost Clotstone | Ability Tarstone (works with the Obsidian Hammer and Veteran's Battle Axe) |
| Child's Feeding Grounds beacon | Appears in the arena; holds several Ova for Marrow Keep |
| Blackmarrow Key | Chest nearby; unlocks a cosmetic shade for a Shell |

## Patch history

- **Balance Patch 1:** base damage −10%, maximum health −15%.
- **Week 1 Update:** replaced a placeholder walk animation, retimed the bite anticipation and removed a bug that could launch you or the boss across the arena.
