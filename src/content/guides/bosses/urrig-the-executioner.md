**Urrig, the Executioner** is the hammer-wielding miniboss of the **Conquered Temple** gate, fought between Winterglass Lake and Droeg. He is built like the Warden-type enemies you have already met but swings a hammer and inflicts **Frost**. His prize, the **Warden's Stone**, is one of the best infusion Tarstones in the game.

![A heavily armoured Shell on a frosty road outside a ruined castle](/images/ms2-hot-7.webp)

## Route

1. In the snowy valley past Winterglass Lake, enter **Mornefrost Grave**, take the right path and loot the crowned body for the **Immaculate Crown**. Stone hands that block paths now move aside for you.
2. At the broken bridge, ring the two bells on the ground and the third bell hidden in fog on the far side (it can be hit from across the gap).
3. Cross the frozen lake – enemies jump out of holes – and vault the log.
4. Past the bats take the right path, then the narrow bridge on the left to its end, and drop into the arena.

## Moveset

| Attack | Answer |
|---|---|
| Single and double hammer swings | Parry – most of his kit is parryable |
| Overhead slam | Parry or Harden |
| **Multi-spin hammer whirl** (unblockable) | Dodge away; it rotates several times, so wait for the last turn |
| Frost on hit | Carry Martyr's Ash from the valley |

## Rewards

**Warden's Stone** (infusion Tarstone that adds Frost to your weapon) and roughly 6,100 Gloom. After the fight, jump on the white spring, cross the burning village and ride the giant elevator up to **Ovate's Chamber**, where Ruk and the Gragu support Shell wait.

## Patch history

The Week 1 Update retuned Urrig alongside The Warden and added new ice attacks to both.
