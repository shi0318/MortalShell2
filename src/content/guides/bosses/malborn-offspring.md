**Malborn Offspring** is usually the first of the three Unfound Path guardians, and many players rate it the hardest of them. It is a humanoid swordsman with a full second health bar, grabs that can kill a lightly armoured Shell outright, and a lightning revival that changes the whole rhythm of the fight.

![A cloaked Shell faces an armoured warrior in a misty Mortal Shell 2 forest](/images/ms2-hot-4.webp)

## Route

From the corrupted beacon platform on The Unfound Path take the left Stone Portal to **Receiving Hall** and clear it (corruption sources replace Ova here). Go back up, climb the stairs and cross the bridges past knife-hand and giant cockroach enemies to **Faithful's Shelter** – its corruption source sits by a small pool. The path on the right leads to the arena.

## Phase one

- **Kick into sword combo** – parry the kick and the follow-up swings.
- **Sword pulled from the ground** – the next swing is unblockable; dodge it.
- After a forced stagger at about 40% health (riposte it), it starts grabbing far more often. **Dodge every grab** unless you are in a heavily armoured Shell such as Lazlo.

## Phase two: lightning

When the first bar empties it revives with a lightning-coated sword, damaging anyone standing next to it. Back off and let it finish standing up.

- Frantic sword spin – leave, do not block.
- Unblockable lightning ground thrusts that make it jump – dodge sideways on the jump.
- Equip a **Galvanized Shard** to cut lightning damage.

## The Genessa method

If parrying every combo is not for you, **Sester Genessa** trivialises much of the fight. With Faithful Doubles at tier 3 and the first column maxed, spam all five Doubles; the Stasis they cause can stun the boss. Run in, hit it to regain Resolve (the Veteran's Battle Axe spin is great for Resolve), and repeat. Before phase two starts, step away, then spam Doubles again once it stands. If Genessa is severed, her Stray form dodges faster; at tier 4 a Stray Double can block a fatal blow and stun – but it does not stop grabs.

## Rewards and known issue

Offspring Trophy (trade item, 5,000 coins) and access to the Corrupted Ovum near Faithful's Shelter. Cold Symmetry has listed a possible bug when using the Grisha Remnant summon in this fight, so leave the summon in your pouch.
