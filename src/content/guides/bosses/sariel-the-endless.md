**Sariel, the Endless** is fought twice in south-eastern Mammon, and the second fight cannot be won by damage alone: as long as the tombstones around the arena stand, Sariel keeps coming back. Beat it properly and you walk away with the **Sariel Shell**, the **Clockwork Scythe** and the **Caged Hystrix**.

![A Shell with a scythe faces a long-legged creature on a frozen plain](/images/ms2-ss-14.webp)

## Fight one: the platform

Activate **The Silent Steps** beacon, head south-east to the large stone platform and use the jump gate to the elevated ledges. Sariel ambushes you in the open area. You only need to take it to roughly **60% health**; it retreats south, phases through a wall and opens the **Chamber of Becoming**.

## Fight two: Chamber of Becoming

Follow it up the steps and push through the dungeon to the inner arena.

- **Destroy all four tombstones** (some guides call them constructs or lightning rods) before you commit. Each one left standing revives Sariel, so health damage before they fall is wasted.
- Move between tombstones and take short trades rather than a long duel.
- Once the last one falls, Sariel is an ordinary scythe fighter: readable swings you can parry or dodge like any other enemy.

## Rewards

| Reward | Where |
|---|---|
| Sariel, the Endless (Shell) | Interact with the remains |
| Clockwork Scythe | Dropped after the fight |
| Caged Hystrix | Climb the stairs at the back – near the dungeon exit |
| Glimpse + Gloom | About 3,500 Gloom |

Merrick in Marrow Keep sells a map that marks the Scythe's location if you want a pointer. The 24 September 2026 hotfix fixed the Clockwork Scythe's elemental effects not applying correctly.
