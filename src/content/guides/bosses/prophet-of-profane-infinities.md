The **Prophet of Profane Infinities** is the miniboss at the bottom of the **Obsidianite Mines** in the Ruins of Mammon. He is one of only three bosses that drop a primary weapon – the **Obsidian Hammer**, the heaviest weapon you can carry – and he also drops **Paradoxical Scripture**, the passive that cures Stasis for the Zmey fight.

![A Shell carrying a long hammer walks toward a shaft of light in a dark mine](/images/ms2-hot-2.webp)

## Route

1. From the **Outskirts of Mammon** beacon head west to the quarry with a large flooded pit (some routes start south of the **Abandoned Outpost** beacon in the slums).
2. Drop into the water / central lower area and find the mine entrance on the western wall.
3. Work through the mine to the large vertical chasm.
4. **Descend ledge to ledge** – do not jump straight down.
5. Past the stone pillars, continue forward to his arena at the bottom of the Deserted Slums.

## Fighting him

The Prophet is a stronger version of the hammer-wielding Prophet enemies in the mines: slow, heavy overheads with long recoveries.

- Parry or Harden the single overheads and punish the recovery.
- Do not trade during multi-swing strings; back out and come in after the last impact.
- His heavy hits break a Shell quickly – this is a good fight to spend Resolve on your Shell ability for a safe opening.
