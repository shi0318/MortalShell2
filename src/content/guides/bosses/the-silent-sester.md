**The Silent Sester** is named once in the official notes – in a riposte-alignment fix for **Proxima** in the Week 1 Update. Because "Sester" is the name of a whole enemy order in Mortal Shell 2, players searching for it usually mean one of the Sester fights below.

![A Shell fights a pale figure on a field of red leaves](/images/ms2-hot-3.webp)

## Every Sester fight

| Encounter | Where | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Twin Sesters → Stray Sester | Sester's Bastion, via the Sester's Gate Beacon (ring the three bells to rebuild the bridge) | Black Needle, Sester's Crest |
| Sester Secundus | Sester's Censer dungeon near Revenant Graves (field of red specters, east of Outskirts of Mammon) | Sester's Censer (unlocks the Genessa Shell), Glimpse |
| Dagger Sester | Inside the Forgotten Tower, reached by jump gate from the Sester's Gate courtyard | Axatana (in a niche) |

## How Sesters fight

- The Twin Sesters resemble the crossbow and rapier Sesters from earlier, with far more health. Kill the ranged one first if you can separate them.
- The Stray Sester that follows plays like the Lance Sesters: committed thrusts with clean parry windows. Focus on parrying.
- Sester Secundus and the Dagger Sester are fast duelists – Harden through the first hit of a string, then dodge.

## The Proxima fix

The only first-party mention of the Silent Sester is that Proxima's riposte animation now lines up correctly on it. If your Proxima riposte looked misaligned on a Sester before 29 August, that was the bug.
