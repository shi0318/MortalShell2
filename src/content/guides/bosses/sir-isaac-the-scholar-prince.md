**Sir Isaac, the Scholar-Prince** is the main boss of the **Faded Citadel**, usually the sixth Corrupted Gate players clear. He is fast, hits hard and rarely gives you a breather between strings, which makes him one of the hardest standard bosses in the game.

![A horned Shell walks through a dark ruined hall in Mortal Shell 2](/images/ms2-hot-6.webp)

## Getting there

Beat the **Starved Harridan** (Vestige of Infinity) for Voltaic Essence, cleanse the **Gate of Infinity** beacon (Gloombound Wraith inside), push through the blue-lantern rooms of the Ashen Sanctum to **Citadel Annex**, defeat **Solnir, the Gloom Seeker** after extinguishing the blue flames, then cleanse **Citadel Atrium**. The bone gate past Ruk leads to Isaac.

## Phase one: sword and shield

- He reacts after you land a hit, with either a sword swing or a **shield bash**. Land one hit, step back.
- Do not stand directly in front of the shield; the bash is quick.
- His single sword swings are easy parries, especially with the **Infinite Seal**, and stuns come quickly.

## Phase two: four swords (around 40% health)

- He drops the shield and his combos get longer and faster. Most swings hit **twice**, so a single parry can fail on the second hit.
- **Harden** is safer than parrying here – it gives you a buffer if the timing slips.
- Wait out the full combo, strike once in the recovery, then back off.

## Shells that make it easier

- **Tiel** – the tier-4 Shadow Clone skill leaves a poisoning clone when you dodge; Isaac attacks it while you hit his back.
- **Sester Genessa** – Faithful Doubles with the first column upgraded distract him and soak hits.
- Lazlo's tanking does little; the double hits break through.

## Rewards

Scholar's Wormstone (Ability Tarstone associated with the Clockwork Scythe), plus the Scholar-Prince's Chamber beacon holding the gate's Ova.
