**The Nameless Captive** is the main boss of the **Prisoner's Domain** Corrupted Gate in south-western Fainweald. It fights with a giant sword embedded in its own head, and the whole encounter is built around parrying – which is also how you earn the trophy **Peter's Perfect Parry**.

![Armoured knights with polearms advance through an underground stone crypt](/images/ms2-ss-08.webp)

## Route

From the Citadel of Penance head south to the Desolate Peak, use the Bone Portals to reach the south-west of the northern region, and cleanse the **Nochtean Gate Beacon** (miniboss: **Vrago, Solikar Champion** – dodge his jump attacks, parry the rest, watch the boomerang wheel). In **Prisoner's Way**, shoot the jars beside the giant statue to break it and climb it, clear the horde at **Hangman's Scaffold**, open the cells and shoot the small plate at **Executioner's Pulpit** (chest: Squall Stone), then beat **Tishina's Confessor** – a white-marked wall to the right of the statue is a shortcut straight to it. The Bone Portals behind the Confessor lead to the Captive. A support Shell can be summoned before the fight.

## Phase one

- **Sword smash x3** – it charges and slams the head-sword into the ground three times.
- **Four horizontal swings** – right-to-left, then left-to-right; the last two are delayed. Parrying both pairs makes phase one easy.
- **Head smashes** (one or three).
- At about 30–50% health it enters a **guaranteed stagger**. Riposte, then get out: it stands up with a fast **fan sweep / 7-hit spin** that can kill in seconds.

## Phase two

- More aggression, plus a **grab that launches you** – dodge it.
- Sword thrusts into the ground release a shockwave as it pulls the blade free; stand off.
- A Grisha summon item can knock it down for free hits.
- Keep your distance during the death animation – it still deals damage.

## Peter's Perfect Parry

Parry **all seven hits of the head spin** in phase two: two slow, four fast, one slow. Make sure its stun meter is empty first so it does not stagger mid-attempt, dodge everything else, and wait for the spin.

## Rewards

Captive's Scabstone (Ability Tarstone associated with the Great Martyr's Blade), about 12,600 Gloom, and the **Captive's End** beacon with the gate's Ova.
