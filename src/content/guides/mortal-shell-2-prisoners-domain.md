**Prisoner's Domain** is the prison-city gate in the south-west of the northern region. It is dense with enemies that fight each other, and it ends with **The Nameless Captive** – a parry-focused boss with a sword in its head.

![Armoured knights with polearms advance through an underground stone crypt](/images/ms2-ss-08.webp)

## Overview

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Entry beacon | Nochtean Gate |
| Gate beacons | Hangman's Scaffold, Executioner's Pulpit, Slumwater Drain, Captive's End |
| Minibosses | Vrago, Solikar Champion; Gloombound Legion; Tishina's Confessor |
| Main boss | The Nameless Captive |
| Key loot | Voltaic Crown, Squall Stone, Confessor's Keepsake, Captive's Scabstone |

## 1. Nochtean Gate – Vrago, Solikar Champion

From the Citadel of Penance go south to the Desolate Peak and use the Bone Portals to reach the Nochtean Gate Beacon. Fight the long-limbed enemies one at a time. **Vrago** waits deeper in after a jump down: dodge his jump attacks, parry the rest, and watch the wheel he throws like a boomerang. Reward: **Voltaic Crown**, two Glimpse and the Ovum.

## 2. Prisoner's Way

Let the factions thin each other out before you engage. Kill the warden at the gate and pull the lever. A note lies on the right side of the path; the left leads to a Resolve root. To go deeper, **shoot the jars beside the giant statue** to break it, then climb it.

## 3. Hangman's Scaffold

A horde fight – the **Gloombound Legion** share one health bar. Shoot the explosive barrel hanging above the battlefield with your sidearm to hit several at once, then cleanse.

## 4. Executioner's Pulpit

- Kill the long-limbed enemy and attack the locks on the cell gates.
- The first cell on the left holds a switch that opens a new path inside the same cell.
- In the hall, avoid the central hole and blow up jars to hurt enemies.
- Shoot the **small plate** next to the locked gate.
- Deeper in, a chest holds the **Squall Stone**; a pressure plate opposite opens the Ova gate.

## 5. Tishina's Confessor

Shortcut: return to before the statue, take the right path and break the **wall with a white marking**. The Confessor swings a scythe – most attacks can be parried, and you can shoot it while it is knocked down. Reward: **Confessor's Keepsake** (charged sidearm shot).

## 6. The Nameless Captive

Through the Bone Portals behind the Confessor. Parry the four horizontal swings, dodge the triple sword smash, riposte the guaranteed stagger and get away from the spin. Parry all seven hits of the phase-two head spin for **Peter's Perfect Parry**. Reward: **Captive's Scabstone** and **Captive's End**. Full fight: [The Nameless Captive](/bosses/the-nameless-captive/).
