**How many dungeons are in Mortal Shell 2?** The official Steam description advertises **more than 80 dungeons**. That is the useful confirmed number for launch planning; it is not a claim that every dungeon has the same size, reward, or boss structure.

## What “more than 80” tells you

The number supports the game’s exploration pitch, but it does not tell you how long a full clear takes. Some dungeons may be short optional branches, while others may be more involved combat spaces. Do not convert the store headline into a promise of 80 large legacy-style levels.

The same Steam description says the world is compact and interconnected, and that players can find Shells, weaponry, upgrades, and curiosities away from the golden path. In practice, the high dungeon count is best treated as a reason to keep mapping landmarks and exits rather than rushing from one main objective to the next.

## How to track dungeons without losing your route

Use the [Mortal Shell 2 map overview](/map/) as a notebook. Record the region, entrance landmark, whether you left an upgrade or Shell behind, and whether the route connects to a known shortcut. A simple checklist will be more useful than trying to remember every unnamed room.

The official source does not publish a complete dungeon list or order. This page will add named entries only when the retail build or an official update supplies verifiable details.
