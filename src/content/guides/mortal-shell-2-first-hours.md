The first hours of Mortal Shell 2 run from the Forlorn Village to your first Corrupted Gate. Here is the order that wastes the least time and Gloom.

![A hooded Shell walks a torch-lit village path in the rain](/images/mortalshell2-rainy-path-combat.webp)

## 1. Prologue (about an hour)

- You wake as the Harbinger. Marigold gives you the **Flower Crown**; touching the egg held by the stone knight lets you inhabit **Harros**.
- Cleanse the **Village Outskirts Beacon** and open the chest for the **Muradean Actuator** – Franz needs it to unlock Tarforge upgrades.
- A green-glowing upgrade item hides under corruption behind the locked gate; find the switch in the room opposite.
- Beat the **Gloombound Ritualist** in the swamp (parry to fill its Break bar).
- Detour for the **Tainted Vestige** below the bridge – its **Burnt Effigy** cuts the Tar Golem's fire damage.
- At **Disciple's Grotto**, crack the wall behind the crates in the Derelict Hideout and cleanse the source.
- Fight the **Tar Golem**. Drain its bar for the "No, You Still Can't Win" trophy; either way Harros saves you and leaves **Vatra's Seal**.

## 2. Marrow Keep

Offer the Ovum at the gates. Inside: Zhirelle (Shells), Franz (Tarforge), Merrick (shop – he gives you the Forgotten Crossbow map free on first talk) and the Gloom Siphon. Take the **north portal**; the corrupted south portal is too hard this early.

## 3. First Shell and first loop

- Fast travel is not open yet. Walk to **Widow's Overlook** and claim **Tiel** from the shallow grave behind the beacon.
- Grab **Proxima** at the Shattered Beacon past Blackridge Pass, and the **Veteran's Battle Axe** in the King's Crypt nearby.
- Siphon five more Ova to earn **Mether's Breath**: fast travel and the ability to clear corrupted growths.

## 4. First Corrupted Gate

The two friendliest starts are the **Glutted Mire** (Vrannic → Magdalena) and the **Sanguine Caverns** (Lucian → The Lost Child). The Lost Child took both a −10% damage / −15% health nerf and a Week 1 animation fix, but it is still the first real wall for many players.

## Spend or save?

Level the Harbinger with Gloom at beacons early – health matters more than anything. Save **Glimpse** for Shell upgrades; marking Shells on the map costs 3 Glimpse, and most early Shells are easy to find without it.
