The first hours of **Mortal Shell 2** are: survive as the Harbinger, take a Shell, light Beacons, then hit an early named boss. **The Lost Child** took both Balance Patch 1 (**-10%** damage, **-15%** health) and a Week 1 animation pass, which is why it reads as the tutorial wall.

![Rainy path in Mortal Shell 2](/images/mortalshell2-rainy-path-combat.webp)

## Practical order

1. Do not spend Gloom until you understand Shell maps from Zhirelle.
2. Light Beacons; they are the retail checkpoint language.
3. Tiel is an early named Shell with its own page if you are hunting a specific body.
4. Magdalena is optional, behind a Corrupted Gate near Mushroom Village Gate Beacon — not the first mandatory fight.
