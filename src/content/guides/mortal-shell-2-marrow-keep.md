**Marrow Keep** is Mortal Shell 2's hub – the fortress you return to between every Corrupted Gate. Almost every system in the game runs through one of its NPCs.

![An old woman holding an accordion in a firelit room](/images/ms2-ss-15.webp)

## Who is in Marrow Keep

| NPC / station | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Zhirelle**, the Shellkeeper | Shell upgrades with Glimpse, marking Shells on the map (3 Glimpse), Shell Severance |
| **Franz** and the **Tarforge** | Weapon upgrades – give him the Muradean Actuator first; sidearms also need the Obsidian Lathe |
| **Merrick** | Shop: consumables, location maps, and the **Rare Findings** tab (Mether's Severance, 5,000 coins) |
| **Gloom Siphon** | Siphon Ova; five more earns Mether's Breath. After the final boss, "Send Ova" starts New Game Plus (no confirmation prompt) |
| **Milos** | Opens The Unfound Path once you have about 160 Ova |
| **Thestus** | Calls the night (Night Mode) after you light the Gloombound Flame on top of the keep |
| **Genessa** | Her Shell, trainer and bond Memories after you deliver Sester's Censer |
| **Ova Nursery** | Leads to the lift, the two portals and later The Unfound Path |

## First visit checklist

1. Offer the Ovum at the gate to get in.
2. Give Franz the **Muradean Actuator** from the Village Outskirts beacon chest.
3. Talk to Merrick – the Forgotten Crossbow map is free on your first conversation.
4. Take the **north portal**. The corrupted south portal is a much harder area.

## Endgame uses

- The Tarforge cap is **+16** until you bring back the **Endless Core** from the Hidden Nave.
- Trade items from the Unfound Path guardians (Offspring Trophy, Monolith Fragment, Orrem's Head) sell for 5,000 coins each.
- In NG+ the keep is your restart point – the prologue is skipped.

## Patch notes

The 6 September update made Zhirelle comment on the corruption in Marrow Keep, stopped Merrick's greeting repeating and the Tarforge talking over itself. The 14 September hotfix fixed multiple Muttons spawning in the hub and Zhirelle not interacting in NG+; 24 September optimised shadows in the hub.
