**Marrow Keep** is the settlement you return to in **Mortal Shell 2**. Community achievement notes place the merchant **Merrick** there selling weapon location maps. The **Tarforge** is the upgrade bench, with a widely cited **+16** cap before a late-game forge item, and Balance Patch 1 cut smelt costs by **75%** Gloom.

![Mortal Shell 2 village](/images/mortalshell2-village-accordion-npc.webp)

## Why the hub matters

Open Beta walkthroughs already routed players back through Marrow Keep after the first region. Retail still uses it as the Shellkeeper and vendor loop: Zhirelle for Shells, Merrick for maps and restocking items such as Mether's Severance.

Treat map pins from Merrick as purchasable hints, not as a free overlay.
