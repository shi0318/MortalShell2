**Seeking the Past** is one of the last achievements most players need in Mortal Shell 2, and for a month it was also one of the buggiest. Here is what it asks for and how to make sure it pops.

![A Shell carrying a long weapon walks toward a shaft of light in a dark hall](/images/ms2-hot-2.webp)

## What it requires

Unlock **every Shell Memory** by maxing the bond with all eight Shells. Memories unlock as you raise a Shell's bond tiers with Glimpse at Zhirelle.

- **Genessa is the trap:** her bond progresses by talking to **Genessa directly**, not to the Shellkeeper. Her five Shell Memories count toward the achievement.
- There is not enough Glimpse in one cycle for every bond. In practice you need **New Game Plus**, where beacon and boss Glimpse rewards respawn.

## The bug and the fixes

| Hotfix | Fix |
|---|---|
| 14 September | Egon's Stone fixes (the stone was involved in the broken trigger) |
| 24 September | "Fixed trophy/achievement issues with Seeking the Past" |

If you had every Memory before 24 September and the achievement did not unlock, update, then open one more Memory (or re-open the Memories menu) to retrigger the check.

## Fastest route

1. Unlock all eight Shells (see [all Shell locations](/mortal-shell-2-all-shell-locations/)).
2. In your first cycle, focus Glimpse on two or three Shells you actually play.
3. Start NG+ and farm the respawned Glimpse from beacons and minibosses (Vrago and the Gloombound Wraith give two each).
4. Talk to Genessa after each bond tier.
5. Use **Mether's Severance** only on Shells you will re-level – it refunds Glimpse but you lose that Shell's tiers until you spend it again.
