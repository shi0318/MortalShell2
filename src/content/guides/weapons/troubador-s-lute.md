The **Troubador's Lute** is the strangest sidearm in Mortal Shell 2: instead of raw damage, it applies **Confusion**, making enemies attack each other for a short time. Balance Patch 1 doubled its attack damage (+100%), the biggest single buff in that patch.

![Troubador's Lute icon from Mortal Shell 2](/images/weapons/troubadors-lute.jpg)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Sidearm – Confusion instrument |
| Region | One-Legged Wolf Tavern, eastern Fainweald |
| Unlock achievement | None |
| Resource | Resolve |

## Where to find it

1. From the Mushroom Village gate, take the bridge north.
2. Cleanse the corruption blocking the path – you need siphoned Ova (Mether's Breath).
3. Follow the path to the tavern, south-east of the One-Legged Wolf beacon, and take the lute to the left of the musician on the stage.
4. **Gragu** is in the same building, so you can start his quest on the same visit.

![A Shell with a lute slung on its back fights on a misty plain](/images/ms2-ss-12.webp)

## How to use it

- Best in corridors and packed camps: play it, step back, and let the room thin itself out.
- Resetting a bad pull costs only Resolve.
- It does little against a lone boss.
