The **Naylshotte** is the starting sidearm – a shotgun-style weapon that fires a spread of nails at close range. Like every sidearm it fires on **Resolve**, not ammo.

![Naylshotte icon from Mortal Shell 2](/images/weapons/naylshotte.jpg)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Sidearm – close-range spread |
| Region | Prologue |
| Unlock achievement | None (starting gear) |
| Resource | Resolve |

## Where to find it

Granted with Harros in the prologue alongside the Iconoclast and Untarnished Seal.

![A Shell aims a firearm at a hulking enemy in a stone town](/images/ms2-ss-10.webp)

## How to use it

- The spread can kill smaller enemies in one shot – use it on jumping devils and the exploding cocoons in the Sanguine Caverns.
- It is the easiest tool for shooting bells (Sester's Gate bridge), plates (Executioner's Pulpit) and explosive jars.
- Keep it as a panic button long after you find the Triarch Repeater or Ballistazooka.
- Sidearm upgrades need the **Obsidian Lathe** from Martyr's Tomb plus the Muradean Actuator at the Tarforge.
