## Short answer

**Black Needle** is one of the thirteen Mortal Shell 2 weapons confirmed by a Valve "Unlock" achievement — Point Taken, unlocked by 38.1% of players.  Its achievement reads "Unlock Black Needle" without the definite article every other item entry uses, which is why the name is often mistaken for a Shell. It is not one: Balance Patch 1 lists it under Weapons.

## What the patches changed

- Balance Patch 1 improved tracking for its light attacks, grouping it with the Axatana and Clockwork Scythe. Shells do not have light attacks, so this settles the Shell-versus-weapon question first-party.
- Has light attacks, therefore a melee weapon rather than a Sidearm.
- No damage values or scaling published.

## Reading the unlock rate

Valve publishes the global achievement rate, which is the only population data available for Black Needle. At 38.1%, it is one of the rarer unlocks on the list — consistent with a late, optional, or well-hidden acquisition.
