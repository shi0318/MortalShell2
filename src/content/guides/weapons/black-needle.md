The **Black Needle** is a spear and one of only three boss-drop primary weapons. Its achievement reads "Unlock Black Needle", which made many players think it was a Shell – it is not.

![Black Needle icon from Mortal Shell 2](/images/weapons/black-needle.jpg)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Primary weapon – spear with throw |
| Region | Sester's Bastion, via Sester's Gate (Ruins of Mammon) |
| Unlock achievement | Point Taken (38.1% of players) |
| Drop from | Twin Sesters → Stray Sester |
| Ability Tarstone | Conqueror's Reward (from Droeg) |

## Where to find it

1. Enter the dungeon from the **Sester's Gate Beacon** (chest of coins at the entrance) and take the elevator down.
2. Broken paths rebuild when you hit bells with your sidearm. The bridge is broken in three places – ring all three bells.
3. In the large arena defeat the **Twin Sesters**, then the **Stray Sester**.
4. The Black Needle drops with **Sester's Crest**.

## How to use it

- Long thrusts outrange most humanoid enemies – fight Sesters and Confessors at the tip.
- Light attack tracking improved in Balance Patch 1.
- It has a throw; the 13 October hotfix fixed the throw and infused stone being unable to aim or interact.
