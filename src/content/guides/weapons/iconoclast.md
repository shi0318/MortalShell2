**The Iconoclast** is the first weapon you hold in Mortal Shell 2 – Harros' light one-handed sword. It has no unlock achievement because you cannot miss it, but it is a genuinely good weapon for the first region and a fine parry-practice blade.

![The Iconoclast icon from Mortal Shell 2](/images/weapons/iconoclast.jpg)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Primary weapon – light one-handed sword |
| Region | Prologue |
| Unlock achievement | None (starting gear) |
| Comes with | Naylshotte sidearm, Untarnished Seal |

## Where to find it

Received automatically during the prologue when you inhabit **Harros**. Nothing to hunt for.

## How to use it

- Fast light attacks are great for stun-locking the multi-legged stone enemies of the Sanguine Caverns.
- It keeps up with parry-riposte play: you are never stuck in a long recovery after a missed swing.
- It is one of the eight melee weapons for **Lord of War** – the others are Axe & Dagger, Veteran's Battle Axe, Great Martyr's Blade, Obsidian Hammer, Axatana, Black Needle and Clockwork Scythe. Sidearms do not count.
- Swap once you find **Axe & Dagger** (Shrine of Trials) or the **Veteran's Battle Axe** (King's Crypt) if you want more damage per hit.
