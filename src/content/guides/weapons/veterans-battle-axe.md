The **Veteran's Battle Axe** is a heavy two-handed axe found at the end of the **King's Crypt**. Its spin attack is a favourite for refilling Resolve, which is why it shows up in so many Genessa and boss-cheese builds.

![Veteran's Battle Axe icon from Mortal Shell 2](/images/weapons/veterans-battle-axe.jpg)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Primary weapon – heavy battle axe |
| Region | The King's Crypt, Blackridge Pass area (Fainweald) |
| Unlock achievement | Cut You Down to Size (59.3% of players) |
| Ability Tarstone | Lost Clotstone (from The Lost Child) |

## Where to find it

1. Travel to Blackridge Pass and head to the **Shattered Beacon** (Proxima's Shell is also here).
2. Go north-west of the Shattered Beacon to the crypt entrance.
3. Work through the traps and enemies to the large square opening near the end.
4. Drop down, defeat the enemy below and take the axe from the dead soldier (some guides describe it as a sarcophagus at the crypt's end).

![Armoured knights with polearms advance through an underground stone crypt](/images/ms2-ss-08.webp)

## How to use it

- The spin attack hits everything around you and returns a lot of Resolve – the core of the Genessa method against Malborn Offspring.
- Heavy weapons received a poise and damage pass in the Week 1 Update.
- Slow recoveries: Harden before you commit a full combo.
