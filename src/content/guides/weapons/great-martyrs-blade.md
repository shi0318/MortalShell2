The **Great Martyr's Blade** is the greatsword of Mortal Shell 2 and the weapon the developers had to buff twice: Balance Patch 1 announced +20% damage that never made it in, and the Week 1 Update made it "even stronger now" to compensate.

![Great Martyr's Blade icon from Mortal Shell 2](/images/weapons/great-martyrs-blade.jpg)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Primary weapon – greatsword |
| Region | Martyr's Prison, Citadel of Penance / Gloomshade Grove |
| Unlock achievement | Big Boi (55.5% of players) |
| Ability Tarstone | Captive's Scabstone (from The Nameless Captive) |

## Where to find it

1. Travel to the **Citadel of Penance** in Gloomshade Grove.
2. Find the entrance to **Martyr's Prison** on the Citadel's eastern side (some routes describe a mysterious coin pouch just outside the Citadel).
3. Interact with the strange coin pouch inside to be transported deeper.
4. Fight through the narrow corridors and prison rooms; the blade waits in the final section.

## How to use it

- Long reach and big poise damage make it the best early stagger weapon.
- Corridors in Martyr's Prison punish wide swings – use thrusts there.
- Pair it with Eredrim for Break-heavy runs.

## Patch history

- Balance Patch 1: announced Attack Damage +20%, which the Week 1 notes admit "didn't actually make it".
- Week 1 Update: deals more damage and is "even stronger now"; heavy weapon poise/damage pass.
