Search results often list **Weltcap** next to the weapons because the Week 1 Update grouped it with weapon changes ("stronger in both its active and passive forms"). In the game it is a **mushroom** – a regular inventory item that the marsh-dweller **Gorf** asks you to find.

![A Shell kneels in swamp water near a pale robed figure](/images/mortalshell2-swamp-serpent-encounter.webp)

## Where it grows

1. Start at the **Widow's Overlook Beacon** and head north toward the combat-trial area.
2. Do not start the trial – turn right into the eastern marsh. **Gorf** sits in the open (you may hear him coughing).
3. Talk to Gorf, then follow the path toward a bridge and onto it.
4. Go down to the lower bridge approach. Near its base is a distinctive tree with exposed roots; the **Weltcap** grows among them.

No combat is needed. Do not go to Mushroom Village for this – the key item there is something else.

## Gorf's trade

| You give | You get |
|---|---|
| 1 Weltcap | **Marsh Pearl** – no use of its own, sells to vendors for 2,000 coins |

Keep spare Weltcaps; Gorf asks for more later.

## Active and passive forms

The patch wording means the mushroom has an effect when consumed (active) and while carried (passive). Both were strengthened on 29 August 2026. Exact values were not published, so treat it as a small bonus rather than a build piece.
