The **Forgotten Crossbow** is the first sidearm most players find after the Naylshotte. It trades spread for range and precision.

![Forgotten Crossbow icon from Mortal Shell 2](/images/weapons/forgotten-crossbow.jpg)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Sidearm – crossbow |
| Region | Flooded Village, north-west of Blackridge Pass (Fainweald) |
| Unlock achievement | Old School (63.9% of players) |
| Resource | Resolve |

## Where to find it

1. Cross the rope bridges to the lower crags where the Spear Maidens patrol.
2. Break the wooden boards on the stone archway to reach the tower entrance (the arch is an enchanted doorway you can walk through).
3. Press the wall switch to lift the cage and ride the elevator down – it collapses on arrival.
4. Clear the underground village, pulling Toxic Stalkers one at a time.
5. Take the **Damp Key** from the box beside the back-left house.
6. Unlock the middle building and take the crossbow from the table.

Merrick gives you this map for free on your first conversation.

![A Shell fires a crossbow at enemies in a sunlit castle courtyard](/images/ms2-ss-13.webp)

## How to use it

- Snipe crossbowmen and casters on ledges before you engage.
- With the **Shuddering Stone** Tarstone it can knock Hexapod back mid-lunge.
