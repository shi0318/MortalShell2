**Rotten Autumn** went live on **October 20, 2026** on Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox. Cold Symmetry's own note calls it a free update to the core game plus a handful of seasonal items. It is not a paid season and it is not a new campaign you buy separately. If your install is current, the new dungeons, the photo mode, and the shell shades are already in the build.

Mortal Shell II has been out since **August 20, 2026**. On October 20 the Steam listing was still **$49.99** with no discount. Reviews were Mostly Positive: **17,558** positive out of **22,172**. That is a different argument from "is the Halloween update real." The update is real. The price did not move because of it.

## The list, without the trailer voice

The highlights in the studio post are specific enough to plan a session around:

- Photo mode.
- A new alternative soundtrack by Sakis Tolis of Rotting Christ.
- **15** new dungeons.
- New shell shades for each shell.
- **10** new Tarstones, including one sheep-specific Tarstone.
- New lighting presets for Nightmode.
- An Overkill graphics setting.
- A Harbinger pumpkin helmet.
- An Impervious lute skin and additional lute tracks.
- A ghost of a lost shell that haunts the land and offers cryptic notes.
- Additional polish and bug fixes.

Fifteen dungeons is the line that changes a weekend. A pumpkin helmet does not. If you only have an hour, open photo mode, equip one new shade so you can see the shell you are wearing, and leave the helmet for later. If you have an evening, the dungeons are the content.

The same post includes a joke you should not turn into a route: "despite whatever you've heard — there is no sheep dungeon." The sheep-specific Tarstone is real. A sheep dungeon is the thing they say is not in the update. Do not spend the night looking for a pen that the patch notes already denied.

## What to do in the first hour

Start in the shell you already understand. Rotten Autumn adds shades, not a new found Shell with a new health bar. A shade is a look. Harden, parry, and the weapon you cleared the last dungeon with are still the kit. Change the shade after you confirm the new dungeon entrance is on your map, not before.

Photo mode is the second stop, not the first. The update is live on three platforms, and the first thing a seasonal patch breaks is sometimes the menu, not the fight. Open photo mode once, take one shot, leave. If it opens, it opens. If it does not, you have a bug report instead of a lost evening in a dungeon you have not mapped.

Then pick one of the 15 dungeons, not all of them. The note does not publish a recommended order, a level requirement, or a loot table. Treating "15 new dungeons" as a checklist you must clear tonight is how you bounce off a free update. Clear one. Write down whether the enemy density feels like the launch dungeons or like something meaner. Then decide if the other fourteen are this week's plan.

Nightmode players should open the new lighting presets before they judge the seasonal dark. The post lists new presets for Nightmode as their own bullet, separate from the dungeons. If Nightmode was already a visibility problem, the preset is the setting to try. The dungeon is not a fix for a lighting complaint.

## Tarstones, the lute, and the ghost

Ten new Tarstones is a build change, not a cosmetic. One of them is sheep-specific. The notes do not print the other nine effects in the highlight list, so do not invent a tier list from the trailer. Equip one, fight one pack, and see whether the passive is doing the thing the item text says. A Tarstone you have not procced is not a build.

The Impervious lute skin and the extra lute tracks are for the player who already uses the lute. They are not a combat buff. The alternative soundtrack is a separate toggle in spirit: it is a Rotting Christ score, not a difficulty modifier. Turn it on if you want the seasonal sound. Turn it off if you are trying to hear a boss tell you already learned in August.

The ghost of a lost shell "offers cryptic notes." That is the whole mechanical claim in the highlight list. Cryptic notes are not a map marker and not a guaranteed rare drop. If you find the ghost, read the note and move. Do not clear the same room ten times because a seasonal ghost might be a vendor. Nothing in the post says it sells anything.

## What this update does not change

The October 13 hotfix is still the note to reread if a fight feels different from launch. Rotten Autumn's own post says "additional polishes and bug fixes" without listing a boss-health nerf. Do not credit a pumpkin helmet for a damage change that belongs to an earlier hotfix. If a swing that used to kill you now doesn't, check which patch you are on before you rewrite your Harden timing.

Steam still lists **53** achievements. On October 20 the common end of the global board was high: achievement **001** at **93.7%**, **003** at **91.5%**, **002** at **84.5%**. The thin end included **039** at **8.7%** and **040** at **13.7%**. A free dungeon pack can move the rare numbers if the new achievements are tied to the new rooms. The highlight post does not name new achievements. Do not assume the pumpkin helmet is an achievement. Look at the in-game list after the update installs.

The price is still **$49.99**. A free update does not make a Mostly Positive game cheaper. It makes the current install larger. Minimum spec on the store page is Windows 10 or 11, an i7-10700K or Ryzen 5 3600, an RTX 2060 SUPER 8 GB or RX 6600 8 GB, and an SSD. Overkill is a new graphics preset. If you are already under the minimum, do not enable Overkill to "see the update." Enable it on a machine that was already holding 60 frames, and only after the dungeons load.

## A session that respects the patch

1. Update on Steam, PS5, or Xbox. The post says the update is live on all three.
2. Open photo mode once. Confirm it is not a crash.
3. Equip one new shell shade so you can tell your shell from the old screenshots.
4. Enter one of the 15 dungeons. Leave when the bag is the prize, not when you are lost.
5. Try one new Tarstone in that dungeon. Unequip it if the passive fights the weapon you already like.
6. Ignore the sheep dungeon. The notes say it is not there. The sheep Tarstone is the sheep content.

[Harden](/mortal-shell-2-when-to-harden/) still spends on the swing you cannot read. Rotten Autumn did not replace that rule with a helmet. The new rooms are worth the install. The seasonal jokes are not a route.
