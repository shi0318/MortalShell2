## What a reset is useful for

Use a reset to answer a focused question: does this Shell perform better when you invest in survivability, posture pressure, or a weapon interaction? Change only the abilities connected to that question, then replay a familiar encounter. This keeps the test readable and avoids confusing a build change with a route or timing problem.

The official Steam listing says the sequel has eight Shells, each tied to innate abilities. A reset therefore supports comparison between roles; it does not remove the reason to explore for additional Shells.

## A safe experiment routine

Record your current Shell, weapon, fragments, and the encounter you are testing. Make one change, fight the same enemy group, and note whether the benefit was damage, control, healing, or survivability. If the change fails, revert it rather than rebuilding the entire character around one short fight.

Do not spend resources assuming every retail option is free. The checked evidence supports the player’s report about Shell abilities, while exact refund rules for weapons, fragments, and other systems remain unverified.
