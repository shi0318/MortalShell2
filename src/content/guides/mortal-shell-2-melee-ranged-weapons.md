## Melee weapons are not the only upgrade path

Melee is the obvious way to pressure posture, but a ranged option can change how you begin an encounter, finish a weakened target, or create space while a group is repositioning. Do not assume that a ranged weapon is only a backup. Its value depends on ammunition, recovery, enemy distance, and how safely it lets you choose the next exchange.

## A simple first-build rule

Keep one reliable melee weapon as your reference point and test ranged damage separately. If you change the Shell, weapon, and fragments at the same time, you cannot tell which change helped. Use the same enemy group or a repeatable route, record the result, and only then spend more upgrade resources.
