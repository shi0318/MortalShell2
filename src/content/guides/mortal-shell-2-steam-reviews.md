## What positive Mortal Shell 2 Steam reviews are praising

The first-day sample repeatedly highlights the game's core loop rather than only its presentation:

- Shells feel distinct and give players different playstyle options.
- Weapons and build testing are a major reason players are exploring the map.
- Combat and boss designs are frequently described as strong.
- Several players praise the atmosphere, world design, music and story presentation.
- Some players report stable performance and no meaningful crashes on their systems.

One positive reviewer reported **23.4 hours** and said they had cleared the map of Shells and weapons to test builds. Another described the game as a major improvement over the first Mortal Shell after **19.1 hours**. Those are individual play sessions, not a claim that every player will have the same result.

## Performance feedback is mixed, not universal

The launch-day review sample includes both stable-performance reports and isolated problems. One reviewer described the game as well-optimised with only one large frame drop in their session. Another short review reported that it was good but crashed a few times.

The safe PC conclusion is therefore:

- The official requirements are a hardware floor, not a guaranteed frame-rate result.
- A crash or stutter report from one player does not prove a universal bug.
- A clean session from another player does not prove every configuration is safe.
- Compare reports against GPU, CPU, SSD installation and the current retail patch before applying a fix.

For hardware planning, use the [Mortal Shell II PC Requirements](/pc-requirements/). For Beta-only reports, keep using the [Beta performance checks](/mortal-shell-2-beta-performance-fixes/) and do not merge those observations into retail benchmarks.

## Launch complaints players should know about

One detailed negative review raises three specific issues. First, the player criticises long boss runbacks, including a reported **two-plus-minute** run from an open-world beacon to a dungeon. Second, the review describes inconsistent enemy damage, where apparently small attacks can remove a large amount of health. Third, it reports very wide enemy aggro ranges that can pull distant enemies into group fights.

These are important search intents for a guide site, but they remain a detailed player's account rather than official patch notes. The article does not turn them into universal rules. Until multiple current-patch reports confirm the same behaviour, treat them as things to watch for during your own run.

## What the first-day reviews say about buying Mortal Shell 2

The early sample is broadly enthusiastic about combat, Shell identity, weapons and atmosphere, while also asking for balance, quality-of-life and bug fixes. A fair launch-day answer is that the game is attracting positive hands-on reactions, but the sample is too new to make a stable long-term score claim.
