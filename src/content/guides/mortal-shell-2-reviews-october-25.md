On **October 25, 2026**, the US Steam page listed Mortal Shell II at **$49.99** with no discount shown. English reviews were **Very Positive**. The page counted **9,890** reviews for the game. In the last 30 days, **84%** of **1,243** reviews were positive.

That is the English store page, not an all-language export from earlier in October. Use this number when you are looking at the button today. Do not paste it over a different filter and call the change a comeback or a collapse. The label on this page is Very Positive. The price is still full.

The game has been out since **August 20, 2026**. Rotten Autumn has been live since October 20, with 15 new dungeons, photo mode, and a seasonal score. The 30-day line includes players who installed that update and players who did not. Check your build before you treat 84% as a review of the pumpkin helmet.

## What 9,890 and 1,243 are

**9,890** is the English all-time count on that page. It is large enough that one thread will not flip the label. **1,243** in 30 days, at **84%**, is a real month, not a 100-review wobble. Very Positive on both the recent slice and the all-time line is the clean reading. People are still finishing fights and still recommending the game two months later, including the weeks around the seasonal update.

It does not mean every dungeon in Rotten Autumn is mapped. The highlight list named 15 dungeons and did not rank them. It does not mean Overkill is safe on a minimum GPU. It does not mean the sheep dungeon exists. The same post that added the sheep Tarstone said there is no sheep dungeon.

Harden is still the combat rule. A Very Positive month does not parry for you. Spend Harden on the swing you cannot read. Take the pumpkin helmet off if it hides your weapon arc. Turn the seasonal score off if it buries a tell. Those are menu choices. The score on the store page will not make them for you.

## Who the price is for today

**$49.99** with no discount, next to Very Positive and a busy 30-day line, is a full-price recommendation. Buy it if you want the shell combat and you are willing to update before you judge a new room. The 15 dungeons are free on top of the game you already own. They are not a reason for a new owner to skip the prologue.

Wait if you need a sale. The October 25 page did not show one. A Very Positive label is not a coupon.

Skip the sheep hunt either way. Equip the Tarstone if the item text is worth a slot. Do not search a field for a pen the update denied.
