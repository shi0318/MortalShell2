There is no stamina bar to manage. **Resolve** and **posture** are the resources. A slow weapon is not "out of stamina." It is a long recovery you eat if the enemy is still swinging.

That is why the [weapons hub](/weapons/) ranks feel, not a hidden DPS sheet. HUD damage numbers are not a published stat block. Do not build a tier list out of one screenshot.

## How to swing with that rule

- **Axatana** and **Black Needle** sit at the top of player write-ups because one is a swap between heavy and fast, and the other keeps you at poke range. Neither needs a stamina cancel.
- **Obsidian Hammer** sits at the bottom of those same write-ups because the recovery is long. Harden can cover one hit during that recovery. It cannot cover three.
- Starters **Iconoclast** and **Naylshotte** are the prologue pair. Learn Resolve on those before you hunt a late weapon. Locations are on [all weapon locations](/mortal-shell-2-all-weapon-locations/).

Upgrade at the Tarforge in Marrow Keep if you want the weapon to keep up. The cap players treat as advertised on a first run is +16. A later chest that goes past that is a report, not a plan.
