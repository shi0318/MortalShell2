**Adaptive Difficulty** arrived in the 6 September 2026 update (1.000.009) as an **experimental, optional** setting. It is off by default. Turn it on under **Game → Game Settings → Adaptive Difficulty**. It is not a Hard mode with a new name: it watches what you defeat and where you die, then moves enemy strength in both directions.

![A Shell fights a pale figure on a field of red leaves](/images/ms2-hot-3.webp)

## What it changes

| You are… | Enemies get | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Winning consistently | More damage, more health, more Break resistance | Can push past the original toughest difficulty and keeps climbing while you keep winning |
| Struggling / dying in the same place | Less damage, less health, less Break resistance | A cap limits how much of your health bar one attack can remove, so you cannot be killed outright from full health |

Adjustments **fade over time**, so the game drifts back toward standard balance if you stop feeding it wins or deaths. **Night Mode is never made easier** by the system, though it can still be made harder.

## The indicator

At launch the setting had no on-screen indicator. The **24 September hotfix** added UI support for Adaptive Difficulty tiers and an improved icon (and made the Night Mode icon easier to see), so you can now tell which way the game has tilted.

## When to turn it on

- You are stuck on one early boss (The Lost Child, Magdalena) and want the run to keep moving.
- You do not care about comparing numbers with friends – two players with the setting on will not fight the same boss.
- You want a harder second half without starting New Game Plus.

## When to turn it off

- **Learning tells and parry windows.** A shifting health pool makes riposte maths and "how many hits" advice useless.
- **Achievement attempts** that depend on a fight going a specific way (Peter's Perfect Parry, the Tar Golem trophy).
- **The final boss.** Zmey was already nerfed on 13 October (lower HP, doubled Cosmic Disease resistance). Learn the fight at a stable number.

It does not replace Shell choice, Harden or weapon upgrades – it sits on top of them. The same update also widened the window for **consecutive Infinite Seal parries** and let status effects apply during riposte recovery, which helps more than the slider does.
