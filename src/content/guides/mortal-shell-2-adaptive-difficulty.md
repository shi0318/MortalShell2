The post-launch update added an optional setting called Adaptive Difficulty, and it is marked experimental. It is not a Hard mode with a new name. It watches what you defeat and where you die, then moves enemy strength in both directions.

That second half is the part people miss. If you are dying in the same corridor, the setting can relax the fight. If you are clearing rooms without trouble, it can push back. Leaving it on and then comparing notes with a friend who left it off will not produce the same boss.

## When to turn it off

Turn it off if you are learning a tell. A moving health pool makes a riposte timing guide useless, including the ones that assume the Week 1 riposte scaling and nothing else. Turn it on if you want the run to stop stalling in one dungeon and you do not care about a stable number.

It does not replace Shell choice, Harden, or a weapon upgrade. It sits on top of those. The [combat page](/combat-system/) still describes the verbs. This page is only the new slider.
