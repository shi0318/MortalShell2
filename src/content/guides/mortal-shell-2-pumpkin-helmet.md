The **Harbinger pumpkin helmet** is on the **Rotten Autumn** highlight list, live **October 20, 2026** on Steam, PS5, and Xbox. It is a seasonal cosmetic. The post does not call it a Tarstone, a shell, or a dungeon key. Wear it when the screenshot is the point. Take it off when you need to see your own weapon arc.

Mortal Shell II is **$49.99**, Mostly Positive at **17,558** of **22,172** reviews that day. A helmet will not move a two-month-old score. It will waste a new-dungeon evening if you treat it as the content. The content is **15** new dungeons. The helmet is the costume for the photo you take after one of them is clear.

## What a cosmetic is allowed to change

A helmet can hide the top of the swing you use to time a parry. That is a visibility cost, not a stat. If the arc you watch is your own weapon, and the pumpkin covers it, the helmet is wrong for that fight. Unequip it. The seasonal item will still be in the menu after the boss.

A helmet does not change Harden. Harden still spends on the attack you cannot read. A helmet does not change which shell you are in. Rotten Autumn added shades for each shell, not a ninth shell. The pumpkin is closer to a shade than to a found body. Put it on the shell you already understand. Do not learn a new shell and a new hat in the same room.

Photo mode is the right place for it. Open photo mode after the room is empty. The update does not say photo mode pauses every aggro. A pose in a live pack is how a cosmetic becomes a death. [Photo mode and Overkill](/mortal-shell-2-photo-mode-overkill/) is the settings page. This page is only the hat.

## What it does not unlock

It does not unlock a sheep dungeon. The same post says there is no sheep dungeon. There is a sheep-specific Tarstone. If a video puts the pumpkin and the sheep in one thumbnail, the thumbnail is merging two jokes. Test the Tarstone by reading its item text. Photograph the helmet in a cleared room. Do not search a field for a pen.

It does not replace the October 13 hotfix. If a boss feels different from August, check that hotfix before you credit a hat. Rotten Autumn says it includes polish and bug fixes. It does not reprint a boss-health table in the highlight list. A pumpkin is not a nerf.

It is not an achievement unless the in-game list says so after the update. Steam lists **53** achievements. **001** was **93.7%** on October 20. **039** was **8.7%**. The highlight list does not name a helmet pop. Look at the list. Do not grind a costume for a guess.

## A wearing rule

| Moment | Helmet |
| --- | --- |
| Cleared room, photo mode open | On |
| First pull in a new dungeon | Off |
| You time parries off your own weapon arc | Off |
| Village, lute, seasonal score | On, if you want the bit |
| Boss corridor | Off until you have seen the tell without it |

Shell shades can stay. A shade is a color. A helmet is geometry in front of the camera. Change one of them at a time. If you die and both are new, you learned nothing.

The alternative soundtrack is the other seasonal toggle. Mute that too if you are learning a tell. One new dungeon, one old weapon, no hat, old score. That is a clean first clear. The hat is the reward, not the loadout.

Mostly Positive players already liked the fights. The pumpkin is for the players who came back for October. Let them have it after the room is safe. The **$49.99** game underneath still kills you the same way if you cannot see the swing.


## One more pass before you close the menu

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. Write down the result in the game's own words. Then decide whether the next door, the next bind, or the next purchase is the one this page was about.

If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.

A shade can stay on while the helmet comes off. Color is not geometry. The Harbinger name is on the October 20 list next to the helmet, which is why people will search the word as if it were a shell. It is not one of the eight shells. It will not change a respec. It will not open a sheep pen the developers denied in the same paragraph. Photograph it. Unequip it. Enter the next new dungeon with the weapon arc visible. The $49.99 fight is still the fight. The hat is the costume you put back on when the room is empty and photo mode is the only thing left to do.
