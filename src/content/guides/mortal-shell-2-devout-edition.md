The base game on Steam is $49.99, app 2584270. The Devout Edition upgrade is a separate app at $10.99. Adaptive Difficulty, the Egon's Stone fix, Proxima's animation fix, and Mether's Severance are on the free hotfix list. Buying the upgrade does not install them, and not buying it does not lock you out of them.

Buy the upgrade if you want whatever bonus that edition advertised when you preordered. Do not buy it because a last-boss guide mentioned an item. That item is in the game. Do not buy it to fix a dungeon that will not load.

If you already own the first Mortal Shell, you still do not need it installed to play the sequel. The [editions page](/editions/) splits the boxes. This page is the October check: patch first, edition second.
