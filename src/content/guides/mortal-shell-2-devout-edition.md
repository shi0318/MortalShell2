Mortal Shell 2 sells in two digital editions on Steam. The **Standard Edition is $49.99**; the **Devout Edition is $59.99**, and owners of the standard game can buy the Devout upgrade separately. Here is what the extra money buys – and what it does not.

![A horned figure walks through a dark Mortal Shell 2 ruin](/images/ms2-hot-6.webp)

## Edition comparison

| Edition | Price | Contents | Pre-order bonus at launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $49.99 | Base game | Skeletal Harbinger Skin, Obsidian Hanger Skin |
| Devout | $59.99 | Base game + **Obsidian Skin Set** | Same skins + 72 hours of early access (17 August) |
| Revered (physical) | $69.99 | Deluxe content + collector's box, artbook, fine art prints, steelbook | — |

The early access window is over – the game launched for everyone on 20 August 2026 – so today the Devout Edition is a **cosmetic** upgrade.

## What the Devout Edition does not include

- **No gameplay items.** Every weapon, Shell, Seal and Tarstone is in the base game. The optional item that makes the last boss manageable (Seedbearer's Scripture) is crafted in-game.
- **No patches.** Adaptive Difficulty, the Egon's Stone fix, Proxima's Biosampler fixes, the Mether's Severance fixes and the 13 October final-boss nerf are free updates for everyone.
- **No soundtrack** is listed in the edition contents.

## Should you upgrade?

Buy it if you like the Obsidian look on your Shells or want to support the studio. Skip it if you were hoping for content, a bug fix or an easier final boss – none of that is behind the upgrade. If you are waiting for a sale, the base game is the purchase that matters.

Steam reviews on 15 October 2026: 17,341 positive of 21,920 across all languages (Mostly Positive); 8,379 of 10,093 in English (Very Positive).
