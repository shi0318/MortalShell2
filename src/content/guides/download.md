## What the Open Beta included

From the official Steam Open Beta description:

- Begin as the **Harbinger** and possess Shells
- Explore the early open-world section
- Cleanse **beacons**
- Enter optional dungeons and face hidden mini-bosses
- Progress toward **Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods**
- Discover **Tiel, the Acolyte** (one of eight playable Shells)

Full breakdown: [Open Beta guide](/open-beta/).

## Progress rules that applied at launch

- Full Beta inventory **reset** (currency, weapons, Shells and collectibles).
- Playing the Beta unlocked **The Flayed Harbinger** cosmetic.
- Progress beyond **Marrow Keep** unlocked an optional **prologue skip** in the full game.

Details: [Beta save transfer](/beta-progress/).
