Mortal Shell 2 has **16 pieces of gear for your weapon slots**: eight melee weapons and eight sidearms. One of each comes with Harros in the prologue; the rest are split between **Fainweald** and the **Ruins of Mammon**. None are missable – free roam stays open after the final boss.

![A Shell aims a firearm at a hulking enemy in a stone town](/images/ms2-ss-10.webp)

## Melee weapons

| Weapon | Region | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| [The Iconoclast](/weapons/iconoclast/) | Prologue | Starting weapon with Harros |
| [Axe and Dagger](/weapons/axe-and-dagger/) | Fainweald – Shrine of Trials | Chapel Key from the plateau near Mushroom Village, solve three rooms, beat the Armored Knight |
| [Veteran's Battle Axe](/weapons/veterans-battle-axe/) | Fainweald – King's Crypt | North-west of the Shattered Beacon; end of the crypt |
| [Great Martyr's Blade](/weapons/great-martyrs-blade/) | Fainweald – Martyr's Prison | Coin pouch at the Citadel of Penance; end of the prison |
| [Obsidian Hammer](/weapons/obsidian-hammer/) | Mammon – Obsidianite Mines | Drop from the Prophet of Profane Infinities |
| [Axatana](/weapons/axatana/) | Mammon – Forgotten Tower | Jump gates from Sester's Gate; beat the Dagger Sester |
| [Black Needle](/weapons/black-needle/) | Mammon – Sester's Bastion | Ring three bells, beat the Twin and Stray Sesters |
| [Clockwork Scythe](/weapons/clockwork-scythe/) | Mammon – Chamber of Becoming | Beat Sariel, the Endless |

Collecting all eight melee weapons unlocks **Lord of War**.

Collecting all eight sidearms unlocks **Guns. Lots of Guns** (each one also has its own achievement).

## Suggested order

1. **Fainweald first:** Axe and Dagger and the Forgotten Crossbow are the two most-collected items on Steam (64.7% and 63.9%), then the Veteran's Battle Axe and the Great Martyr's Blade.
2. **Mether's Breath** (five more Ova siphoned at Marrow Keep) opens the east: Troubador's Lute and the Gragu Shell in one trip.
3. **Mammon** in any order; do Sariel's two fights in one trip for the Scythe and the Hystrix.

## Upgrades

All weapons upgrade at the **Tarforge** in Marrow Keep (give Franz the Muradean Actuator from the Village Outskirts beacon). Sidearms also need the **Obsidian Lathe** from Martyr's Tomb, south of Widow's Overlook. The normal cap is +16; the **Endless Core** in the Hidden Nave removes it. Merrick sells location maps for several weapons.
