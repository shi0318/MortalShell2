Mortal Shell II's October 30 Rotten Autumn hotfix targets several problems that can make an exploration session feel unfinished even after you win the fight. Sideways can be entered again, Killer Ritual receives encounter-completion tracking fixes, and particular missing-item and quest-delivery problems are addressed. The efficient response is a targeted revisit, starting from the save you already have. This guide explains how to organize that revisit, what to inspect before repeating an encounter, and when to stop treating a progression symptom as a build problem.

## Start with one unresolved objective
List the problem in concrete terms: a dungeon entrance will not accept re-entry, an encounter appears incomplete, a reward is absent, or a journal cannot be delivered. These are separate tasks. Choose one before leaving a familiar checkpoint and note the objective text visible in the current save. If you have not played since the seasonal update arrived, install the available update first. Replaying a long route on an older build adds no useful evidence about a fix you have not actually received.

Inspect your equipment and supplies before the revisit, especially if you changed shells during your previous attempt. The same hotfix includes several shell and weapon fixes, and it is easy to mistake a missing melee weapon for a dungeon-specific restriction. Confirm the current loadout through the inventory and perform a safe attack before departure. You are preparing to inspect progression, not conducting a high-risk damage experiment. Bring a setup you already understand so that unfamiliar resource behavior does not distract from the objective you are trying to resolve.

## Re-enter Sideways through its normal route
The hotfix allows players to enter the Sideways dungeon again. Return through the normal entrance rather than searching for a clipping method or an unofficial save edit. Before crossing, check the name and surroundings so you do not confuse a nearby route with the dungeon you intended to revisit. Enter once, allow the transition to complete, and check whether the interior loads correctly. A successful re-entry answers the access question even if you still have additional exploration to complete inside.

If the entrance still fails, record what happens when you interact: no prompt, an inactive interaction, an unexpected transition, or a loading failure. Those observations point to different issues. Do not keep hammering the interaction while a transition or checkpoint save is active. Return to the nearest safe state through normal play if possible, then retry once. If the same problem persists, preserve the sequence and platform information. Repeating the entire campaign or deleting the profile should not be the first reaction to an entrance problem that now has a specific fix.

## Verify Killer Ritual completion deliberately
Encounter tracking in the Killer Ritual Dungeon is corrected by the update. On a revisit, pay attention to the encounter's actual completion feedback rather than assuming the last enemy's death automatically resolves every objective. Check for remaining enemies, delayed events, and the objective state shown by the game. Take a moment after the fight ends before moving through the next transition. This is a verification routine; it does not imply a hidden final enemy or an extra ritual step beyond what your current encounter displays.

If the encounter remains incomplete, note exactly which feedback is missing. Did the enemies stop appearing? Did the exit remain closed? Did a reward fail to appear despite completion feedback? Keep those distinctions clear. A completion tracker problem and a missing loot issue require different reports and may respond to different revisits. Resist the temptation to invent an extra requirement because an older forum comment guesses one. Your goal is to establish whether the updated save registers the completed encounter, using visible state and a reproducible route.

## Check missing rewards in the inventory first
The Wellspring Tarstone could previously fail to enter the inventory. After updating, inspect your Tarstones and search carefully before repeating the associated activity. A reward that was overlooked in a crowded list is different from a reward that was never granted. Read item names and effects rather than relying on the icon you remember. If the game offers relevant sorting options, use them to make the search easier. Keep a record of what you found and what remains missing before planning another expedition.

The hotfix also addresses duplicated drops in certain areas. Do not build your return route around reproducing a duplication trick, and do not assume that fewer duplicate items now means a legitimate reward has been removed. Compare the objective and item you need with the inventory you actually have. If a completed activity still fails to provide its expected reward, describe the exact encounter and item rather than reporting only that the loot feels worse. A precise missing-item description is much more useful than an unsupported general claim about reduced rewards.

## Deliver Rickart's Journal without mixing quest problems
Ruk is adjusted to appear at the top of Marrow Keep so Rickart's Journal can be delivered. Confirm that the journal is present and the relevant objective is active before making the trip. Then go to the stated location and inspect the interaction normally. Do not substitute a different character with a similar name or assume an old location remains correct. The point of this revisit is a specific delivery, so keep the journey focused until you know whether the conversation can proceed.

If Ruk is present but the delivery does not advance, record the dialogue options, current objective, and whether another event occurs first. Presence and quest completion are two different checks. Leave the conversation through the normal interface and inspect the journal again before concluding that an item disappeared. Avoid repeatedly changing equipment or traveling across several unrelated regions while trying to diagnose one conversation. Keeping the sequence short makes it easier to tell whether the remaining obstacle is the journal state, the interaction, or a different prerequisite visible in your save.

## Leave the dungeon in a clean state
The hotfix improves dungeon transitions, including checkpoint saving and memory handling. Let transitions finish and avoid forcing a shutdown merely because an entry fade is slower than expected. Once the objective resolves, leave through the intended route and check the persistent state after the next normal checkpoint. If the game indicates a save is occurring, let it finish before ending the session. This is basic save discipline, especially useful when your original problem involved a completion state failing to persist.

End by updating your unresolved-objective list. Remove what the revisit actually fixed and keep the remaining symptom narrow. “Sideways opens, Killer Ritual clears, journal delivered” is far more actionable than “the patch seems better.” If something still fails, preserve a short sequence showing the current build, entrance or encounter name, visible objective, and outcome. A targeted revisit lets the hotfix recover value from your existing campaign while keeping exploration enjoyable. You should spend the next session discovering a dungeon, rather than repeatedly wondering whether the previous one counted.
