The **Mortal Shell 2 leveling system** does not map neatly onto a traditional strength, dexterity, or intelligence menu. A public launch review describes progression as permanent growth to the main stats, while Shell abilities and weapon fragments provide much of the build experimentation.

## What the launch discussion says

One public Steam review says the leveling system initially felt unusual because it did not offer a conventional pure-strength path. The same review says that, after finding more Shells, weapons, and fragments, the player could experiment more freely and reset Shell abilities without a fee.

That is a player account, not a published developer stat table. It is still useful for search intent because it answers the question new players ask when the first upgrade screen does not behave like another Soulslike.

## How to plan your first upgrades

Do not spend your first session trying to recreate a build from the original Mortal Shell. Start by identifying whether your current problem is survivability, posture pressure, recovery, or weapon reach. Then test one Shell or weapon change at a time so you can tell which part of the kit helped.

Steam describes eight Shells scattered through the world and says their innate abilities can be awakened. That means exploration is part of progression, not a separate collectible checklist. If a route feels difficult, looking for another Shell or upgrade may be more productive than forcing the same encounter.
