The **Glutted Mire** is the poison swamp behind the **Mushroom Village Gate** in the east of Fainweald. It is usually the first or second Corrupted Gate players clear, and it ends with **Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods** in her burning cart.

![A woman in a spiked cart sets the swamp ablaze in Mortal Shell 2](/images/mortalshell2-swamp-cart-boss.webp)

## Overview

| Detail | Value |

## 1. Mushroom Village Gate – Hall of Illusions

Cleanse the beacon to enter the Hall of Illusions. Many dead ends open once you pass the ritual gates; hit the illusory wall beside the Resolve tree to reveal the path. In the room with no exit, look up – **Vrannic** stands on the planks above and drops you into the fight. Parry the first two hits of his three-hit combo, dodge the third and the long thrust. His Ovum opens the gate.

## 2. Into the swamp

- Dash past the **arrow tripwires** on the approach.
- Many enemies are poison users. **Tiel** with a raised bond reduces poison and can even heal from it.
- In the first village, light **three candles** to open the chest: one on a fence outside a house, one on a small stone pillar near the ritual spot, one in a cage guarded by a poison enemy. The chest holds the **Siegebreaker's Stone**.
- Past the village, **Seeping Mire** makes you fight a horde at once – the **Circle of the Grasping Root** in Rootwater Hollow.

## 3. Sunken Village

Cross the wooden bridge into the eel-filled settlement and follow the path to the cliff edge and the **Sunken Village Beacon**. Inside: clear the ground floor, take the stairs, beat the big hammer enemy near the end and drop off the cliff. The chest holds the **Corroded Stone**; the Ovum is just beyond. Back on the surface, grab the **Gland** near the cultist group.

## 4. The Wandering Shepherd

Across the bridge from Sunken Village. Do not hit the Shepherd – hit the **red-glowing sheep** to fill his Break bar, then riposte. Behind the arena: **Etching Needles** (needed to upgrade Tarstones), Ruk and a support Shell summon.

## 5. Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods

Drop onto her platform in a secluded part of Sunken Village. Bring the **Burnt Effigy**. Stay behind her cart – the front roller makes charges unblockable – and back off when she spins fire. At half health the cart breaks down and she summons babies once; kill them, then punish. Reward: **Magdalena's Memento** and the **Magdalena's Glade** beacon with Ova. Full fight: [Magdalena boss guide](/magdalena/).
