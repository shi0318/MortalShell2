The **Conquered Temple** is the frozen gate east of the Outskirts of Mammon. Frost is the theme from the first valley to the boss, so carry **Martyr's Ash** – the valley is full of it.

![A Shell in heavy armour on a frosty castle road outside ruined towers](/images/ms2-hot-7.webp)

## Overview

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Entry beacon | Sester's Gate |
| Gate beacons | Winterglass Lake, Ovate's Chamber, Conqueror's Hoard |
| Minibosses | Twin Sesters / Stray Sester; Caged Grisha; Urrig, the Executioner; Tyrant's Sycophants |
| Main boss | Droeg, the Conquerer |
| Key loot | Black Needle, Sester's Crest, Immaculate Crown, Thawed Gland, Fusillade Stone, Warden's Stone, Frostshard Stone |

## 1. Sester's Gate

From the Outskirts of Mammon head south-east through the red leaves and Sester's Refuge to the **Sester's Gate Beacon** (the Abbey Entrance beacon is near the Refuge). Inside: coins at the entrance, an elevator down, and a bridge broken in three places – ring the three bells on the ground with your sidearm. In the arena beat the **Twin Sesters** and then the **Stray Sester** for the **Black Needle** and **Sester's Crest**.

## 2. Valley of the Revered and Winterglass Lake

Snowy valley, hard-hitting enemies that freeze you – one parry staggers them. Grab **Martyr's Ash** and, in the great hall, the **Thawed Gland** (one of the three glands for Seedbearer's Scripture). In **Mornefrost Grave**, take the right path and loot the crowned body for the **Immaculate Crown** – stone hands that block paths now move aside. Beat the frost **Caged Grisha** (Fusillade Stone) to cleanse Winterglass Lake.

## 3. Broken bridge and Urrig

Ring two bells on the ground and a third hidden in the fog on the far side. Cross the frozen lake (enemies leap out of holes) and vault the log. **Urrig, the Executioner** waits past the brigands: parry most hits, dodge the multi-spin hammer. Reward: **Warden's Stone**. Then jump on the white spring, cross the burning village and ride the giant elevator to Ruk, the Gragu support summon and **Ovate's Chamber**.

## 4. Droeg, the Conquerer

Cleanse Ovate's Chamber (brigands and frost zombies), then take the traversal gate across the gap. Parry the club sweeps, dodge the delayed smash, and hide behind the left stone slab or right tree when he curls up and fires orbs. Reward: **Conqueror's Reward**. Full fight: [Droeg](/bosses/droeg-the-conquerer/).

## 5. Conqueror's Hoard

Many Ova wait inside. A passage behind them leads to a beacon-like shrine – pray to open the path. The blizzard damages you in the open; move from bonfire to bonfire, cross the wraith field and enter the giant skull's mouth. The chest at the end holds the **Frostshard Stone**; a warp point returns you to the beacon.
