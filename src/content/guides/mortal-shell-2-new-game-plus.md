**New Game Plus** in Mortal Shell 2 is called up from Marrow Keep after the final boss. It keeps almost everything you own and makes every enemy tougher.

![A towering stone guardian lit by fire in a dark arena](/images/mortalshell2-cathedral-guardian-boss.webp)

## How to start NG+

1. Defeat **Zmey, the Unbidden**. You can keep exploring afterward.
2. Go to the **Gloom Siphon** in Marrow Keep and choose **Send Ova**.
3. There is **no confirmation prompt** – the ending plays and the new cycle starts in Marrow Keep, with the prologue skipped.

## What carries over and what resets

| Carries over | Resets |
|---|---|
| Harbinger level, Gloom, Glimpse, coins | Ovum count |
| All eight Shells, Shell Points, bond tiers, Memories | Corrupted Gates and dungeons (reseal) |
| Weapons and sidearms at their upgrade level | Beacons, except two starting ones (Fainweald, Mammon) |
| Tarstones, Seals, materials, quest keys | NPC quests (killed NPCs return) |
| Tarforge and Mether's Pulse upgrades | Opened chests refill |
| Fast travel and the revealed map | Your map markers |

Weapons, sidearms and Tarstones you already own do not respawn – their pickup spots give upgrade materials instead. Revered Glands, Synaptic Vessels and beacon/boss Glimpse rewards do respawn.

## How it is harder

Enemies and bosses have more health, deal more damage and are more aggressive. Movesets are the same, but punish windows are shorter. **Night Mode** stacks on top for even more health, damage and speed – and more Gloom and coin drops.

## NG+ patch notes

- **Week 1:** the Wandering Shepherd's sheep respawn properly.
- **6 September:** fixed Baghead's quest lockout in NG+.
- **14 September:** Zhirelle interacts again in NG+.
- **13 October:** you can collect **3 extra Ova** in the final slice dungeon.

## Before you press Send Ova

Finish anything you want on this cycle – the two prologue achievements cannot be earned after the transition. You need NG+ for 100% because **Seeking the Past** requires maxing every Shell bond.
