**Sester Genessa, the Wayward** is the secret Shell. There is no body to find – you earn her by retrieving her censer. Once unlocked she controls fights from range with summoned Doubles and, uniquely, does not sever when her health runs out.

![A horned figure walks through a dark Mortal Shell 2 ruin](/images/ms2-hot-6.webp)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Region | Eastern Ruins of Mammon (Revenant Graves) and Marrow Keep |
| Requirement | Defeat Sester Secundus, deliver Sester's Censer |
| Unlock achievement | Sester (33.9% of players) |
| Playstyle | Summoner, range control |

## Abilities

- **Faithful Doubles** (active): summons Doubles that perform a weapon attack and vanish. Upgrading lets you field up to five.
- **Duality** (passive): when her health is depleted she becomes **Stray** instead of being severed from the Harbinger. Stray Genessa dodges faster.
- Tier-4 skill: a Stray Double can block a fatal blow and stun the attacker.

## How to unlock

1. Travel to the **Outskirts of Mammon Beacon** and jump to the eastern landmass.
2. Find the field of red, flickering specters near Revenant Graves and interact with the point among them.
3. Defeat **Sester Secundus** in the separate arena and take **Sester's Censer**.
4. Fast travel to Marrow Keep, give the Censer to Genessa and complete the Memory.
5. For bond progression, talk to Genessa directly, not the Shellkeeper – her five Shell Memories count toward the **Seeking the Past** achievement.

## How to play it

- Doubles are the cheese strategy for Malborn Offspring and help a lot against Sir Isaac.
- The 14 September hotfix let Mether's Severance be used at the Genessa trainer; 24 September fixed Stray Genessa not being interactable after Severance.
- Doubles copy your weapon – a heavy weapon (Obsidian Hammer) makes them hit much harder; the 13 October hotfix fixed Lost Clotstone clones getting stuck with the hammer.
