**Smert, the Apostate** is the high-risk Shell. He gambles with his own health, fights unarmed in a stance once upgraded, and gets stronger the closer he is to death. Week 1 patch notes describe him as having a "permanent fight stance" that can destroy projectiles.

![A gaunt figure stands behind a long table in a dim Mortal Shell 2 hall](/images/ms2-ss-11.webp)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Region | Prophet's Rest, near Outskirts of Nochte (western Fainweald) |
| Requirement | Fill the blood-offering circles |
| Unlock achievement | My Brether (49.1% of players) |
| Playstyle | Risk/reward, unarmed Chaos |

## Abilities

- **Miracle** (active): halts time and enters **Fight Stance**; unarmed attacks apply **Chaos** stacks that detonate when time resumes. You can push it into **Overtime** at a Health cost.
- **Deadly Revelation** (passive): at 10% Health or lower Smert gains **Faith**; while Faith is active, melee hits restore extra Resolve.
- **Chaos** applies a random effect when it expires. Smert also has a unique dodge animation.

## How to unlock

Start at the **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon** and clear enough cultists to work safely. At an empty circle choose **Make Blood Offering** – it forces you out of your Shell into Harbinger form, so reclaim your Shell before the next one. Fill every circle, then interact with Smert's body.

## How to play it

- Open boss windows with Miracle and dump Chaos stacks before time resumes.
- Overtime is strongest when you are already low – which is also when Deadly Revelation is active.
- Keep Harden and a Revered Gland ready; his risk is real against grabs (Malborn Offspring, Zmey).
