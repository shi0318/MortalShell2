## Short answer

**Smert** is one of the eight playable Shells in Mortal Shell 2. Built around a stance rather than a cooldown ability: the Week 1 patch notes describe Smert as having a "permanent fight stance", which no other Shell is described that way.

![Mortal Shell II character portrait of a masked woman in red with gold jewelry](/images/mortalshell2-masked-shell-portrait.webp)

## How we know it is a Shell

Week 1 Update notes, Shells section

## Confirmed facts

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Unlock achievement | My Brether |
| Global unlock rate | 49.1% |
| Acquisition | Found near Zhirelle, the Shellkeeper, like most Shells; the location can be tracked by spending Gloom (community-reported). |

## Abilities on the record

- Permanent fight stance (named in the Week 1 Update notes)
- Can destroy projectiles while in that stance
- No stats or numbers published.

## What is not published

No stat sheet, Resilience value, or ability cooldown for Smert has been published by Cold Symmetry. Numbers appearing in community videos are player measurement, not developer data.
