**Sariel, the Endless** is a Shell that wants to be hit. Damage turns into Pain, Pain turns into speed and Curse, and enough Curse makes the next enemy attack bounce back. You earn him by beating him – twice.

![A Shell with a scythe faces a long-legged creature on a frozen plain](/images/ms2-ss-14.webp)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Region | The Silent Steps / Chamber of Becoming, south-eastern Mammon |
| Requirement | Defeat Sariel, the Endless twice |
| Unlock achievement | The Alchemist (35.2% of players) |
| Playstyle | Pain conversion, Curse, reflection |

## Abilities

- **Exodus of Thorns** (active): expels thorns that hunt nearby enemies and inflict **Curse** stacks. Each use inflicts **Pain** on Sariel.
- **Purge** (passive): with no Pain, 50% of incoming damage becomes Pain; at maximum Pain Sariel dodges faster.
- **Curse**: at high stacks, the target's next melee attack is nullified and reflected.
- **Pain** converts some health into **Grey Health**, which vanishes if you are hit but can be regained with melee attacks.

## How to unlock

Activate **The Silent Steps** beacon, use the jump gate to the elevated ledges and drive Sariel off in the open area (about 60% health). Follow it into the **Chamber of Becoming**, destroy the four tombstones/lightning rods during the rematch, then kill it and interact with the remains. The fight also drops the **Clockwork Scythe**, and the **Caged Hystrix** sits near the exit.

## How to play it

- Stay aggressive to win back Grey Health.
- Fire Exodus of Thorns early in a fight to stack Curse before the boss's first big string.
- As a support summon, Sariel draws aggro brilliantly – especially against Orrem.
