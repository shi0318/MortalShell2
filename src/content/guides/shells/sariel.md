## Short answer

**Sariel** is one of the eight playable Shells in Mortal Shell 2. New name from the retail achievement list. Its achievement is titled "The Alchemist", which hints at a theme but confirms no mechanic.

![Player confronting a tattooed bear-headed enemy with antlers in Mortal Shell II](/images/mortalshell2-bear-shaman-encounter.webp)

## How we know it is a Shell

Community achievement guide only — no first-party mention

## Confirmed facts

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Unlock achievement | The Alchemist |
| Global unlock rate | 35.2% |
| Acquisition | Found near Zhirelle, the Shellkeeper; the location can be tracked by spending Gloom (community-reported). |

## Abilities on the record

- Nothing published by the developer — Sariel is absent from every official announcement.
- Players report a pain-conversion mechanic where damage becomes pain and pain heals on hit; unverified.

## What is not published

No stat sheet, Resilience value, or ability cooldown for Sariel has been published by Cold Symmetry. Numbers appearing in community videos are player measurement, not developer data.
