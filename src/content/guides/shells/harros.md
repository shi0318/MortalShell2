**Harros, the Vassal** is the ninth Shell and the first one you play. He carries you through the prologue and is lost at its end – which is why he does not count toward the eight-Shell **Shell Seeker** achievement.

![A Shell with a sword wades through a swamp toward a pale robed figure](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-3.webp)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Region | Prologue (Forlorn Village → Disciple's Grotto) |
| Requirement | Automatic |
| Achievement | None – not part of Shell Seeker |
| Starting gear | Iconoclast, Naylshotte, Untarnished Seal |
| Fate | Sacrifices himself after the Tar Golem |

## Abilities

- Harros fights with the **Iconoclast** (a light one-handed sword) and the **Naylshotte** sidearm (a close-range shotgun spread).
- His Resolve skill petrifies an enemy in place, which lets you build Break safely – very useful against the Tar Golem.
- He starts with the **Untarnished Seal**, whose Perfect Guard has a wide timing window.

## How to unlock

You wake in the Forlorn Village as the Harbinger. A stone knight holding a large egg speaks to you; touching the egg starts a vision with UnderMether, who explains that the knight is Harros. Walk to Harros in the vision to inhabit him. If you are knocked out of him during the prologue, deal melee damage to enemies to return.

## How to play it

- Learn the parry on Harros – the prologue enemies (shield bearers, the halberd knight) are designed for it.
- Cleanse the **Village Outskirts Beacon** for the **Muradean Actuator** (give it to Franz for Tarforge upgrades).
- At the Tar Golem, drain its bar for the trophy; either way Harros saves you and turns to stone, leaving **Vatra's Seal**.
