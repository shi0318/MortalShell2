Beyond the ten main bosses, Mortal Shell 2 has dozens of named minibosses. They hold most of the game's **Tarstones**, several Shells and weapons, and the Glimpse you need to mark Shells on the map. Clearing them is also how you finish the Tarstone achievement.

![A giant armoured figure swinging two burning flails in a dark arena](/images/ms2-ss-09.webp)

## Priority kills

1. **Bloodcursed Lithopod** – opens the Citadel of Penance (Eredrim, Great Martyr's Blade) and the Trebuchaxe.
2. **Tainted Vestige** – Burnt Effigy before the Tar Golem.
3. **Prophet of Profane Infinities** – Paradoxical Scripture for the final boss.
4. **Starved Harridan** – Voltaic Essence is required to reach the Faded Citadel.
