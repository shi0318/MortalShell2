Mortal Shell 2 has **eight Shells** you can keep, plus **Harros**, who carries you through the prologue and is lost at its end. Five are in Fainweald, three in the Ruins of Mammon and Marrow Keep. Collect all eight for the **Shell Seeker** achievement.

## Finding Shells on the map

Spend **Glimpse** at the Shellkeeper's room in Marrow Keep (3 Glimpse) to mark a Shell's shadow on your map. Glimpse is scarce – many minibosses drop one each – so only mark the Shells whose routes below you cannot follow.

## Fainweald

- **Tiel:** fast travel to Widow's Overlook, turn around, follow the dirt path to the yard and touch the body in the shallow grave.
- **Proxima:** cross the ravine to Blackridge Pass, head north-east past the Treant and clear the Shattered Beacon dungeon.
- **Eredrim:** open the Citadel of Penance with the Citadel Gate Lever (Bloodcursed Lithopod), take the elevator in the locked arena and beat The Warden.
- **Smert:** at Prophet's Rest near Outskirts of Nochte, "Make Blood Offering" at every circle (each one throws you out of your Shell – reclaim it before the next).
- **Gragu:** with Mether's Breath, talk to him at the tavern, fetch the Heart of Vatra from the corrupted statue at the top of the eastern temple grounds, and give it to him.

## Ruins of Mammon

- **Lazlo:** High Lord's Courtyard → keep → Royal Crypt → beat Vellen → chamber behind the arena.
- **Sariel:** Silent Steps → drive it off → Chamber of Becoming → break the tombstones → kill it.
- **Genessa:** from Outskirts of Mammon jump east, interact among the red specters, beat Sester Secundus for Sester's Censer and give it to Genessa in Marrow Keep. Talk to her directly for bond Memories (they count for **Seeking the Past**).
