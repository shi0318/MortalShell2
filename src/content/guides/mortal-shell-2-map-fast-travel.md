People who like the combat still complain about the map. The note that keeps showing up is specific: locations are turning corridors with strange connections, and fast travel does not make the knot obvious. That is a layout complaint, not a "git gud" complaint.

## What actually saves time

- Unlock the beacon or checkpoint you just cleared before you chase the next orange mark. A death run through an unmapped loop costs more than the fight.
- When two corridors look identical, drop a Gloom pile or a body as a breadcrumb only after the Egon's Stone hotfix. Before that hotfix the stone could delete open-world Gloom.
- Do not treat the world map as a dungeon map. The interior connections are the part that lies.

The [map page](/map/) is the landmark list. This page is the habit: clear, plant the travel point, then explore the spoke. Adaptive Difficulty will not straighten a corridor.
