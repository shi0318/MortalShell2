Players who love Mortal Shell 2's combat still complain about its map. The complaint is specific: interior routes twist back on themselves and the world map does not show how corridors connect. Here is how fast travel actually works and the habits that save time.

![A heavily armoured Shell on a road outside ruined castle towers](/images/ms2-hot-7.webp)

## How fast travel unlocks

You cannot fast travel at first. Siphon about **five more Ova** at Marrow Keep to earn **Mether's Breath**. It unlocks fast travel between cleansed beacons and lets you clear corrupted growths in the overworld. The 6 September update added icons for smaller beacons in the Mether's Breath menu, and the 13 October hotfix added extra warnings when you use Mether's Breath (backing out no longer soft-locks).

## The travel network

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Beacons (49) | Checkpoints; cleansed ones are fast-travel points. Three start pre-cleansed: Marrow Keep, Widow's Overlook, Outskirts of Mammon |
| Return to Marrow Keep | Available from any cleansed beacon menu |
| Bone Portals | Fixed teleports across regions (e.g. Citadel of Penance to Desolate Peak) |
| Jump gates / springs | One-way leaps across ravines (Forgotten Tower, Silent Steps) |
| Map fragments | Fill in the map; Merrick sells maps marking specific items |

## Habits that save time

1. **Cleanse the beacon you just reached before exploring the spoke.** A death run through an unmapped loop costs more than the fight.
2. **Use Glimpse markers sparingly.** Marking a Shell costs 3 Glimpse; buy Merrick's maps for weapons instead.
3. **Follow Ruk.** After the 6 September update he appears at map stations when you warp to an overlapping beacon.
4. **Treat Corrupted Gates as levels.** Each has 3 beacons in a straight line; the last one appears only after its boss.
