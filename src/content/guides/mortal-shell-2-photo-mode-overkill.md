**Photo mode** and **Overkill** both arrived in the **Rotten Autumn** update on **October 20, 2026**. They are not the same tool. Photo mode is how you frame a shell. Overkill is a graphics preset. Turning one on does not improve the other, and neither one changes Harden, parry, or the **15** new dungeons except by making the machine work harder while you walk into them.

Mortal Shell II is **$49.99** on Steam with no discount that day, Mostly Positive at **17,558** of **22,172** reviews. A seasonal graphics preset is not why that score exists. The score was already Mostly Positive before the pumpkin helmet. Use the new tools after the update installs, not as a reason to rebuy the game.

## Photo mode, in practice

The studio post lists photo mode as the first highlight, live on Steam, PS5, and Xbox. It does not publish a control card in that note. So the first session is a test, not a shoot.

Open it in a place you already know: the shell you cleared the prologue in, with the weapon you can name. Take one shot. Leave the mode. If the game returns to combat, photo mode is safe enough to use between pulls. If it does not return, you have a bug, and the 15 dungeons can wait until you are back in control of the character.

What to shoot, if the mode holds:

- The new shell shade, before and after, so you can tell the seasonal look from the launch look in your own screenshots.
- The Harbinger pumpkin helmet, once, so you know whether it hides the tell you use to read your own swing. A helmet that covers the weapon arc is a cosmetic you take off before a boss.
- One of the new lighting presets in Nightmode, if you play that mode. The post lists those presets separately. A dark shot that hides the enemy is a bad preset, not a moody one.

Do not open photo mode in the first room of a new dungeon while a pack is still aggroed. The update does not say photo mode pauses every encounter. A mode you have not tested is not a pause button. Clear the room, then frame the shell.

## Overkill is a preset, not a reward

Overkill sits with the new graphics settings in the same highlight list. It is optional. The store page's minimum PC line is still an i7-10700K or Ryzen 5 3600, an RTX 2060 SUPER 8 GB or RX 6600 8 GB, Windows 10 or 11, and an SSD. The recommended block on the US store page was empty when checked on October 20. That absence matters. Do not invent a "recommended for Overkill" card the store did not print.

If your machine is on the minimum line, leave Overkill off for the first new dungeon. Fifteen new rooms are a loading and streaming test. A new high preset on top of a new dungeon pack is how a free update becomes a stutter. Play one dungeon on the preset you used in August. If that holds, then try Overkill in a cleared room, not in a boss corridor.

Console players do not get a PC preset to argue about. The update is live on PS5 and Xbox as well. Photo mode is the shared tool. Overkill is the PC graphics name in the notes. Do not hunt a menu entry that belongs to the other version.

## Nightmode presets are the third toggle

New lighting presets for Nightmode are their own bullet. If you already turned the sun off, open the preset list before you blame the seasonal dungeons for being unreadable. A new dungeon in a new lighting preset is two changes at once. Change one.

A workable order:

1. Load the shell. Confirm the shade you want.
2. If you use Nightmode, pick one new preset and walk a road you already know.
3. Enter one new dungeon on your old graphics preset.
4. Only then try Overkill, in a room with no aggro.
5. Open photo mode after the room is clear.

That order keeps a crash or a frame drop attached to the change that caused it. Opening photo mode, Overkill, Nightmode, and a new dungeon in the same minute makes the bug report useless.

## What not to expect from a screenshot mode

Photo mode will not show you the sheep dungeon. The Rotten Autumn note says there is no sheep dungeon. The sheep-specific Tarstone is an item. Photograph the Tarstone if you want. Do not photograph empty fields looking for a pen the developers already denied.

Photo mode will not raise an achievement by itself. Steam lists **53** achievements. On October 20, **001** was at **93.7%** and **039** was at **8.7%**. The Rotten Autumn highlight list does not name a photo-mode achievement. If one exists, it will be on the in-game list after the update. Do not grind poses for a pop the notes did not mention.

The alternative soundtrack and the extra lute tracks are audio, not graphics. They belong in the sound menu, not in Overkill. A lute skin named Impervious is a skin. It is not Harden. [When to Harden](/mortal-shell-2-when-to-harden/) is still the combat page. This page is the menu page.

## A clean settings card

| Toggle | Turn it on when | Leave it off when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo mode | The room is clear and you want the new shade on record | A pack is still swinging |
| Overkill | One old-preset dungeon already held its frame rate | You are on the minimum GPU and the new dungeon has not loaded once |
| Nightmode preset | You already play with the sun off and the old dark is the complaint | You are judging a new dungeon for the first time |
| Pumpkin helmet | The shot is the point | You need to see your own weapon arc |

Rotten Autumn is a free content drop with a camera and a preset attached. Use the camera after the fight. Use the preset after the dungeon loads. The **$49.99** game underneath did not become a photography sim because the patch notes led with photo mode.
