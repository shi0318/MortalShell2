Steam does not publish a complete material table or every upgrade cost on the store page.

## Upgrade planning before you spend materials

Choose a main weapon by role first. A weapon that keeps you safe at mid-range may be more valuable than one with a higher single-hit number if you are still learning enemy timing. Keep a secondary option in mind for encounters where reach, recovery, or posture pressure matters more than raw damage.

The official description explicitly mentions both melee and ranged weapons. That makes upgrade planning broader than a one-sword route: spending everything on the first weapon you find may leave no flexibility for a sidearm or ranged option later.

## Use exploration as part of progression

Steam describes Mortal Shell II’s world as compact and interconnected, with weapons and upgrades placed off the main route. When a path looks optional, mark it on the [Mortal Shell 2 map overview](/map/) rather than assuming it is disposable content. A short detour may contain the upgrade that makes a later route more manageable.
