The **Harbinger** is the default body in **Mortal Shell 2** — the weak form you play before claiming a Shell. Week 1's Zmey notes say the tail grab used to take both the Shell and the Harbinger from full health to zero at once. That is official language, not a fan nickname.

![Mortal Shell 2 warrior](/images/mortalshell2-shell-greatsword-closeup.webp)

## How it plays

Reviews describe hatching weak and collecting eight Shells. Harden-style defense from the first game is not restated as a Harbinger-only move in the retail patch notes, so this page does not invent a kit.

When a boss note mentions the Harbinger, it usually means you are un-shelled or the grab hits both layers.
