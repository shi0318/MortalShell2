## The safe order for the reported Gloom room

Based on the public Steam discussion and its replies:

1. **Do not open every chest immediately.** First inspect the room and the door or route that continues the dungeon.
2. **Read the prompt before crossing the exit.** The reported interaction asks the player to give up the Gloom currently being carried.
3. **If you intend to clear the dungeon, perform the sacrifice first.** The community reply says this prevents the accumulated Gloom from being wasted when the room is completed.
4. **Return to the room after the clear.** The same reply says the chest rewards can be collected on a later visit after the dungeon has been cleared.
5. **Treat the exact amount as variable.** The player’s roughly 120,000 Gloom report is a snapshot, not a fixed reward table.

The important idea is order, not a magic farming route: resolve the sacrifice prompt before turning the room into a cash pickup.

## Why the room feels like a Gloom farm

The original post describes many chests and Gloom trees that respawn, which naturally makes the area look like an unlimited resource loop. But a room can contain repeatable enemies or trees and still attach a one-time dungeon decision to the exit. Do not collect a large amount and then assume that walking through the next door is harmless.

## Do not use this as a universal farming exploit

The checked thread does not establish any of the following:

- the official name of the cave or dungeon;
- a guaranteed 120,000-Gloom payout;
- the exact respawn timer for the Gloom trees;
- whether the room behaves identically on every platform or patch;
- a permanent duplication exploit;
- whether the sacrifice can be skipped without changing the clear state.

That boundary is important for a live-service launch window. If your room has a different prompt, screenshot it before accepting and record the current build. Do not spend your entire inventory based on a copied number from another player’s run.

## A practical Gloom checklist

Before entering an unfamiliar dungeon with a large balance:

- Spend only the Gloom you already know you need for a weapon or Shell upgrade.
- Mark the nearest beacon and the route back on your notes.
- Read every exit prompt instead of treating it as a normal door.
- If a sacrifice is offered, decide whether progression or loot is your immediate goal.
- Return after the clear if a community report says the room remains accessible.

Balance Patch 1 made experimentation cheaper at the Tarforge, so a safer strategy is to spend Gloom on a known upgrade and test the weapon rather than risking a full unverified balance for a screenshot-sized farming claim.

For the reported Mortal Shell 2 Gloom room, **sacrifice first, collect the chest rewards later** is the safest community-supported order.
