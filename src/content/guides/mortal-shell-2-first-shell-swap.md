**Harros** teaches the prologue and is destroyed after the Tar Golem. He is not one of the eight Shells you keep. The first real swap happens at **Zhirelle**, after you have a body from the world.

Bond caps at Tier IV. Glimpses are no longer a finite stack after the Week 1 update. **Merrick** in Marrow Keep sells **Mether's Severance** if you want the points back. That is a respec, not a new Shell.

## Before you offer anything

Smert's route is the trap. The altar near Outskirts of Nochte wants offerings in two blood pools. Offering knocks you out of the Shell. Never offer as **Harbinger**, or you come back as the wrong body and have to walk the swap again.

Gragu is a fetch, not an altar. With **Mether's Breath**, take the Heart of Vatra from the eastern temple back to the One-Legged Wolf Tavern. The lute is on the same stage. Routes are on [all Shell locations](/mortal-shell-2-all-shell-locations/).

Pick the Shell for the next gate, not for a tier list. The eight you keep are the roster. Harros stays a prologue page.
