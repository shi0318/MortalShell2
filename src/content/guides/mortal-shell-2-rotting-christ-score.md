**Rotten Autumn**, live **October 20, 2026** on Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox, includes a new alternative soundtrack by Sakis Tolis of Rotting Christ. The same list adds an Impervious lute skin and additional lute tracks. Those are audio. They are not Harden, they are not a damage buff, and they are not one of the **15** new dungeons.

Mortal Shell II has been out since **August 20, 2026**. The October 20 Steam listing was **$49.99** with no discount. Reviews were Mostly Positive: **17,558** positive out of **22,172**. A seasonal score does not move that number. It changes what a fight sounds like while you learn a new room.

## What to turn on, and when

Turn the alternative soundtrack on when you already know the room. A new score plus a new dungeon is two changes. If you miss a tell, you will not know whether the mix hid it or the enemy is new. Clear one of the 15 dungeons on the score you learned in August. Then switch.

Turn it off if you are learning a boss windup. The highlight post does not say the new score ducks sound effects. Assume it can sit under them until you have heard one swing both ways. A parry you miss because the mix is louder is not a git-gud problem. It is a menu problem. [When to Harden](/mortal-shell-2-when-to-harden/) still depends on hearing or seeing the swing you cannot read. Do not let a seasonal album take that read away.

The lute skin and the extra tracks are for the player who already uses the lute. Impervious is a skin name. It is not a seal and not a Tarstone. Equip it in the village, play one line, and leave it if you came for a dungeon. The pumpkin helmet is the visual joke in the same list. The score is the audio joke. Neither one is the route.

## How this sits next to the rest of the update

The October 20 highlight list is a pile if you enable all of it at once:

- Photo mode.
- The alternative soundtrack.
- 15 new dungeons.
- Shell shades.
- 10 new Tarstones, including a sheep-specific one.
- Nightmode lighting presets.
- Overkill.
- A Harbinger pumpkin helmet.
- Lute skin and tracks.
- A ghost with cryptic notes.

The score is the least dangerous item in that pile, and the easiest to misblame. A stutter in a new dungeon is more likely Overkill or the new room streaming in than a music file. Minimum spec is still an SSD, an RTX 2060 SUPER or RX 6600 with 8 GB, and a recent six- or eight-core CPU. Drop Overkill before you mute the score. If the frame holds and the tell is still buried, then mute the score.

There is no sheep dungeon. The notes said so in the same post. A soundtrack will not reveal one. Do not wander a field with the seasonal score on, waiting for a pen. The sheep content is a Tarstone. Test that in a room you already cleared, on the old score, so you can hear whether the passive did anything.

## A listening order

1. Update. The score is in Rotten Autumn, not in the August build.
2. Enter one known road with the new score. Confirm the game still returns to combat audio after a fight.
3. Enter one new dungeon on the old score. Learn one enemy.
4. Replay that enemy with the new score. If the tell vanished, leave the seasonal album for the village.
5. Try the lute tracks only after the dungeon has an exit you can name.

**53** achievements are on Steam. On October 20, achievement **001** was at **93.7%** and **039** was at **8.7%**. The highlight post does not name a soundtrack achievement. Do not grind poses or songs for a pop the notes did not list. If the in-game list mentions the score after the update, follow that list. Until then, the score is optional flavor on a free update.

Mostly Positive at this review count means the August game already worked for a lot of people. Rotting Christ's name will pull some of them back for an evening. Let it. Just do not retune Harden around a mix you have not A/B tested. The **15** rooms are the content. The album is the weather.


## One more pass before you close the menu

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. Write down the result in the game's own words. Then decide whether the next door, the next bind, or the next purchase is the one this page was about.

If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.

The village is the right place to decide you like the album. A dungeon you have not mapped is the wrong place. Sakis Tolis and Rotting Christ are the names on the highlight list, which is why the search exists. The list also includes fifteen dungeons, ten Tarstones, photo mode, and a pumpkin helmet. Enable the score after one of those dungeons is a room you can leave. If a tell disappears under the new mix, the album waits in the menu. The August score waited two months. It can wait one more fight. Mostly Positive at 17,558 of 22,172 did not depend on a seasonal album. The seasonal album is the reason some of those players reinstall. Let them hear it on a road they already survived.
