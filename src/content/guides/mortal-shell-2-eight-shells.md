Mortal Shell 2 has **eight playable Shells** you can keep, plus **Harros**, who is only playable in the prologue. Each Shell is a full kit: health pool, two abilities, a Temperament/upgrade tree and a bond with Memories to unlock.

![The Mortal Shell 2 equipment screen listing a Shell's abilities](/images/ms2-hot-0.webp)

## The roster

| Shell | Role | Signature ability | Steam unlock rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Tiel, the Acolyte](/tiel/) | Evasive assassin | Lingering Shadow, Shadow Dash, clones | 79.7% |
| [Proxima, the Broodseeker](/proxima/) | Aggressive all-rounder | Biosampler hook + Lightning | 78.0% |
| [Eredrim, the Venerable](/eredrim/) | Stagger and executions | Shoulder Bash, Ethereal Diapason | 56.7% |
| [Smert, the Apostate](/shells/smert/) | High risk, unarmed Chaos | Miracle time-stop, Overtime | 49.1% |
| [Gragu, the Insatiable](/shells/gragu/) | Sustain brawler | Staggering Blow, Revered Heart | 48.3% |
| [Sariel, the Endless](/shells/sariel/) | Pain and Curse | Exodus of Thorns, Purge | 35.2% |
| [Sester Genessa](/shells/sester-genessa/) | Summoner | Faithful Doubles, Duality | 33.9% |
| [Lazlo, the Justicar](/shells/lazlo/) | Tank | Retribution / Overheat | 31.4% |

Unlock rates are Steam global achievement percentages; they roughly track how early each Shell sits on the map.

## Picking your first three

- **Tiel** first – no requirements, and his clones are the best boss tool in the game.
- **Proxima** next – one dungeon away at Blackridge Pass and great for closing distance.
- **Eredrim** once you have the Citadel Gate Lever – the stagger Shell for big humanoids.

## Getting all eight

Collecting all eight unlocks **Shell Seeker**; Harros does not count. Our [all Shell locations route](/mortal-shell-2-all-shell-locations/) lists every unlock, and Glimpse at the Shellkeeper marks a Shell's shadow on the map.

## Balance changes so far

The 6 September update buffed Sariel (Thornfall works in enclosed spaces and hits moving targets, Agonize no longer needs full Pain, Affliction shares damage between cursed enemies) and nerfed Genessa's Augmentation (no more infinite Resolve or infinite Doubles). Later hotfixes fixed Proxima's Biosampler animation, Lazlo's Temperament loop, and a bug that blocked Gragu from being possessed.
