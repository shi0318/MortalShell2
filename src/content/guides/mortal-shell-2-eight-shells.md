Cold Symmetry's retail game has **eight playable Shells**. Reviews even spell that number out: you start weak and collect bodies. Valve's Unlock achievements plus the Week 1 Shells patch heading are how the names were locked in.

![Mortal Shell 2 Shell portrait](/images/mortalshell2-masked-shell-portrait.webp)

## The eight names

Tiel, Proxima, Eredrim, Smert, Gragu, Sariel, Sester Genessa, and Lazlo. Unlock rates on 1 September 2026 ran from Smert at **49.1%** down to Lazlo at **31.4%**, which is the best public hint at find order.

Sariel is the one name with no first-party patch mention; it sits on the achievement list and a community guide.
