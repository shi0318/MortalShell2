**Harden** comes from **Vatra's Seal**, the item on Harros after the Tar Golem. It turns the body to stone for one hit, you take nothing, and the swing you already started keeps going. It is not a parry, and it is not the Untarnished or Infinite Seal.

Spend it on the attack you cannot read in time. Save the parry for a swing you already know. Dodge the red, unblockable landing if the fight page names one.

## A three-way split

| Input | Use it when | Do not use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harden | The tell is late, or the string is multi-hit and you are already committed | You are fishing for a riposte you have not learned |
| Parry | You know the swing and want the riposte | The attack is named unblockable |
| Dodge | A leap or slam has a landing spot | You are trying to cancel a swing Harden would have carried |

The prologue halberd knight is the first drill. Magdalena's slow fire charge and Droeg's sweeps are the next two, after the seal is yours. See [Harden still works](/mortal-shell-2-harden-still-works/) for the seal table.

Infinite Seal and Slayer Seal are different tools. This page does not give them find spots. "Found later" is still the honest line until a route is cited.
