## Short answer

**Proxima, the Broodseeker** is officially confirmed as a **playable Shell in Mortal Shell II**, and — unlike Tiel and Eredrim — she is **new to the sequel**, with no counterpart in the 2020 game. She was one of three Shells shown in Cold Symmetry's Official Gameplay Reveal.

Source: [Official Gameplay Reveal](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2584270) (Cold Symmetry, 1 April 2026).

## What "Broodseeker" tells us

This is where honesty matters: **we have a title and nothing more**. Cold Symmetry has not published Proxima's stats, abilities, or how she plays, so anyone presenting a kit for her right now is guessing.

What is real is the framing the reveal put on all three Shells — each "carves a different trail." Since Tiel reads as mobile/aggressive and Eredrim as the durable tank, Proxima occupies the third, distinct corner of that triangle. The name "Broodseeker," and her debut alongside the reveal's spider-like creatures, invite speculation about a summoning or creature-linked identity — but we are flagging that as **inference, not fact**, and it stays off the confirmed table above until Cold Symmetry says so.

## Why she matters

Proxima is the clearest signal that Mortal Shell II's roster is not just the 2020 line-up brought forward. A brand-new Shell built for the sequel, shown front and centre in the first extensive gameplay look, suggests the eight-Shell roster mixes returning bodies (Tiel, Eredrim) with new ones designed around the sequel's interconnected open world.

## Roster context

Mortal Shell II has **eight playable Shells**. Three are named: **Tiel**, **Eredrim**, and **Proxima**. This page tracks the sourced identity of Proxima; retail stats and the acquisition route are added only when they are documented in the current build.
