**Proxima, the Broodseeker** is new to the sequel and the second Shell most players find (78%). She is the aggressive all-rounder: a hook arm that drags enemies in, Lightning that stacks into shockwaves, and armour that occasionally shrugs off a hit.

![The Mortal Shell 2 equipment screen showing Proxima, the Broodseeker and her abilities](/images/ms2-hot-0.webp)

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |

## Abilities

- **Biosampler** (active): fires a hook from her left arm that pulls an enemy to you – or you to it – then strikes and inflicts **Lightning**.
- **Grafted Armour** (passive): a base 15% chance to mitigate a melee or ranged hit, reducing it by a base 50% and preventing stagger.
- **Lightning**: stacking enough on one target triggers a damage-over-time shockwave.

## How to unlock

Start at the **Widow's Overlook Beacon** and cross the ravine to Blackridge Pass. Activate the **Blackridge Pass Beacon**, head north-east past the enemies near the large Treant and enter the **Shattered Beacon** dungeon. Clear it and interact with her remains at the end.

## How to play it

- Use Biosampler to pull ranged enemies off ledges and to close distance on bosses like Hexapod and The Monolith.
- Grafted Armour is a bonus, not a plan – keep Hardening.
- Pair her with a fast weapon so Lightning stacks quickly.
- Hotfix notes: 14 September fixed Proxima getting stuck in the Biosampler animation; 13 October fixed cancelling the ability stopping weapons re-equipping and improved Biosampler on The Monolith and Bor.
