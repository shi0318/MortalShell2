On **1 April 2026**, Cold Symmetry released the first extensive look at Mortal Shell II — a roughly **12-minute Official Gameplay Reveal**. This page collects what that showcase confirmed about the world, separated from the parts still open. It reads the reveal and the Open Beta store text; nothing here is invented.

## The biggest change: a real open world

The original Mortal Shell confined you to **Fullgrim** and its hub-and-spoke branches. The sequel's headline structural change is a **compact, interconnected open world** — a genuine departure, not a bigger version of the same layout.

The Open Beta backs this up in practice: it grants the **Prologue plus an early section of the open world**, so the structure is not just a trailer promise — players have already walked it.

## Region variety

The showcase moved through visibly different biomes, including **snowy landscapes** and **arid mountains**. That range matters for a series previously defined by one grim, unified palette; it signals the open world is built to feel like distinct places rather than one continuous ruin.

## Three Shells, three trails

The reveal put three playable Shells on screen, each described as carving **a different trail** through the world:

- **Tiel, the Acolyte** — returning; reads as the mobile, aggressive option. See [Tiel](/tiel/).
- **Eredrim, the Venerable** — returning; the durable tank. See [Eredrim](/eredrim/).
- **Proxima, the Broodseeker** — new to the sequel. See [Proxima](/proxima/).

These are three of the **eight** playable Shells Cold Symmetry has confirmed. As in the first game, you come across the bodies of fallen warriors and awaken them.

## Fast travel via Shells

One detail from the showcase coverage: Shells were seen **opening to reveal what appear to be fast-travel points**. We flag the word *appear* deliberately — the reveal did not spell out the fast-travel system, so treat "Shells double as fast-travel nodes" as a **strong read of the footage, not a stated mechanic**. It fits an open world that wants quick movement between regions, but we will confirm it against the shipped game.

## What is still not confirmed

- The map's total size and how many regions there are.
- Whether the open world is fully seamless or gated into connected zones.
- How fast travel actually unlocks and works.
- Which of the remaining five Shells appear, and where.

## How this fits the launch picture

Mortal Shell II released **worldwide on 20 August 2026** on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. The Open Beta, which ran before release, let players test the Prologue and an early open-world slice — see our [Open Beta record](/open-beta/) — and beta progress **did not** carry into retail saves.
