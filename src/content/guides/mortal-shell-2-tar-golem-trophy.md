The **Tar Golem** in Disciple's Grotto is scripted. Empty the bar or let it empty you, and Harros still sacrifices himself. The difference is the trophy **"No, You Still Can't Win"**. You only get that if the bar actually hits empty.

Be in Harros. From the grotto beacon, drop the slope, take the web launches, and enter the stone-ruins arena. The fight page is [Tar Golem](/bosses/tar-golem/).

## How to empty it

- Parry the axe swings into a riposte. Do not try to Harden every normal swing. Save Harden for the one you cannot read. [When to Harden](/mortal-shell-2-when-to-harden/).
- The red jump slam is unblockable. Dodge sideways off the landing.
- Optional: clear the Tainted Vestige first and bring the Burnt Effigy. Fire resist matters if you are dragging the fight to the trophy, not if you are letting the script end it.
- After the cutscene, interact with Harros's stone form for **Vatra's Seal**. That is Harden for the rest of the game.

If you only want to leave the prologue, you can lose. If you want the pop, drain it before the script does.
