**Before leaving the opening, check Mid Summer? and No, You Still Can't Win. Before defeating The Nameless Captive, check Peter's Perfect Parry.** These are three encounter-dependent objectives worth protecting early. This is a focused checklist, not a claim that exactly three achievements can ever be missed: the published Steam conditions do not provide a complete table of irreversible choices or current-patch recovery rules.


## Choose the check that matches your save

| Your current position | Check now | Decision if it is still locked |
|---|---|---|
| Still exploring the prologue | Mid Summer? | Complete the optional opening conversation before continuing. |
| Approaching or fighting the Tar Golem | No, You Still Can't Win | Treat reducing the Golem's health as a separate challenge; reaching the next story scene is not proof of success. |
| The Nameless Captive remains alive | Peter's Perfect Parry | Plan the required seven-hit spin attempt before finishing the boss. |
| Already beyond one of those encounters | The actual platform unlock state | Preserve your campaign and identify an available replay opportunity before replacing a save or beginning another cycle. |

Check the achievement list itself rather than relying on a notification. An overlay can be hidden, and remembering the scene does not establish that a particular optional action registered. If an objective is already unlocked, remove it from your immediate checklist; there is no reason to repeat an opening just because an old guide calls it missable.

## Mid Summer?: do the optional opening interaction

Steam names this achievement **Mid Summer?**, with a space. Older player descriptions also use “Midsommar?”; search under the current platform name when checking your account. Its published goal concerns entering the Festival. The detailed player guide connects it to speaking to **Marigold** near the start and receiving her flower wreath.

If you are still at that opening stage, stop moving along the main route long enough to complete the conversation and its visible interaction. Then check whether Mid Summer? is unlocked before pushing onward. This separates the optional action from simply walking through the opening area. Do not substitute talking to a later NPC or visiting a similarly decorated location because the achievement title sounds applicable.

If the prologue is already behind you, avoid deleting the ongoing character merely to test the interaction. A separate fresh save, where your platform offers one, can isolate an opening achievement attempt from a valuable campaign. Confirm the selected slot first. This page does not promise that starting New Game Plus replays the prologue or that an unrelated later conversation repairs the missed interaction.

## No, You Still Can't Win: story progress is a different result

The Tar Golem objective asks you to bring that enemy down to its minimum health. The community route places the attempt in the prologue fight. **Surviving until a story transition and earning the achievement are separate outcomes.** If you are pursuing completion, make the health objective the purpose of that encounter instead of assuming the next cutscene means it was awarded.

During an attempt, watch the Golem's remaining health and your own ability to continue safely. Keep enough separation to recognize the next attack before committing again. These are practice priorities, not a guaranteed damage rotation or an invented equipment requirement. No published condition here requires a particular Shell upgrade, weapon level or number of successful attacks.

After the encounter resolves, check the unlock before deciding whether to replay. Some player guides recommend force-closing during a cutscene; that is not an official save-recovery guarantee and is not the procedure recommended here. Once a result has been saved, quitting may simply reload that result. Preserve your current campaign, identify whether a legitimate earlier checkpoint or separate opening attempt exists, and avoid treating a forced shutdown as a reliable undo button.

## Peter's Perfect Parry: identify the right spin first

The published condition concerns The Nameless Captive's headspins. A developer reply adds the critical distinction: the boss has **three spin attacks**, and the achievement targets the **seven-hit horizontal sequence**, rather than the more vertical headbanging motion. The developer places it in the later phase transition. This is more useful than the broad instruction to parry everything that looks like a spin.

Prepare the attempt around recognizing that sequence. Keep the encounter available while learning its visual cue, and distinguish an ordinary defensive success from completing the whole required attack. If you respond correctly to a different spin, you may have defended well without satisfying this objective. Likewise, an interrupted sequence is not evidence that every required contact was handled.

A practical attempt plan is:

1. Confirm that Peter's Perfect Parry is still locked, then enter with the intention of attempting it before killing the boss.
2. Recognize the later horizontal sequence. Do not use the vertical headbanging version as your success test.
3. Follow the inputs and equipment descriptions in your current build. Count the target sequence's contacts as a learning aid, rather than replacing it with an assumed universal rhythm.
4. Check the platform unlock after a clean attempt. If it remains locked, separate a wrong-attack attempt from a missed contact or an interrupted sequence.
5. If the fight is still available, retry deliberately. Before ending it, decide whether you accept postponing the achievement.

Player discussions mention different Seals and stance breaks. The developer also asked about Tiel's Seal Affinity perk, but asking a diagnostic question does not prove that the perk is required or forbidden. Do not build a mandatory loadout around that question. Record your Seal and perk when reporting a failure so another player or support can understand the actual attempt.

## When the opportunity has already passed

Separate three states: an encounter you can still attempt, an earlier save you genuinely retain, and a completed encounter with no confirmed return. Only the first two offer an immediate retry. A general world-exploration checklist cannot reopen a defeated boss or prove that an optional prologue conversation remains available.

Keep the newer save until any proposed recovery has loaded successfully and you understand what progress it replaces. Use the platform's supported backup procedure where available; do not assume cloud synchronization is a permanent historical archive. An additional cycle or separate save is a planning choice, not a reason to overwrite hours of progress without checking what that option actually preserves.

For a persistent non-unlock, report the platform, installed update, encounter, equipment and exact action. A short description of which Captive spin occurred is more actionable than a global unlock percentage. Rarity cannot establish that your attempt was correct, that the achievement is broken, or that a specific number of players missed it for the same reason.

## Quick answers

### Are there exactly three missable achievements?

This page covers three early checklist priorities. Steam's achievement data establishes their names and conditions, not an exhaustive missability classification. Keep other optional NPC decisions separate and do not treat an old three-item warning as permission to skip everything else.

### Does finishing a fight prove its extra achievement unlocked?

No. Defeating a boss and completing a special action during that fight are different checks. Confirm the named achievement itself before marking the task complete.

### Should I start New Game Plus to repair a missed opening objective?

Do not start another cycle solely on that assumption. Check which encounters the next cycle actually includes and retain the current save until you have a supported plan. The general [achievement reference](/mortal-shell-2-achievements/) covers the wider list; this page focuses on protecting limited encounter opportunities.
