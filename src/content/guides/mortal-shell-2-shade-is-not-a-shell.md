Rotten Autumn, live since October 20, added new shell shades for each shell. A shade is a color. It is not a found body, not a new health bar, and not a respec. The shells you already unlocked are the kit. The shade is how you tell a seasonal screenshot from an August one.

On **October 25, 2026** the US page was **$49.99**, Very Positive, **9,899** English reviews, **84%** of **1,186** in 30 days. People will search the seasonal look as if it were a new character. It is not one of the eight. The pumpkin helmet is closer to a shade than to a shell, and it is worse, because it can hide your weapon arc. A shade does not.

## Wear the color after the room

Enter the first new dungeon in the shell you already understand, in the look you already recognize. Change the shade after you have an entrance written down. If you die in a new color, in a new room, with a new helmet, you learned nothing. One change. The shade can be the second session's change. The helmet can be the photo after that.

Respec is still the tool for a shell whose abilities you want back, not for a color you dislike. Mether's Severance and the respec pages on this site are about Glimpse and abilities. A shade does not refund them. Do not spend a respec because the seasonal palette feels wrong. Unequip the look.

Harden, parry, and the weapon you cleared the last dungeon with do not move because the shell is a different color. If a swing feels different, check the October 13 hotfix and whether you are actually on Rotten Autumn. Do not credit a palette for a damage change that belongs to an earlier note.

## What the search gets wrong

"New shell" in a thumbnail is often the shade, the helmet, or the sheep Tarstone. The sheep item may transform the look if its text says so. Read the text. It is still not a ninth shell, and there is still no sheep dungeon. The ghost with cryptic notes is not a shell vendor. The notes called the notes cryptic. Read them and move.
