## What full price is paying for

The August 20 game, plus whatever the current build added for owners. Shells, Harden, weapons, the map, and now the seasonal pack if you update. Not a sheep dungeon. Not a private promise that Overkill will hold 60 frames on a minimum GPU. Not a second copy for the couch.

If you own it, the price question is over. Update. Test one Nightmode preset only if you already play in the dark. Enter one new dungeon on the weapon you know. The 30-day reviews include people doing that. Join them on the current build, not on an August install you are still blaming.

If you do not own it, **$49.99** next to Very Positive and a busy month is the ordinary full-price pitch. Buy it if the combat is the thing you want. Wait if a sale is the thing you want. The page can grow a discount later. It had not grown one on October 25.

## Do not invent a bundle

The Devout edition was a separate upgrade on earlier listings. Today's check was the base price. Do not assume a costume pack is discounted because the seasonal helmet is free for owners. Free for owners means free after **$49.99**, not free instead of it.

Photo mode will not show you a lower price. The alternative soundtrack will not. A Tarstone will not. The store button is the price. Everything else is a menu inside a game you have or have not bought.
