**Seals** are the third equipment slot in Mortal Shell 2, next to your weapon and sidearm. They decide what your block button does – and that changes every boss fight. There are four.

![A Shell walks a torch-lit village path toward a gate](/images/ms2-hot-1.webp)

## The four Seals

| Seal | Effect | How you get it | Community tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Untarnished Seal** | Perfect Guard: a correctly timed block negates the attack and deals Break damage. Wide window; holding guard keeps blocking until your stun bar fills | Starting gear with Harros | S |
| **Infinite Seal** | Parry-style Seal: smaller window than Untarnished, more Break damage on success | Found later in the world | A |
| **Slayer Seal** | Periodically restores health and energy when you perform certain actions; using it locks certain achievements for that run | Found later in the world | A |
| **Vatra's Seal** | Cancels one incoming attack completely, then goes on cooldown (Harden) | From Harros' stone form after the Tar Golem | B |

## Which Seal for which job

- **Learning the game:** Untarnished. The wide window teaches boss rhythms, and a late press still blocks for chip damage.
- **Parry bosses** (The Nameless Captive, Sir Isaac phase one, Monolith ball slams): Infinite Seal. Faster stuns, as long as you can hit the tighter window.
- **Long fights and endgame:** Slayer Seal for sustain – but check the achievement list first if you are hunting 100%.
- **Bosses with poorly telegraphed hits** (Hexapod, Sir Isaac phase two, Zmey stomps): Vatra's Seal. Harden takes the hit you cannot read.

## Patch notes involving Seals

The 24 September hotfix fixed the Slayer Seal becoming unresponsive, and the 13 October hotfix fixed several Slayer Seal and Punch issues. If your Slayer Seal stopped triggering before mid-October, that was the bug.

## Tips

- Seals swap at any beacon (Change Equipment), so match the Seal to the boss rather than keeping one forever.
- Perfect Guard and parries both build Break; a full Break bar opens a riposte, which scales with enemy health since the Week 1 Update.
