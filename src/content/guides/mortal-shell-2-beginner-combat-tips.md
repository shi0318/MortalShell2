## The three ways to avoid damage in Mortal Shell 2

The Steam guide identifies three reliable defensive options. Learning at least two of them for every enemy is safer than trying to force a single reaction into every attack.

### 1. Leave the hitbox

Running out of range or strafing behind an enemy avoids the damaging hitbox without spending a dodge. This is the least glamorous option, but it creates space when an attack has a long reach, a grab, or an unclear timing window.

### 2. Dodge through the attack

The guide reports that a successful dodge grants a brief invulnerability window, commonly called dodge i-frames. It also notes that dodging normally prevents grab attacks from connecting. Use the dodge for a committed attack, then return to a safe position instead of immediately mashing a second input.

### 3. Use the Seal defensively

The launch-day guide groups block, parry and harden timing under Seal-based defense. The practical lesson is to watch the enemy's attack animation and press the defensive action at the correct moment, rather than treating harden or parry as a panic button.

## What the red attack sigil means

Some attacks cannot be parried or blocked. The community guide says a red sigil appears on the enemy during these attack animations. When that warning appears, do not stand still waiting for a perfect parry: create distance, strafe behind the enemy, or use the dodge instead.

This is one of the most useful early habits because it prevents a common mistake: reading every attack as a parry test when the game is actually signalling that movement is required.

## Healing with Mether's Pulse

The same Steam guide reports that early healing is weak, while **Mether's Pulse** becomes stronger and gains more charges as it is upgraded. Treat a heal as a deliberate opening rather than something to mash when health is low.

The practical sequence is:

1. Avoid or stagger the next attack.
2. Wait for a real gap in the enemy's pressure.
3. Use Mether's Pulse during that opening.
4. Resume defense immediately instead of assuming the heal makes you safe.

The exact upgrade costs and final charge values should be verified from the retail build before being added to a numerical build guide.

## Shielded enemies: try heavy attacks

The public Steam guide reports that enemies carrying shields are especially weak to heavy attacks. Test a heavy hit when a shielded enemy is blocking rather than spending the whole encounter on light attacks that fail to create an opening.

This is a community-tested matchup tip, not a universal damage multiplier. If a patch changes shield resistance or stagger values, the retail result should take priority over this first-day report.

## A simple first-run combat routine

- Keep two dependable answers ready: dodge plus movement, or dodge plus Seal timing.
- Read the red sigil before attempting a parry.
- Use movement to reset a fight when several enemies aggro together.
- Save healing for an actual opening.
- Test heavy attacks against shielded enemies.
- Record enemy-specific timing only after repeating it in the current retail patch.
