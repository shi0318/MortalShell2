Steam App 2584270 lists **53 achievements** for **Mortal Shell II**. The Open Beta app, 4711740, lists **9**. Those are not the same checklist. A beta pop does not fill a retail row.

The retail count is the completion ceiling on October 16, 2026. It is not a published missable table.

## What to clear first

- The eight Shells you keep, plus Harros in the prologue. [All Shell locations](/mortal-shell-2-all-shell-locations/) is the index. Harros is not a ninth row in the keep table.
- Weapons and sidearms. [All weapon locations](/mortal-shell-2-all-weapon-locations/) is the table. Iconoclast and Naylshotte are starters, not hidden achievements by themselves.
- Boss pops follow the named fights. [Bosses](/bosses/) names the item, not a fake Gloom total.

If a retail pop fails, confirm you are on App 2584270 and the current hotfix before you replay a beta route.
