**Mortal Shell II** makes Tarstones and Gloom part of the long-term upgrade decision. The most important post-launch fact is not an invented farming rate: Balance Patch 1 cut Tarforge smelting costs by **75% Gloom**, making experimentation substantially cheaper than at launch.

## What the patch confirms

The developer described the original Tarforge smelting cost as excessively high and reduced it by 75%. The same post-launch balance record names buffs to five Tarstones, but it does not provide a complete public table of every Tarstone effect, tier or location.

That means a “best Tarstone” list needs a test date. A Tarstone that feels weak before its buff or a cost change may be a different investment after the update.

## A low-waste upgrade routine

1. Keep enough Gloom for one controlled comparison instead of spending everything on the first attractive icon.
2. Change one Tarstone at a time.
3. Test ordinary hits, posture pressure, a parry and the return route.
4. Record whether the result solves a survival, range or recovery problem.
5. Re-check after Balance Patch 1 or the Week 1 Update changes a named weapon or combat rule.

This approach is more reliable than farming for an exact “Gloom per minute” number that changes with route, difficulty, deaths and patch version.

## Tarstones and the achievement list

The Steam achievement list tracks finding all Tarstones with **Stoned**, while **Over 9000** asks you to max out a weapon at the Tarforge. Those goals encourage exploration and one deliberate maxed weapon; they do not prove that every Tarstone must be maxed before the story.
