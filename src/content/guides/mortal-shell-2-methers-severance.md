The hotfix that unstuck Proxima also turned Mether's Severance back on. Before that pass, the item could not be used. If a guide told you to socket it and nothing happened, the guide was describing the broken build, not a hidden condition.

Use it after the patch the way you use any other stone: equip it, confirm the prompt, then take it into a fight you already know. Do not test a dead item and a new boss in the same pull.

## What this hotfix is not

It does not respec your Shell. That refund arrived in the Week 1 update. It does not make Glimpses infinite. That was the same Week 1 change. It does not fix Egon's Stone eating a dungeon load. That is the other hotfix.

If you are patching in October 2026, install every live hotfix and then check three things: the hook on Proxima returns control, Mether's Severance fires, and a dungeon door with Egon's Stone actually loads. Those are the three bugs people are still searching.
