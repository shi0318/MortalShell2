Checked October 12, 2026: Mortal Shell 2 is Very Positive — 8,331 positive out of 10,043. The live PC price is $49.99. Fifty-three achievements. Cold Symmetry developed it. Playstack published it. That is almost two months after the August 20 launch, not a review-bomb spike.

The praise clusters on combat variety, Shells as loadouts, and a dense map. Files at 30–45 hours still recommend it as a soulslike that punches. The complaint that survives is the last stretch: a first half people finish, then an endgame and a final boss they call obnoxious, unfinished, or the worst-designed fight in a while. Both can be true on the same score.

## How to read the score

- If you want the first-half loop — Shells, weapons, Tarforge, beacons — the Very Positive pile matches that run.
- If the last boss is the question, the negative files at 40+ hours are the ones that finished. They are not a refund-week tantrum.
- Adaptive Difficulty is an experimental setting from a later update. It does not rewrite the last-boss design. See the [adaptive difficulty page](/mortal-shell-2-adaptive-difficulty/).

A sale does not change the last arena. It changes whether $49.99 for the first 20 hours feels fair to you.
