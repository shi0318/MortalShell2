Mortal Shell 2's English-language Steam rating is **Very Positive** in October 2026, while the all-language score sits at **Mostly Positive**. Both are true, and the gap explains a lot about how the game is landing.

![A Shell faces an antlered shaman in a dark forest](/images/mortalshell2-hero-banner.webp)

## The scores on 15 October 2026

| Filter | Positive | Total | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| English reviews | 8,379 | 10,093 | Very Positive (83%) |
| All languages | 17,341 | 21,920 | Mostly Positive (79%) |

Compared with the 12 October snapshot (8,331 of 10,043 English), the rate is stable – no review bomb, no sudden recovery.

## What the positive reviews say

The praise clusters around **combat variety**, **Shells as loadouts** and a **dense map**. Reviews at 30–45 hours keep recommending it as the soulslike that "punches above its weight".

## What the negative reviews say

The complaint that survives is the last stretch: a first half players finish happily, then an endgame and final boss they call obnoxious or unfinished. Many of those reviews predate the **13 October hotfix**, which reduced the final boss's HP, doubled Cosmic Disease resistance and gave its grab more wind-up. Expect the next month of reviews to show whether that moved the needle.

## How to read the score as a buyer

- If you want the first-half loop – Shells, weapons, Tarforge, beacons – the Very Positive pile matches that run.
- If the final boss is your worry, read the [Zmey guide](/bosses/zmey/) and the [last boss item page](/mortal-shell-2-last-boss-item/) – preparation changes the fight.
- Adaptive Difficulty (6 September) helps a stuck run but does not rewrite the last boss.
