The released **Mortal Shell II** PC listing asks for Windows 10 or 11, 16 GB of RAM, a graphics card around RTX 2060 SUPER or RX 6600 class, and 70 GB of available storage. Those are the published requirement tiers, not a guarantee of one frame rate on every system.

## Safe first checks

1. Install the game and the latest platform patch on an SSD with more than the listed 70 GB free.
2. Update the graphics driver for the installed GPU.
3. Disable overlays one at a time if frame pacing changes.
4. Test a repeatable path rather than judging the menu or a single cinematic.
5. Record resolution, upscaling, frame cap, GPU, RAM and patch version before reporting a fix.

Avoid copying an `.ini` tweak without knowing what it changes. A setting that improves one GPU can create texture, input or stability problems on another.

## Why launch guides can be stale

Mortal Shell II received a Balance Patch 1 and a Week 1 Update after launch. They are gameplay-focused, but any post-launch build can also change loading, shader compilation or frame pacing. Always compare the installed build and the publication date of a troubleshooting page.
