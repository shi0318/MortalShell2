**Mortal Shell 2** sits at **Very Positive** on Steam: **7,812** of **9,444** reviews are positive. That is about **83%**. The game launched **20 August 2026** at **$49.99** on PC.

![Mortal Shell 2 forest encounter](/images/mortalshell2-hero-banner.webp)

## What players keep repeating

Recent reviews call it a souls-like with an open-world feel and dungeon structure. Several compare the overworld to Elden Ring and the interiors to Dark Souls 3. The eight playable Shells are the core fantasy: you start weak and inhabit bodies with different kits.

The other side of the same thread is balance and performance. Players describe basic enemies as arbitrarily punishing, open-world traversal stutter, and frame generation that does not feel reliable.

## Who it is for

Buy it if you want a hard action RPG with Shell swapping, named bosses, and a large interconnected map. Wait for a sale if stutter or cheap deaths are dealbreakers. The mix is strong enough that most owners stay; it is not a unanimous masterpiece.
