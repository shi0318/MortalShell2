Is Mortal Shell 2 worth buying two months after launch? Short answer: **yes for Souls-likes fans who liked the first game**, with a caveat about the endgame and PC stutter.

![A Shell faces an antlered shaman in a dark forest](/images/mortalshell2-hero-banner.webp)

## The numbers

On **15 October 2026** Steam shows **8,379 positive of 10,093 English reviews (Very Positive, 83%)** and **17,341 of 21,920 across all languages (Mostly Positive, 79%)**. The game costs **$49.99** (Devout Edition $59.99) and has **53 achievements**.

The English-language score is higher than the global one because Chinese- and Russian-language reviews are more negative, mostly about performance and difficulty.

## What players praise

- **Combat variety:** eight Shells that play like different characters, plus Harden and four Seals.
- **Boss design in the first half:** Magdalena, The Nameless Captive and Sir Isaac are regularly named as highlights.
- **A dense map** full of optional dungeons, puzzle entrances and over 30 minibosses.
- **Fast patching:** five updates in eight weeks, several of them directly answering review complaints.

## What players criticise

- **The last stretch.** The Unfound Path and the final boss Zmey drew the most negative reviews. The 13 October hotfix cut Zmey's HP and doubled Cosmic Disease resistance, which addresses much of that.
- **Map readability.** Corridors with odd connections; fast travel only after Mether's Breath.
- **PC traversal stutter**, improved by the October shader-cache rebuild.

## Who should buy it

| You are… | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A fan of Mortal Shell (2020) | Buy |
| A Souls-like player who wants something shorter than Elden Ring | Buy |
| New to the genre | Buy, and turn on Adaptive Difficulty |
| Sensitive to stutter on an older PC | Wait for more patches or a sale |
