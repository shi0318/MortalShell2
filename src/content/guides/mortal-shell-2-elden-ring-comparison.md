Mortal Shell 2 is open world, and the first question from Souls players is whether it is "Elden Ring-like". Structurally it borrows the open map; in practice it plays much closer to the first Mortal Shell.

![The Mortal Shell 2 world map showing Fainweald and the Ruins of Mammon](/images/mortalshell2-world-map-cover.webp)

## Side by side

| | Mortal Shell 2 | Elden Ring |
|---|---|---|
| World | Two overworld regions (Fainweald, Ruins of Mammon) plus a hub and an endgame area | One huge continent with many legacy dungeons |
| Checkpoints | 49 Beacons, 46 of them cleansable | Sites of Grace |
| Main progression | Six **Corrupted Gates**, each a self-contained biome with its own beacons and boss | Shardbearers and Great Runes |
| Character build | Swap between eight Shells with fixed kits | Free stat allocation and classes |
| Defence | Harden, Seals (Perfect Guard / parry), dodge | Block, parry, dodge |
| Mount / traversal | On foot, jump gates, Bone Portals | Torrent |
| Length | Roughly 30–45 hours for most finishers | 80+ hours |

## Where it feels like Elden Ring

- You choose the order: the six gates can be cleared in most orders once Mether's Breath opens the map.
- Minibosses everywhere – over 30 named ones guard Tarstones, Shells and weapons.
- Optional dungeons with puzzle entrances (illusory walls, coin pouches, bell bridges).

## Where it does not

- **Shells replace builds.** You are not making a strength or dex character; you are choosing a body with its own abilities.
- **Harden** still defines combat. It is a block that becomes a counter, not a stamina bar.
- **Density over size.** The map is smaller and corridor-heavy; many players say interior connections are confusing even when fast travel is unlocked.
- **Corrupted Gates feel like linear levels** with a boss at the end, closer to the first game's temples.

## Who will like it

If you enjoyed the first Mortal Shell and wanted more of it, this is that game with a bigger map. If you want Elden Ring's freedom of builds and open-ended exploration, expect a tighter, more authored experience.
