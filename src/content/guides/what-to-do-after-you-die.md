Death is not a wiped file. **Gloom** drops and can be recovered. A Glimpse spent to summon a Shell spirit in a boss room is the cost of that summon, not a punishment for dying.

Do not start a new game because the first gate killed you. Go back to the last cleansed beacon.

## After the death screen

1. Retrieve the Gloom before you pull a second elite. A second death can cost the pile.
2. If the tell that killed you was late, that is a Harden. If it was a named unblockable landing, that is a dodge. [When to Harden](/mortal-shell-2-when-to-harden/).
3. If you are out of the Shell, swap at Zhirelle. Do not offer at an altar as Harbinger. [First swap](/mortal-shell-2-first-shell-swap/).
4. Upgrade only if the weapon is the one you will swing again. The Tarforge is in Marrow Keep, not on the death screen.

Hardcore-style full wipes are not this game's published death rule. Do not import one from another Soulslike.
