- French

- Japanese
- Polish
- Portuguese - Brazil
- Portuguese - Portugal
- Russian
- Simplified Chinese
- Spanish - Latin America
- Traditional Chinese
- Korean
- Ukrainian

The asterisk in Steam's listing marks the language with full audio support. The other language names are present in the supported-language field, but the page does not separately label their voice-over coverage.

## Does Mortal Shell 2 have Chinese language support?

Yes. Steam lists both **Simplified Chinese** and **Traditional Chinese** for Mortal Shell II. That confirms the languages appear in the retail product's supported-language field. It does not, by itself, tell us whether every menu, subtitle line, achievement description, item name or future update has identical translation coverage.

If you need Chinese text, launch the retail build, open its language or display settings, and verify the available option on your platform. The store listing is the right source for supported-language status; the in-game menu is the right place to confirm the setting exposed by your installed build.

## Is Mortal Shell 2 fully voiced in every language?

The official listing only marks **English** with full audio. It does not state that French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Korean or the other listed languages have full voice acting. Therefore, the accurate answer is:

- **English:** Steam marks it as full audio.
- **Other listed languages:** Steam lists them as supported, but the checked product page does not specify full audio for them.

Do not turn “supported language” into “full voice-over language.” Those are different claims, and the current store data does not justify the stronger one.

## Why localization is an active launch topic

Steam's Suggestions section contains a public **localization request megathread** with 141 replies. That explains why players are searching for language information soon after launch, but a request thread is not an official promise of a future translation or a change to the current language list.

## Language-support checklist

Before purchasing or starting a long playthrough:

1. Open the current [Mortal Shell II Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2584270/Mortal_Shell_II/) and check the language field for your region.
2. Distinguish text support from English full audio; the listing does not promise full voice acting in every language.
3. Confirm the actual language selector in the installed retail build after a patch.
4. If a translation is missing or inconsistent, record the exact language, platform and build before using the official feedback channels.

This page will be updated when the developer publishes a new language matrix or changes the official Steam listing.
