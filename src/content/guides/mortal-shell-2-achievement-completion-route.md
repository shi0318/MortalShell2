The retail Steam list contains **53 Mortal Shell II achievements**. It separates story completion, Shells, weapons, sidearms, map fragments, Tarstones, Ova, Shell Memories and the all-achievements goal, so a completion route is easier when you track these as separate projects.

## The cleanest order

First collect normal exploration progress while following the story: Shells, map fragments, Tarstones and Ova. Next build one Shell to maximum bonding for **Decked Out**, and max one weapon for **Over 9000**. Leave “all” goals such as Shell Seeker, Lord of War, Guns. Lots of Guns and Bring My Ova Back to Me for a checklist pass after the map is familiar.

## Three completion warnings

1. **53 is the full Steam count.** Hidden entries are still part of the list; do not assume a visible-name-only checklist is complete.
2. **Weapon and sidearm sets are separate.** The achievements track them independently, and the patch notes do not publish a complete category table for every named item.
3. **Global unlock rates are snapshots.** The percentage on a Steam achievement page changes as more players progress and is not a difficulty score.

## Endgame checks

The story-completion achievement is **This is Not an Achievement**. Full completion is **No Lifer**. The rarest exploration goals in the checked snapshot include all Tarstones, all Shell Memories and all achievements, which is a reminder to log return markers rather than rely on memory.
