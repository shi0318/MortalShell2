Combat is **Resolve** and posture, not stamina. A slow recovery is a Harden problem. A fast poke is a spacing problem. Putting Harden on every Black Needle swing wastes the seal you need when the hammer actually comes out.

**Vatra's Seal** is the one you have after the prologue. Untarnished and Infinite are different tools. This page only plans around Harden, because that is the seal the trophy fight hands you.

## Pairings that match the feel

- **Iconoclast** or **Black Needle:** parry the swing you know. Harden only the late tell. Locations: [weapon index](/mortal-shell-2-all-weapon-locations/).
- **Axatana:** swap to the heavy axe for the stagger, then back to the katanas. Do not stay in the axe for a fast string.
- **Obsidian Hammer:** Harden the recovery, or do not swing it at a boss that chains. Player lists put it last for that reason. [Resolve](/mortal-shell-2-resolve-not-stamina/).

Upgrade at the Tarforge if the weapon is the one you will keep. +16 is the advertised first-run cap. Do not chase a past-+16 chest before the seal plan works at +0.
