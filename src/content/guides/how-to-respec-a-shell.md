**Merrick** in Marrow Keep sells **Mether's Severance**. That is the respec. It returns the points you spent on the Shell you are in. It does not turn Harros back on, and it does not move you to another body.

Body swaps happen at **Zhirelle**. Offering at Smert's altar as Harbinger knocks you out of the Shell. Do the respec in the keep, then swap on purpose. [First swap](/mortal-shell-2-first-shell-swap/).

## When to buy it

- You spent the early points on a poke Shell and the next gate wants Harden uptime. Refund, then pair the weapon. [Weapon and seal](/mortal-shell-2-weapon-and-seal/).
- Do not buy it to recover Gloom. Death already drops Gloom. Pick that up. [After you die](/what-to-do-after-you-die/).
- Bond still caps at Tier IV. A respec does not raise the cap.

No page here publishes the Coin price. Check Merrick's menu on your build.
