**Rotten Autumn** lists new lighting presets for Nightmode as their own bullet, separate from the **15** new dungeons. The update went live **October 20, 2026** on Steam, PS5, and Xbox. If you already play with the sun off, the preset is the first menu to open. A new dungeon in a new dark is two changes, and you will blame the wrong one.

Mortal Shell II launched **August 20, 2026**. The October 20 price was **$49.99**. Reviews were Mostly Positive, **17,558** of **22,172**. Nightmode was already a visibility argument before the seasonal pack. The presets are the patch's answer to that argument. They are not a new difficulty and not a sheep dungeon. The notes denied the sheep dungeon in the same post.

## Test the preset on a known road

Load the shell you already fight in. Leave the pumpkin helmet off. Leave Overkill off. Open the Nightmode preset list and pick one. Walk a road you cleared in August.

You are looking for one thing: can you see the windup you already know? If yes, the preset is usable. If the enemy is a silhouette until the hit lands, that preset is a screenshot, not a combat setting. Switch it before you enter a new dungeon. Do not "get used to it" inside a room you have never mapped.

Then enter one new dungeon on that preset, still on your old graphics setting. If the new room is unreadably dark and the old road was fine, the dungeon is the change. If both are unreadably dark, the preset is the change. Write that down. A free update with 15 rooms does not come with a recommended order. Your note is the order.

## What not to stack

Overkill is a PC graphics preset in the same highlight list. Minimum spec is an RTX 2060 SUPER or RX 6600, 8 GB, and an SSD. A new lighting preset plus Overkill plus a new dungeon is how a seasonal patch becomes a stutter. The recommended block on the US store page was empty when checked October 20. Do not invent an "Overkill for Nightmode" card the store did not print.

Photo mode can show you the preset. It cannot tell you the preset is fair. Take the shot after a cleared pull. A dark photo that hides the enemy is a bad preset, not a moody one.

The alternative soundtrack does not light the room. Mute it while you judge the preset. A tell you cannot see and cannot hear is two problems. Fix the light first.

Shell shades are visible in the dark only if the preset allows it. A new shade plus a new dark is another stack. Keep the shell look you already recognize until the preset is chosen.

## The combat rule does not change

Harden is still for the swing you cannot read. A darker preset makes more swings unreadable. That is a reason to change the preset, not a reason to spend Harden on every attack. If you are hardening everything, the light is wrong. [When to Harden](/mortal-shell-2-when-to-harden/) assumes you can see some tells. Give yourself those tells before you enter dungeon two.

Tarstones are the other new variable. Ten arrived with the update, one of them sheep-specific. Do not equip a new Tarstone while you are judging a lighting preset. You will feel tankier or weaker and credit the dark. Equip the Tarstone after the road test, in a room you can already see.

**53** achievements. The thin end of the October 20 sample included **039** at **8.7%**. Nightmode is not identified on the highlight list as an achievement. If the in-game list names a dark-mode pop, follow that name. Do not assume the pumpkin helmet in the dark is the pop.

## A clean dark session

1. Update to Rotten Autumn.
2. Pick one Nightmode preset on a road you know.
3. Reject it if a known windup disappears.
4. Enter one new dungeon on the preset that passed.
5. Leave Overkill, the helmet, the new score, and the new Tarstone for the second session.

The 15 dungeons will still be installed tomorrow. The preset will still be in the menu. A death you cannot explain is the expensive part. Spend one road to keep the explanation attached to the change that caused it.


## One more pass before you close the menu

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. Write down the result in the game's own words. Then decide whether the next door, the next bind, or the next purchase is the one this page was about.

If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.

Players who never turned the sun off can ignore this page. The presets are for the people who already accepted a darker game and then installed fifteen new rooms. Judge the preset on a road from August. Reject it if a known swing vanishes. Accept it only if that swing is still a swing. Then, and only then, take it into one new dungeon. Overkill, the helmet, and the new score stay off for that test. A death you can explain is cheaper than a seasonal stack you cannot. The rooms will still be there after you pick a light you can fight in.
