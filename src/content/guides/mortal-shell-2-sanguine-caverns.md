The **Sanguine Caverns** sit in the far north of Fainweald behind the **Stonebled Gate**. It is a vertical gate: collapsing bridges, exploding cocoons and a long drop into the cavern floor before **The Lost Child**.

![A bloated, toad-like creature with bloody jaws facing a Shell in a cavern](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-5.webp)

## Overview

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Entry beacon | Stonebled Gate |
| Gate beacons | Pulsing Caves, Sanguine Shoreline, Child's Feeding Grounds |
| Minibosses | Lucian, the Thirsting Knight; Tarblighted Stoner |
| Main boss | The Lost Child |
| Key loot | Serpent Stone, Blackblood Stone, Tarred Fragment, Lost Clotstone, Blackmarrow Key |

## 1. Stonebled Gate – Lucian, the Thirsting Knight

The gate stays corrupted until you cleanse the Stonebled Gate Beacon. **Lucian** retreats at half health – follow him through the small crack in the wall. In the second half he turns invisible; stand in the **light in the middle of the arena** to keep him exposed. Reward: **Serpent Stone**, Glimpse and the Ovum.

## 2. The caverns

- **Crawling stone enemies:** counter the jump once, then parry and riposte.
- **Multi-legged stone enemies:** stun-lock them with light attacks; use the sidearm on groups.
- **Jumping devils** die in one hit but grab.
- **Cocoons** explode when approached – shoot them.
- **Exploding fliers** try to knock you off bridges; dodge on the red icon.
- The bridge collapses when you step on it – keep moving to **Pulsing Caves**.

## 3. Pulsing Caves and the drop

Inside, a chest holds the **Blackblood Stone**. Back on the surface, clear the crawling stone enemies around the hole, detonate the cocoons inside it and jump. Fall damage is small; land on the platforms and avoid the holes with spikes. Shoot the three cocoons that block the path, then cleanse **Sanguine Shoreline**.

## 4. Tarblighted Stoner

Take the bone teleporter near Sanguine Shoreline. The Stoner is an aggressive rock-thrower whose attacks are mostly unblockable – stay mobile, and wait out the all-directions rock throw at low health. Reward: **Tarred Fragment**. Jump on the white spot to enter the boss arena.

## 5. The Lost Child

Parry its left arm swipe, dodge the bites, dash side to side through the projectile barrage, and treat everything below 60% health as dangerous (triple bite, instant hand strike, jaw inhale). Reward: **Lost Clotstone**, the **Child's Feeding Grounds** beacon (several Ova) and the **Blackmarrow Key** chest. Full fight: [The Lost Child](/bosses/the-lost-child/).
