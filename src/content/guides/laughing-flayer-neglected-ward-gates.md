**If the Laughing Flayer stops taking damage during the final-phase transition, hold your next shot until the transition ends. If you defeated it in the first room and the Neglected Ward gates stayed shut, the November 16 hotfix addresses that specific gate failure.** The first case happens during the fight. The second concerns the exit after a kill. Check which state you are in before spending another attempt.

The [official November 16 hotfix notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2584270/announcements/) list the changes under different headings. Cold Symmetry says the Laughing Flayer cannot be shot or damaged until the final-phase transition finishes. It also lists a fix for the Neglected Ward gates that could fail to open after a first-room kill. The announcement gives no frame count for the transition and no instructions for repairing a save whose gate was already stuck. The cover is a general screenshot from the [official Steam gallery](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2584270/Mortal_Shell_II/).

## Check your current room and encounter state

Start with the information that survives a hectic fight. Confirm the area name when the game displays it and remember which room you were in when damage stopped or the fight ended. The patch is specific to **Neglected Ward**. The same notes also fix an incorrect **Central Sewers** name showing in **Neglected Prison**, a separate location and display bug. If a clip uses one of those names, use the environment and the current map or location label to orient yourself before following its route.

- **The boss is in its phase transition.** Stay mobile, leave room to react, and let the transition finish. Test damage with a small commitment once combat resumes.
- **The boss is still fighting after the transition.** Continue the encounter. An attack that misses or an unavailable ability calls for a different check from an exit that stays closed.
- **The boss is defeated in the first room.** Check the gate from inside the room while your view of the fight’s ending is fresh. This is the outcome the November fix names.
- **The defeated boss was elsewhere.** Note the kill room and the exact gate. Those details matter if the symptom looks similar but falls outside the circumstances named in the patch.

## Use the final-phase transition to reset your position

Avoid treating a transitional animation as a free damage window. As soon as you recognize the change, use the time to find clear ground and verify the equipment you expect to use afterward. If you are tight against a wall or prop, take the space you need to see the boss when it becomes active. A short movement now can save the next defensive input and keeps you from judging damage while the boss is protected.

Wait for the visible transition to finish and for normal combat to resume. The patch gives no fixed timer, so use what is happening on screen during your attempt. Start with a low-commitment move you can repeat reliably. If you see a normal damage response and still have room to defend, bring in the longer sequence you practiced. If no damage registers, reset your distance and try once from a position where you can clearly see whether the attack connected.

If a ranged action never starts, inspect your equipped weapon and Resolve before concluding that the boss is still in the transition. The same update reworked Fusillade Stone and fixed Lazlo’s aim with the Triarch Repeater. For those two cases, the [Tarstone build checks](/rotten-autumn-tarstone-build-reset/) show how to retest on an ordinary enemy. Keep your loadout fixed when you move back to the boss so you can compare the same action under two conditions.

## Retest the post-kill gate on your existing save

1. **Confirm your game has received the November 16 update.** Mortal Shell II is available on Steam, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Let your own launcher or console finish any pending installation, close the game fully, and reopen it. The Steam announcement identifies the hotfix by date.
2. **Load the affected character and continue from the most recent normal checkpoint.** Preserve the save where practical. Keep note of whether it loads before the fight, inside the dungeon after the fight, or at an earlier location. A test after a fresh kill and a test on an already completed encounter answer different questions.
3. **Check the affected exit once the combat has ended.** If you are still in the first room after the kill, walk to the gate by the normal route. Pay attention to whether the gate opens, whether you see an interaction prompt, and whether the game still treats the encounter as active. Record the outcome before you try a reload.
4. **If a normal later load puts you before the fight, replay it once with the updated build.** Let the transition play out and note where the final blow occurs. If the original defeat already persisted and the gate remains shut, keep that save state and move to a support report. The patch does not specify whether it rewrites a previously stuck encounter.

Do not run the same route repeatedly just to obtain a different result. Once you know the build, room, kill state and gate outcome, a screenshot or short clip makes the problem easier to report than another run with a different setup. The [Rotten Autumn dungeon guide](/mortal-shell-2-rotten-autumn-dungeon-guide/) covers broader dungeon preparation if you need a clean run, and the [map guide](/map/) provides context for optional dungeon exploration.

## Record what the gate does after the kill

Write down the platform and any version visible in the game or client. Include the name of the area, whether the boss was killed in the first room, whether the final-phase transition had ended, and the gate state immediately after the kill. Add what changed after a normal reload. If you can capture one clip, start before the blow that defeats the boss and keep recording as you walk to the gate. A record of the full sequence lets someone distinguish a failed post-kill trigger from difficulty reaching a different door.

For the next attempt, change only the thing your check identified. If you lost health while waiting, practice the transition with the [boss preparation guide](/mortal-shell-2-boss-preparation-guide/) and choose better ground. If normal damage resumed and you killed the boss but the same gate stayed shut, keep the evidence for the developer and preserve the character. That is a progression issue even if the rest of the fight worked as expected.
