This **Mortal Shell 2 fragments and weapon skills** page explains the build question raised by launch players: fragments are described as skills for weapons, and some can be applied broadly while others are tied to a weapon type. The exact fragment catalogue is not published in the checked official sources, so this is a planning guide rather than an invented all-fragments list.

## How to think about fragments

Treat a fragment as a way to change how a weapon behaves, not as a replacement for learning its base moveset. First decide whether the weapon’s reach, recovery, and posture pressure fit your preferred distance. Then add a fragment that reinforces that role rather than trying to fix every weakness at once.

One public Steam review says fragments can be applied to all weapons or to a certain weapon type. That report is useful for build planning, but it does not establish exact names, values, drop locations, or whether a fragment can be removed for free.

## A low-risk testing route

Test one fragment against a familiar enemy before taking it into a boss route. Record whether the change improves stagger, damage uptime, safety after a miss, or ranged pressure. If the effect is unclear, keep the original setup until you can compare the same attack sequence again.

Steam describes weapon upgrades as part of exploration, so optional paths may be relevant to your build. Do not skip an unexplored branch solely because your current weapon already feels comfortable.
