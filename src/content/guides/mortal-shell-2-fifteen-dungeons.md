**Rotten Autumn** adds **15** new dungeons. That number is from Cold Symmetry's update post on **October 20, 2026**, the same post that put the patch live on Steam, PS5, and Xbox. The post does not name the fifteen, does not rank them, and does not attach a recommended shell. Anyone selling a numbered route through all fifteen on day one is writing ahead of the notes.

Mortal Shell II launched **August 20, 2026**. Two months later the Steam score is Mostly Positive, **17,558** positive out of **22,172**, and the price is still **$49.99**. The dungeons are free on top of that. They are also the first large dungeon pack since launch, which means the habits that worked in the August rooms are the habits to bring, not a new combat system.

## What "15 new dungeons" is allowed to mean

It means fifteen new places to enter. It does not mean fifteen bosses with published health bars, fifteen achievement pops, or a difficulty spike the notes forgot to mention. The same post lists 10 new Tarstones, shell shades, photo mode, Overkill, a pumpkin helmet, lute tracks, and a ghost with cryptic notes. Those are separate bullets. A dungeon can contain a Tarstone. A dungeon is not the Tarstone.

The sheep line is the one to keep out of the route. The notes say there is no sheep dungeon. There is a sheep-specific Tarstone. If a video tells you dungeon seven is the sheep pen, it is arguing with the patch post. Walk the dungeon for the loot the room actually drops.

Global achievement rates on October 20 still describe a game most owners start and fewer finish. Achievement **001** was **93.7%**. **003** was **91.5%**. **039** was **8.7%**. **040** was **13.7%**. A new dungeon pack can create new rare pops. It cannot, by itself, tell you which of the fifteen is the one those pops live in. Open the achievement list after the update. Let the in-game names lead, not a guess.

## How to pick the first one

Use the shell you have already swapped into, not a shade you just equipped for the screenshot. A shade is a color. The found shell is the health and the move set. [The first shell swap](/mortal-shell-2-first-shell-swap/) is still the progression page. Rotten Autumn did not add a ninth shell in the highlight list. It added shades for each shell.

Bring the weapon you can parry with. The October 13 hotfix is the recent combat note. Rotten Autumn says it includes polish and bug fixes, and does not reprint a weapon buff table. If a weapon felt wrong last week, it will feel wrong in a new room until you confirm the patch you are on. Do not "test every weapon" in dungeon one. Test the room.

Bring one new Tarstone, not ten. Ten new passives in one bag is how you stop knowing which effect fired. Equip the sheep one if you want the joke. Equip any other if you want a build. Fight one pack. If you cannot tell whether the Tarstone did anything, take it off and clear the room with the weapon. You can theorycraft after you have seen the enemy.

Leave when the run is the prize. A new dungeon with an unknown exit is the same problem as a launch dungeon with an unknown exit: the bag you die with is the bag you do not keep, if that is how the zone works. Do not learn that rule again in a seasonal room. Turn around when you are carrying something you would be angry to lose.

## What to write down

The notes do not give you a map. Your first clear should make one, even if it is three lines:

- The entrance you used, and whether it was marked after the update or hidden until you walked past it.
- The enemy that killed you, and whether Harden, a parry, or a dodge was the input you were late on.
- The Tarstone or item the room actually gave you, in the game's words, not a guess from the helmet.

That note is more useful than a tier list of fifteen dungeons nobody has numbered yet. After three entrances you will know whether the pack is "more of the launch dungeons" or "a different density." Both are possible. The patch post does not say which.

Photo mode waits until the room is clear. Overkill waits until one dungeon has loaded on your old preset. Those toggles are on [the photo mode page](/mortal-shell-2-photo-mode-overkill/). They are not part of the route.

## A first-dungeon card

| Bring | Leave behind |
| --- | --- |
| The shell you already fight in | A brand-new shade as your only change |
| One weapon you can read | A weapon you are "trying because of the patch" |
| One new Tarstone | All ten, equipped to see what happens |
| Harden for the swing you cannot read | A hunt for a sheep dungeon |
| An exit plan | A promise to clear all 15 tonight |

The ghost with cryptic notes may show up while you are in there. Read the note. Do not reset the dungeon because the note was vague. Vague is what the highlight list already called it.

Minimum PC spec remains an SSD, 8 GB on the named GPUs, and a recent six- or eight-core CPU. Fifteen new dungeons are fifteen new loads. If the first one stutters, drop Overkill before you drop the shell. The content is free. The frame time is not.

Mostly Positive at this review count means a lot of people already liked the August game. The 15 rooms are extra, not a repair. Clear one tonight. The other fourteen will still be installed tomorrow.

The shell shades, the pumpkin helmet, and photo mode can wait in the menu while you do that clear. A shade does not change the swing. A helmet can hide it. Photo mode is a reward you take in a room that is already empty. If you open all three before the first new entrance, you will not know whether a death was the dungeon or the toy. The update post listed them as separate highlights for a reason. Play them as separate sessions.

Tarstone number two can also wait. The first new passive is the one you can actually name after the fight. The second is the one you add tomorrow, on the same pack, so the difference is visible. Ten passives in a bag you have not mapped is not a build. It is a pile. The sheep-specific one is the pile's joke. [Leave the pen](/mortal-shell-2-sheep-tarstone/) out of the route. Bring the item if the text is worth a slot. Do not bring a rumor.

If the first dungeon will not load, drop Overkill and confirm the SSD the store page already required. Fifteen new rooms are fifteen new streams. A minimum-spec machine — RTX 2060 SUPER or RX 6600, 8 GB, Windows 10 or 11 — can enter one of them. It should not be asked to enter one of them on a brand-new high preset in the same minute the pack installs. Load the room. Then decorate the shell. The free update is still free if you take it in that order.
