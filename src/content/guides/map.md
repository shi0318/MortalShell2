## Officially named early-game structure

From the [Open Beta Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4711740/Mortal_Shell_II__Open_Beta/):

1. **Prologue**
2. **First explorable region** of the open world
3. **Beacons** you can cleanse
4. **Optional dungeons** with hidden mini-bosses and challenges
5. Progress toward **Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods**
6. **Marrow Keep** — progress threshold for the prologue-skip unlock
