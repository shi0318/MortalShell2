If a dungeon door ate your loading screen, check that you are on the hotfix that names Egon's Stone. The notes are specific: dungeons were not loading correctly when that stone was in use. The same pass fixes the Seeking the Past trophy and achievement, and it stops Egon's Stone from removing Gloom you had already dropped in the open world.

That last one is the expensive bug. Gloom on the ground is not a visual. If the stone was deleting it, a farming route written before the hotfix was lying about your pouch. Re-drop after you patch. Do not assume the old pile is still there.

## What to retest

- Enter the dungeon that failed, with the stone equipped, then without it.
- Pick up a fresh Gloom drop in the open world and confirm it stays after you use the stone.
- If Seeking the Past still will not pop, the hotfix is the first thing to install, not a new playthrough.

A later hotfix is a different list. It fixes Proxima's Biosampler animation and Lazlo's Temperament perk. Do not mix the two notes when you are arguing with a door.
