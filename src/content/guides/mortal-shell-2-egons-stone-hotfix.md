**Egon's Stone** is a Tarstone that interacts with Gloom – and for most of September it was the most bug-reported item in Mortal Shell 2. Three hotfixes touched it. If a dungeon would not load or your Gloom vanished, this page tells you which fix you need.

![A Shell with a greatsword walks a dark overgrown path](/images/ms2-hot-5.webp)

## The fixes in order

| Hotfix | What it fixed |
|---|---|
| 14 September | Egon's Stone Gloom exploit closed; dungeons not loading correctly after using Egon's Stone |
| 24 September | Dungeons not loading when using Egon's Stone; Egon's Stone removing Gloom dropped in the open world; **Seeking the Past** trophy/achievement issues |
| 13 October | Unequipping Egon's Stone could prevent Gloom loss (the reverse exploit) |

## Symptoms and what to do

- **A dungeon door loads forever or drops you back outside.** Make sure you are on the 24 September hotfix or later, then re-enter with the stone unequipped once to confirm the dungeon loads.
- **Gloom you dropped on the ground is gone.** Before 24 September the stone could delete open-world Gloom piles. That Gloom is not coming back; re-farm it. The [Gloom farming guide](/mortal-shell-2-gloom-farming-guide/) has the best spots (the pod cliff on The Unfound Path is excellent).
- **You never lose Gloom on death.** That was the 13 October exploit; it is closed now.
- **Seeking the Past will not unlock.** Patch first, then talk to Genessa directly for her Memories – see the [Seeking the Past page](/mortal-shell-2-seeking-the-past/).

## Related stones

The 14 September hotfix also fixed **Justiciar Stone** durability, **Gloombound Stone** losing durability from non-enemy Gloom, and made the **Colossus Stone** cancellable mid-execution. The 6 September update made **Fragile Tarstones** grant Tarcores when they break.
