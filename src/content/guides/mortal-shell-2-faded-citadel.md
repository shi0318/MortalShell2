The **Faded Citadel** is the white, washed-out gate in the far south of Mammon and usually the last of the six Corrupted Gates. Its gimmick is **blue lanterns**: enemies standing in their light cannot be damaged.

![A horned Shell walks through a dark ruined hall](/images/ms2-hot-6.webp)

## Overview

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Access | Voltaic Essence from the Starved Harridan |

## 1. Starved Harridan

In the **Vestige of Infinity**, reached through the Lonesome Spire south of Castigator's Keep. Dodge the unblockables, parry the rest – three parries stagger it – and stay clear of the weight on its leg chain, which it swings harder as its health drops. Reward: **Voltaic Essence** (opens the way) and Voltaic Amber.

## 2. Gate of Infinity – Gloombound Wraith

Take the coin chest, fight through, and interact with the right bookshelf. The pedestal needs the book on top of the shelves surrounded by candles. The **Gloombound Wraith** staggers easily; past 50% it gains a red barrier – destroy the glowing red books on opposite sides of the room. Reward: **Curseblood Stone** and Glimpse.

## 3. Ashen Sanctum and the blue lanterns

Draw enemies out of lantern light or hit the lantern a few times to put it out. The second lantern, guarded by a dual-wielding dancer, opens two gates – one to the **Summoning Stone**, one deeper. Lure the crowd off the third lantern, kill the scythe enemy by the pond and reach **Citadel Annex**.

## 4. Citadel Annex

Before entering, follow the circular path south of the beacon for the **Faded Gland** (third gland for Seedbearer's Scripture). Altars on the bridge grant invulnerability for some health. Ride the giant bug across – stand still on its head – and kill the slithering enemy for the **Stillblade's Stone**.

## 5. Solnir and Citadel Atrium

Extinguish every blue lantern in **Solnir's** arena, then fight him as a stronger dancer. Reward: **Solnir Shard**, which opens the path. At **Citadel Atrium**, put out only one of the two lanterns before engaging the crowd.

## 6. Sir Isaac, the Scholar-Prince

Phase one: hit once, back off, avoid the shield bash. Phase two (about 40%): four swords, double hits – Harden and punish only recoveries; Tiel's clones help. Reward: **Scholar's Wormstone** and the gate's Ova. Full fight: [Sir Isaac](/bosses/sir-isaac-the-scholar-prince/).
