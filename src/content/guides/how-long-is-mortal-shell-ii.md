## Short answer

The official **Mortal Shell II Open Beta** is described by Steam as roughly the **opening three hours** of the game. That is the only clear time estimate currently published by an official source.

The full-game completion time has **not** been officially published. The retail listing does confirm a larger interconnected world and more than 80 dungeons, so the Beta length should not be treated as the length of the full campaign.

## How long is the Open Beta?

Steam says the Beta runs from the **Prologue** into the **first explorable region**. It includes:

- Shell possession and the early Harbinger route;
- Beacons;
- optional dungeons;
- hidden mini-bosses and challenges;
- the route toward Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods.

Players who explore every optional branch may spend longer than the store's approximate three-hour description. The estimate describes the scope of the build, not a guaranteed completion time for every player.

## How long is the full game?

There is no reliable official answer yet for a standard campaign, completionist run or every-Shell playthrough. The official full-game listing gives scale signals instead:

- interconnected open world;
- eight playable Shells;
- more than 80 dungeons;
- extensive weapon upgrades;
- optional exploration away from the main route.

Those facts show that the full game is substantially larger than the Beta, but they do not justify inventing a number of hours.

## When will a real completion-time estimate be possible?

After the August 20, 2026 retail launch, a useful estimate should separate at least three play styles:

1. main-route completion;
2. normal exploration with optional dungeons;
3. completionist runs covering Shells, weapons, collectibles and side content.

Until those runs and a larger player sample exist, the honest answer is **“Open Beta: roughly three hours; full-game length: not officially stated.”**
