Recent files at 40–99 hours keep the same split. The map, the Shells, and the first-half bosses are the reason they stayed. The last four fights, and especially the final one, are the reason the recommendation comes with a warning. One long positive file calls the last boss obnoxious and then says there is eventually a way. That is the honest page, not a move list.

Do not treat Adaptive Difficulty as a last-boss skip. It is an experimental slider that answers how you have been playing. It does not publish a phase chart. The optional last-boss item still matters more than a new setting. See [bring the optional item](/mortal-shell-2-last-boss-item/).

## What to do on the approach

- Finish the Shell and weapon you actually use. A four-Shell experiment in the last door is how a good file becomes a rage quit.
- If a dungeon door ate the load, check the [Egon's Stone hotfix](/mortal-shell-2-egons-stone-hotfix/) before you blame the boss.
- Ledges with slideable cracks are a separate complaint. They steal Gloom on the way in. They are not the last-boss pattern.
- This page does not invent HP, phases, or a “best Shell for the last fight.” Nobody on this wiki has a published frame sheet for it.

If you record the build, the Shell, the weapon, and what actually punished the last phase, that is the missing piece — [get in touch](/contact/).
