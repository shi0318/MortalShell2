Mortal Shell 2's reviews split at the same place: the **Unfound Path** endgame and the final boss **Zmey, the Unbidden**. Here is what the endgame contains, why it frustrated players, and what the patches changed.

![A towering stone guardian lit by fire in a dark arena](/images/mortalshell2-cathedral-guardian-boss.webp)

## The endgame in order

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Unlock | Return about 160 Ova and talk to Milos in Marrow Keep |
| Three guardians | [Malborn Offspring](/bosses/malborn-offspring/), [The Monolith](/bosses/the-monolith/), [Orrem](/bosses/orrem-the-reclaimed/) – any order, each guarding a Corrupted Ovum |
| The Hidden Nave | Endless Core chest (removes the +16 cap); the gate demands **all** your Gloom |
| Final boss | [Zmey, the Unbidden](/bosses/zmey/) – Burn, Stasis and Cosmic Disease |

Full route: [The Unfound Path walkthrough](/mortal-shell-2-unfound-path/).

## Why players struggled

- **Three back-to-back hard bosses** with corrupted beacons instead of Ova.
- **Cosmic Disease** in Zmey's second phase can strip your Shell, weapon and Tarstones, and its breath builds the bar even through parries.
- **The tail grab** was close to instant at launch.
- **Malborn Offspring's grabs** kill light Shells outright.

## What the patches changed

| Patch | Endgame change |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Zmey's tail grab no longer removes Shell and Harbinger at once (still on in Night Mode) |
| Balance Patch 1 | The Monolith: −19% damage, −10% health |
| 6 September | Malborn Offspring no longer invulnerable during phase transitions |
| 24 September | Zmey's fire ring attacks tweaked; fixed Orrem dying instantly |
| 13 October | **Zmey HP reduced, player Cosmic Disease resistance doubled, grab given more anticipation**; Orrem's triple slam adjusted; main-boss phase changes fixed |

## What to bring

- **Seedbearer's Scripture** – the [last boss item](/mortal-shell-2-last-boss-item/), crafted from three glands.
- **Paradoxical Scripture** for Stasis, a **Galvanized Shard** for Malborn's lightning, **Burnt Effigy** for Orrem.
- **Tiel** (clones) or **Genessa** (Doubles) – the two Shells that trivialise the most endgame mechanics.
- Level up before the Hidden Nave; the Gloom is gone either way.
