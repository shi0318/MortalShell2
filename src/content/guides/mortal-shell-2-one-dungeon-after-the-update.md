Rotten Autumn added **15** new dungeons on October 20. The US Steam page on **October 25, 2026** listed the game at **$49.99**, Very Positive, **9,899** English reviews, and **84%** of **1,186** in the last 30 days. That month includes the update. It does not include a recommended order for the fifteen rooms. Clear one. Leave the costume for after.

If you already own the game, the price question is over. If you do not, Very Positive and a busy month are the buy signal, and the first session is still one dungeon, not all of them.

## The session

Update. Load the shell you already fight in. Leave the pumpkin helmet off. Leave Overkill off. Leave the seasonal score off. Enter one new dungeon on the weapon you can parry with. Write down the entrance, the enemy that killed you, and whether Harden, a parry, or a dodge was the late input. Leave when the bag is the prize, or when you have an exit you can name.

Harden is still for the swing you cannot read. A new room does not change that. A helmet that covers your weapon arc makes more swings unreadable. Take it off until the tell is learned. Photo mode waits until the room is empty. The update does not say photo mode pauses every pack.

There is no sheep dungeon. The same post that added a sheep Tarstone said so. If you spend the evening looking for a pen, you are not clearing the one room this page asked for. Equip the Tarstone later, alone, on an enemy you already know.

## What the 1,186 reviews are not

They are not a tier list of the 15 dungeons. They are not proof Overkill holds a frame on a minimum GPU. Minimum spec still wants an SSD and an 8 GB card in the class the store listed. A stutter in a new room is a stream or a preset until you have loaded the room once on the old graphics setting. Then try Overkill in a cleared corner, not in a boss corridor.

They are not a sale. **$49.99** had no discount on the October 25 page. Owners should stop refreshing and enter the room. New buyers should buy for the combat or wait for a button that shows a discount. The helmet will still be in the patch notes tomorrow.

**53** achievements are on the store record. The highlight list did not name a photo-mode pop or a helmet pop. Look at the in-game list after the update. Do not grind a costume for a guess.

## One room, then stop

The other fourteen dungeons will still be installed. Nightmode presets can be tested on a known road later, if you already play with the sun off. The alternative soundtrack can be turned on after you know whether a tell survived the mix. Stacking all of that in dungeon one makes the death unexplained.

Very Positive at this size means a lot of English reviewers still recommend the fights. Recommend them to yourself with one clear, helmet off, old score, one weapon. Then decide if the seasonal pile is the second evening. The **$49.99** game underneath is what 84% of 1,186 recent reviews were willing to stand behind. The hat is optional. The update is not, if you want those rooms at all.

84% of 1,186 recent English reviews, on a $49.99 Very Positive page with 9,899 all-time, is a reason to update and enter one room, not fifteen. Helmet off. Overkill off. Seasonal score off.
