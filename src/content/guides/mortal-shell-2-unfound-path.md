**The Unfound Path** is the endgame of Mortal Shell 2. It opens beneath Marrow Keep once you have returned enough Ova, splits into three guardian routes, and ends with **Zmey, the Unbidden**.

![A Shell walks toward a moonlit fortress at night](/images/mortalshell2-official-keyart-4.webp)

## How to unlock it

Gather about **160 Ova**, then talk to **Milos** in Marrow Keep. A path appears in the Ova Nursery Chamber – you do not need to cleanse all six Corrupted Gates. Take the stairs down: the right path leads to a chamber with three lanterns (one per guardian), the Stone Portal ahead leads to the main route. The first portal's cliff has enemy-hatching pods that are good for farming Gloom.

## Overview

| Route | Beacons | Guardian | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left | Receiving Hall → Faithful's Shelter | Malborn Offspring | Offspring Trophy (5,000 coins) |
| Centre-left | Brether's Court → Obsidian Vault | The Monolith | Monolith Fragment (5,000 coins) |
| Right | Bridge of Procession → Empty Cradle | Orrem (Discarded Golem → Reclaimed) | Orrem's Head (5,000 coins) |
| Final | The Hidden Nave | Zmey, the Unbidden | Ending, New Game Plus |

Beacons here have **corruption sources** instead of Ova. After each guardian, sever the Corrupted Ovum it was guarding.

## Malborn Offspring route

Left Stone Portal → Receiving Hall → stairs and bridges (knife-hand enemies, giant cockroaches) → Faithful's Shelter (source by the small pool) → right-hand path to the arena. Bring a **Galvanized Shard** for the lightning phase. [Boss guide](/bosses/malborn-offspring/).

## The Monolith route

Left, then right to the jump spring and Stone Portal → snake-tailed enemy → Brether's Court (switch left of the locked gate) → elevator, dancer, stairs → Obsidian Vault (hammer enemy, spiders in the water) → downward path on the right. [Boss guide](/bosses/the-monolith/).

## Orrem route

Right, drop via jump spring → Stone Portal → spider and scythe enemies → staircase on the left → Bridge of Procession (eggshell cover) → across the bridge. Afterwards an elevator leads to Empty Cradle; the last Corrupted Ovum is down the tight spider path behind it. [Boss guide](/bosses/orrem-the-reclaimed/).

## The Hidden Nave

With all three sources severed, the Hidden Nave beacon cleanses from outside. Take the torch at the campfire. The chest with the **Endless Core** removes the +16 weapon cap at the Tarforge. Chests in the great hall give Gloom, but the gate asks you to **offer all of your Gloom** – level up first.

## Before Zmey

Craft **Seedbearer's Scripture** from the Depleted, Thawed and Faded Glands (Forgotten Tower near Sester's Refuge, at night) and carry **Paradoxical Scripture** for Stasis. [Zmey boss guide](/bosses/zmey/).
