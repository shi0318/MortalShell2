**Mortal Shell II** launched on **August 20, 2026**. The released Steam feature set lists **Full Controller Support**, **DualSense Controller Support**, **Steam Cloud**, **Family Sharing**, and **Single-player**. Those labels answer the main compatibility question, while the detailed button layout and several controller behaviours remain outside the verified retail record used for this guide.

## Quick answer: what Mortal Shell II supports

| Listed feature | What it confirms | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full Controller Support** | Controller support is listed for the PC version. | A universal button layout, prompt style, remapping path, or dead-zone setting. |
| **DualSense Controller Support** | DualSense support is listed. | Adaptive triggers, haptics, vibration strength, gyro, or wired-versus-wireless behaviour. |
| **Steam Cloud** | Steam Cloud is listed for the PC version. | Cross-save with PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X\|S, conflict handling, or a backup guarantee. |
| **Family Sharing** | Family Sharing compatibility is listed. | The details of account, DLC, achievement, or entitlement behaviour. |
| **Single-player** | The game is listed as a single-player experience. | Co-op, PvP, matchmaking, or an online multiplayer mode. |

The safest answer is therefore yes: the retail PC listing includes controller support and DualSense support. It is not a promise that every controller option or every connection method behaves identically.

## PC controller settings: the verified boundary

Launch the **full Mortal Shell II** retail app, then open the current controls or options screen exposed by the game. Read the prompts and action labels shown by that build. This page does not publish a button map because the verified record does not establish one.

For a PC setup check, keep these questions separate:

1. Does the retail app recognise the controller at all?
2. Which action names and prompts does the current controls screen display?
3. Does the menu expose remapping or other controller options in this build?
4. Is the problem limited to a particular device, connection, or input action?

Only the first-level support labels are confirmed here. A complete in-game mapping, vibration matrix, gyro option, or remapping workflow needs a separate retail-build verification before it can be stated as fact.

## DualSense on PC

**DualSense Controller Support** is a listed feature for the PC version. That is enough to answer whether DualSense belongs in the supported-controller discussion. It is not enough to claim adaptive-trigger effects, haptic feedback, a gyro feature, a specific USB or Bluetooth requirement, or a particular prompt layout.

If you are checking a DualSense setup, use the controls screen and the prompts displayed by your current installation. Record the exact behaviour you can reproduce instead of converting the store label into a feature that has not been confirmed.

## Steam Cloud is not cross-save

The Steam listing includes **Steam Cloud**. That describes the PC storefront feature. It does not establish that a save moves between Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, and it does not document save conflicts, slot limits, or a replacement for local backups.

For a PC issue, first identify whether the problem is controller recognition or save synchronisation. They are separate checks. The controller-support label does not answer the cloud-save question, and the Steam Cloud label does not answer the controller-layout question.

## Family Sharing and the single-player limit

**Family Sharing** is listed for Mortal Shell II, while **Single-player** is also listed. Together, those entries describe storefront access compatibility and the advertised play mode. They do not add co-op, PvP, matchmaking, or cross-platform progression to the game.

They also do not establish the exact treatment of DLC, achievements, accounts, or entitlements in a shared-library situation. Keep those details out of a controller setup checklist unless a future retail record verifies them.

## Safe controller troubleshooting checklist

When a controller does not behave as expected on PC, use a narrow report:

- Confirm that you opened the retail Mortal Shell II app rather than the archived Open Beta app.
- Open the current controls or options screen and copy the action label and prompt exactly as displayed.
- Test one input action at a time instead of changing several mappings together.
- Record the controller model, connection method, current retail build, and the repeatable action that fails.
- Keep Steam Cloud, Family Sharing, and controller recognition as separate questions.

Do not infer a button layout, vibration behaviour, gyro support, cross-save path, or multiplayer mode from the five listed feature labels. Those are the boundaries that keep this guide useful while the unverified details remain unclaimed.
