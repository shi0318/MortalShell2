Rotten Autumn's highlight list says it includes polish and bug fixes. It does not reprint the October 13 hotfix. If a boss feels different from August, read the October 13 note before you thank a pumpkin helmet. A hat is not a nerf. A seasonal score is not a balance pass. Fifteen new dungeons are new rooms, not a retroactive change to a fight you already learned.

On **October 25, 2026** the US page was **$49.99** and Very Positive: **9,899** English reviews, **84%** of **1,186** in 30 days. That month contains both the hotfix and the seasonal pack. A review that says "they nerfed it" may mean either. Your patch list can separate them. The percentage cannot.

## Separate the notes

October 13 is the combat hotfix to reread if a named fight changed: projectiles, ally damage from a Tarstone combo, a Parasitic Stone exploit, and the other lines in that post. If your complaint matches a line in that note, you are on a combat patch. Install it. Retest the fight with the helmet off and the old score on, so the costume is not in the experiment.

October 20 is the content pack: photo mode, alternative soundtrack, 15 dungeons, shell shades, 10 Tarstones including a sheep-specific one, Nightmode presets, Overkill, a Harbinger pumpkin helmet, lute tracks, and a ghost with cryptic notes. Plus polish. Polish is not a published boss-health table. Do not invent one because the word appears.

If you change the helmet, the score, Overkill, and the dungeon in the same minute, you cannot tell which note you are feeling. The rule is boring and correct. One change. The hotfix first, if the fight is an old fight. The new dungeon second, on the old look. The costume third, in a cleared room.

## What the costume is allowed to change

Visibility. A pumpkin can hide the weapon arc you time parries on. A Nightmode preset can hide a windup you used to see. A louder score can sit under a tell. Those are reasons to take the toy off, not reasons to claim the October 13 numbers moved. Unequip. Retest. If the fight is still different, then you are allowed to talk about the hotfix.

The sheep Tarstone is an item, not a dungeon and not a hotfix. Read its text. Equip it alone on a known enemy. Unequip it if you are trying to judge an old boss. There is no sheep pen. The update said so in the same breath as the item.

## A clean blame

1. Update fully. An August install is neither note.
2. Load the old fight with no new cosmetic and no new Tarstone.
3. If it matches August, the hotfix did not change that swing. Enter one new dungeon.
4. If it does not match August, read the October 13 lines before the October 20 list.
5. Put the helmet on only after the comparison is written down.

**$49.99** does not include a personal patch historian. You are the historian for your own file. Very Positive reviewers are not inside your boss room. Harden is. Spend it on the swing you cannot read, on the build you have actually identified. The costume can wait in the menu. It has nowhere else it needs to be.
