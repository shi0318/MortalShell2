![The approach to Marrow Keep in Mortal Shell 2](/images/ms2-ss-15.webp)

You wake as the Harbinger. Harros is gone. **Marrow Keep** is the hub: the Gloom Siphon, Zhirelle for Shell swaps, Merrick for maps and **Mether's Severance**, and the Tarforge.

Siphon Ova here. About five more earns **Mether's Breath**, which is fast travel between cleansed beacons and the tool that clears corrupted growth. Do not walk the whole map before that unlock if a beacon is already cleansed behind you. [Map and fast travel](/mortal-shell-2-map-fast-travel/).

## One loop, then a gate

1. Talk to the keep NPCs before you pick a dungeon. Merrick's maps mark weapon pins if you would rather spend Coins than search.
2. Swap into the first Shell you actually found. Do not offer at Smert's altar as Harbinger. [First swap](/mortal-shell-2-first-shell-swap/).
3. Pick one gate. The six gates are not a forced order once Mether's Breath is up. One clear teaches the seal and the weapon better than three half-runs.

Gloom drops on death and can be picked back up. Do not treat a first death as a wiped file.
