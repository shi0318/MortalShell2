![A creature encounter in Mortal Shell 2](/images/mortalshell2-gloom-spider-creature.webp)

The line in the **Rotten Autumn** post is doing two jobs at once. Cold Symmetry says the update, live **October 20, 2026** on Steam, PS5, and Xbox, includes **10** new Tarstones and one of them is sheep-specific. In the next breath the same post says that despite whatever you have heard, there is no sheep dungeon.

Both sentences are the guide. The item is in. The joke dungeon is not. If you spend the evening looking for a pen, you are playing the rumor the developers already answered.

Mortal Shell II is two months old as of that post, **$49.99**, Mostly Positive at **17,558** of **22,172** reviews. A seasonal Tarstone does not reset that score and does not replace Harden. It is one passive in a pack of ten, attached to a free update that also added **15** dungeons, photo mode, shell shades, and a pumpkin helmet.

## What a Tarstone is allowed to change

Tarstones are passive effects on equipment. The highlight list does not print the sheep effect in words beyond "sheep-specific." So the honest guide is procedural, not a damage number.

When you find it:

1. Read the item text in the menu. The effect is whatever that text says on your build, not whatever a thumbnail guessed.
2. Equip it alone. The update added ten Tarstones. Stacking three new ones and then "feeling tankier" does not tell you which line fired.
3. Fight one pack you already know. A new dungeon plus a new Tarstone is two variables. Test the sheep effect on an enemy you can already parry.
4. Take it off if the effect fights the weapon. A passive that turns a riposte into a joke is still a passive you can unequip. Seasonal does not mean mandatory.

The Russian write-up of the same update said one of the new Tarstones turns your shell into a sheep. Treat that as a description of the joke item, and still read the in-game text before you build around it. A transformation that hides your weapon arc is a cosmetic with a cost. [Photo mode](/mortal-shell-2-photo-mode-overkill/) is where that cost is funny. A boss corridor is where it is not.

## Why the dungeon rumor sticks

Seasonal updates attract fake routes. "Sheep dungeon" is a better thumbnail than "sheep Tarstone." The studio answered it in the same post that announced the item, which is unusual and useful. They would not have bothered if the rumor was not already moving.

There is also a ghost of a lost shell that offers cryptic notes. Cryptic notes are how rumors start inside the game. If a note mentions sheep, it is a note. It is not a key to a dungeon the patch post said does not exist. Read it, screenshot it if you want, and go back to the room you were clearing.

Fifteen new dungeons are enough to get lost in without adding a sixteenth that is not there. [Pick one](/mortal-shell-2-fifteen-dungeons/) and write down the entrance. The sheep item will show up in a drop, a chest, or a menu you already have a reason to open. It will not show up because you circled a field.

## What else arrived in the same list

Keep the sheep in proportion. The same highlight list includes:

- Photo mode.
- An alternative soundtrack.
- 15 new dungeons.
- Shell shades for each shell.
- 10 new Tarstones, sheep included.
- Nightmode lighting presets.
- Overkill graphics settings.
- A Harbinger pumpkin helmet.
- An Impervious lute skin and more lute tracks.
- A ghost with cryptic notes.

If the sheep Tarstone is the only thing you came for, you are ignoring the part of the update that changes where you spend the evening. The dungeons are the content. The Tarstone is a passive you test in one fight. The helmet is a shot. The lute skin is audio. None of them is a hidden pen.

Achievement rates do not rescue the rumor either. Steam lists **53** achievements. On October 20, **039** was the thin one in the saved sample at **8.7%**, and **001** was **93.7%**. The Rotten Autumn post does not say "sheep dungeon achievement." If a new pop mentions sheep, it will name a Tarstone or a helmet, and you will see that name in the list after the update. You will not see it by wandering.

## A rule you can keep

Equip the sheep Tarstone when you want the effect the item text describes. Unequip it when you need to read a swing. Do not search for a sheep dungeon. The developers used a full sentence to say it is not in the update.

Harden is still for the attack you cannot read. A sheep shell does not parry for you. If the transformation makes the windup harder to see, take it off before the next elite and put it back on for the photo. That is the whole seasonal decision. The **15** dungeons are the rest of the night.

Keep the item in proportion to the rest of the October 20 list. Photo mode, an alternative soundtrack, shell shades, Nightmode presets, Overkill, a pumpkin helmet, lute tracks, and a ghost with cryptic notes all shipped beside the ten Tarstones. Any one of those can eat an hour if you let a thumbnail promote it. The sheep line is the one the studio bothered to deny as a dungeon, which is why it gets its own page. It does not outrank the fifteen rooms. Clear a room. Test the passive in that room. Photograph it if the mode holds. Then leave.

A second file is the right place for a full sheep evening, not the file you are using to learn the new dungeon entrances. Shades are per shell. The sheep effect, if the in-game text really does transform you, is per equip. You can have both, on a shell you already understand, after the entrance is written down. You cannot learn an entrance while you are laughing at the transform and missing the windup. The Mostly Positive score at **17,558** of **22,172** was not built on a costume. It was built on the fights. The costume is allowed to be funny after the fight.

If a cryptic note from the lost-shell ghost mentions sheep, archive the wording and keep walking. Cryptic is the adjective the highlight post already used. A note is not a key. A key would have been a dungeon, and the same post said that dungeon is not in the update. The Tarstone is the content they confirmed. Equip it, read it, and unequip it when the swing matters more than the joke. That is a complete use of the seasonal item. You do not need a pen to finish it.
