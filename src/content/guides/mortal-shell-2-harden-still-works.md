Harden is still the thing this series is about. A block that becomes a counter, not a stamina tax you hold until empty. People coming from the first game recognize it immediately. People coming from a parry-only soulslike treat it like a worse parry and then complain the game is unfair.

It is not a worse parry. It is the commit. You harden into the swing, you take the hit on the stone, you answer. Dodge is for the attack that tracks a harden. Riposte is for the health bar that Week 1 taught to respect enemy HP.

Proxima's Grafted Armor does not retire Harden. A 15 percent mitigation chance is a bonus on a hit you already decided to take. If you are inhabiting her and skipping Harden because the tooltip mentions stagger, you will stagger anyway the other 85 percent of the time.

Learn it on a normal enemy in the first dungeon, not on the last boss. The [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/) has the rest of the opening hour.
