**Harden** is still the verb Mortal Shell is built on. It turns your body to stone for a moment: the next hit lands on stone, you take nothing, and your swing continues. In Mortal Shell 2 it comes from **Vatra's Seal**, which you receive from Harros' stone form after the Tar Golem.

![A Shell walks a torch-lit village path toward a gate](/images/ms2-hot-1.webp)

## Harden vs the other Seals

| Seal | What the block button does | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Vatra's Seal | Harden – cancels one incoming attack completely, then cooldown | Poorly telegraphed or multi-hit attacks |
| Untarnished Seal | Perfect Guard – wide window, Break damage on success | Learning bosses |
| Infinite Seal | Parry – tight window, more Break; consecutive parries got a wider window on 6 September | Parry-heavy bosses |
| Slayer Seal | Sustain – restores health and energy on certain actions | Long fights (locks some achievements) |

## Why Harden is not a "worse parry"

A parry rewards you for reading the exact frame. Harden rewards you for **committing**: you can be mid-swing, Harden the counterattack, and finish your combo. That makes it the safe answer when:

- an attack hits twice (Sir Isaac phase two),
- a tell is too short to read (Hexapod),
- a stomp has an odd hitbox (Zmey's triple stomp – Harden the first, roll out).

## Common mistakes

- **Hardening too early.** It lasts a moment; press it as the hit arrives.
- **Skipping it on Proxima.** Grafted Armour only mitigates a hit 15% of the time. The other 85% you still need Harden.
- **Using it against grabs.** Grabs and red unblockable attacks are dodge checks.

## Where to practise

The prologue halberd knight and the lance knights on the bridge before Disciple's Grotto are designed for it. Once Vatra's Seal is yours, Magdalena's slow fire charges and Droeg's sweeps are good second tests.
