Shell progression in **Mortal Shell II** has three visible tracks: finding the eight playable Shells, watching their Shell Memories and raising a bond at the Shellkeeper. Those tracks are related, but they are not interchangeable.

## Shells, memories and bonding are different

Finding a Shell satisfies the collection side of the system. A **Shell Memory** is a separate story or progression entry, and the achievement **Seeking the Past** asks for all of them. Bonding is the upgrade relationship tracked by **Decked Out**, which requires maximum bonding for one Shell.

That distinction matters when a checklist says “finish a Shell.” First identify whether it means finding the body, watching a memory, or increasing the Shellkeeper bond. Treating those as the same objective creates unnecessary backtracking.

## A clean ability-testing route

1. Equip one Shell and keep the same weapon and sidearm for the whole test.
2. Enter a low-risk encounter where you can observe a normal hit, a guard, a dodge and a riposte.
3. Test one ability or bond change at a time.
4. Record the input, the animation, the recovery and whether the enemy can punish the end of the action.
5. Repeat after a patch before calling the behaviour permanent.

The game’s no-stamina-bar combat makes recovery and positioning especially important. An ability that looks strong in a menu can still be a poor fit if it leaves you in an unsafe animation.

## When to change Shells

Change Shells when the current problem is defensive rather than numerical: you cannot read a boss phase, you keep losing a long run on the return route, or a sidearm setup leaves no safe punish window. Do not swap just because another player calls one Shell “best”.
