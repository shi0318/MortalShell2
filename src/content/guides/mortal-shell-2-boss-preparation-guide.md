Before a **Mortal Shell II** boss attempt, the most useful preparation is not a guessed level number. It is a repeatable setup: one Shell, one weapon, one sidearm, a known Tarstone plan and a route back to the arena.

## The pre-fight checklist

- Confirm the Shell you can control under pressure.
- Equip the weapon whose recovery you can read after a light attack.
- Keep a sidearm for a situation where the boss leaves melee range.
- Check Tarstones and the Tarforge cost before spending the whole stock.
- Activate or identify the nearest safe return point.
- Decide which attack you will punish and which red or wide attack you will simply avoid.

Mortal Shell II has no stamina bar for attacks or dodges, but that does not make every dodge safe. Recovery, distance and the next enemy animation still decide whether a punish is real.

## Six Corrupted Gate bosses

The achievement record confirms six Corrupted Gate boss encounters as a meaningful completion group. It does not publish a universal recommended Shell, exact health values or a single reward table for all six. Treat each Gate as a separate timing test instead of copying one boss’s weakness to the next.

## The riposte-first plan

The Week 1 Update changed ripostes so player riposte damage scales with enemy health, keeps pace into NG+ and Night Mode, and uses more consistent invulnerability windows. That makes a clean parry plan valuable even when your weapon ranking is uncertain.

Spend the first attempt watching the normal attack. On the second, parry that attack and record the recovery. On the third, add one punish and disengage. This produces a better boss guide than a damage claim without a patch number.
