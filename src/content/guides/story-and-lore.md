## Short answer

You play **the Harbinger** — described in Cold Symmetry's own store text as *"a being prophesied to reclaim the blessed Undermether's stolen Ova from the daunting creatures that ruthlessly guard her unborn."*

That single sentence is the entire confirmed premise. Everything below unpacks it using only official wording, and clearly separates what the studio has said from what it has left open.

## Why people search this page

*Mortal Shell*'s first game was famously tight-lipped, and the sequel's marketing is written in the same register — evocative nouns, almost no exposition. Searchers want to know whether there is an actual plot, whether it follows on from *Fullgrim*, and what words like **Ova**, **Mether** and **Harbinger** mean. This page collects the official phrasing rather than inventing a wiki-style summary around it.

## The confirmed premise, term by term

| Term | Official wording | What it does and doesn't tell you |
|---|---|---|
| **The Harbinger** | "a being prophesied to reclaim the blessed Undermether's stolen Ova" | This is you. A prophecy exists and you are its subject. Nothing states your origin. |
| **The Undermether** | "the blessed Undermether" | A revered figure whose Ova were taken. The store text calls the unborn *hers*. |
| **Ova** | "stolen Ova… the creatures that ruthlessly guard her unborn" | Unhatched offspring, held by enemies. Recovering them is the stated objective. |
| **Mether** | Open Beta post: "recover the lost Ova for your **Mether**" | Used where the store page says *Undermether*. Read as the same being, shortened. |
| **Disciples** | Open Beta post: "discover your disciples" | You gain followers. Their function is not explained. |
| **False gods** | Short description: "dethrone false gods" | The antagonists are being framed as deities, not just monsters. |

Also worth noting the tagline the game leads with: **"Their Flesh Is Your Weapon."** That is a description of the Shell mechanic, not a metaphor.

## Shells are the story delivery system

The lore is not told through cutscenes so much as through the bodies you wear. Official text on this is unusually specific:

- **Dormant forms of forgotten warriors are scattered throughout the shattered world.**
- Once found, each Shell can be **awakened and possessed**, unlocking its innate strengths and unique abilities.
- You **unravel their secrets through the discovery of lost memories**.

So each Shell is a character with a past you excavate by playing them. That is why our per-Shell pages — [Tiel, the Acolyte](/tiel/), [Eredrim, the Venerable](/eredrim/) and [Proxima, the Broodseeker](/proxima/) — are the practical entry point into the narrative.

## Where the story takes place

The official description of the world is a list of places rather than a map, and the imagery is consistent — sacred things left to rot:

- **Derelict temples of ancient deities**
- **Forbidden forests** and **icy graves**
- **Citadels carved from bone**
- **More than 80 dungeons** scattered between those landmarks

Those regions connect over "winding footpaths guarded by the desperate and fanatical," and the reward for wandering is explicitly listed as **new story elements**, alongside Shells, weapons, upgrades and curiosities. In other words, exploration *is* the narrative pacing. See the [world map page](/map/) for the region layout.

## Do you need to play the first game?

**No.** Cold Symmetry call *Mortal Shell II* a **standalone sequel** in the first line of the store description. We cover the caveats — what a returning player will recognise, and what is genuinely new — on [do you need Mortal Shell 1](/do-you-need-mortal-shell-1/).

## The Open Beta's opening beat

The Open Beta covered the **Prologue plus an early open-world section**, and Cold Symmetry framed it in story terms: *"Your journey as the Harbinger begins now. Claim your first shells, discover your disciples, and recover the lost Ova for your Mether."* It included dungeons, mini-bosses and **"one truly formidable foe."**

That confirms the Ova hunt is the main quest from the first hour, not a late-game reveal. Details on the build itself: [Open Beta guide](/open-beta/).

## Confirmed vs not confirmed

- **Confirmed:** you are the Harbinger; a prophecy names you; the Undermether's Ova were stolen; creatures guard her unborn; you gain disciples; false gods are to be dethroned; Shells are possessed and hold recoverable lost memories; the world holds 80+ dungeons; the game is a standalone sequel.
- **Not confirmed:** who stole the Ova and why; what the Harbinger is; whether the Undermether appears as a character; how many Ova there are; whether the first game's characters or regions are referenced; and whether there are multiple endings. Cold Symmetry have published none of this.
