## When a ranged option is useful

Use range to control the opening exchange, tag an enemy while it is separated from a group, or finish a target without walking into a risky hitbox. It should support your melee plan rather than replace it. Keep enough space to recover after a shot and know what you will do if the enemy closes the gap.

## Build around the encounter, not a rumoured tier list

The official description says weapons have extensive upgrade options and that exploration can reveal new weaponry. Test one ranged option alongside a stable melee weapon. If you change your Shell and fragments at the same time, you will not know whether the improvement came from the weapon or the build.

The [Mortal Shell 2 melee and ranged weapons guide](/mortal-shell-2-melee-ranged-weapons/) covers the broader weapon split, while the [Mortal Shell 2 beginner combat tips](/mortal-shell-2-beginner-combat-tips/) covers dodge, parry, harden, and healing fundamentals.

No sidearm list, ammo economy, reload timing, or guaranteed ranged build is stated as fact. Those details need a repeatable retail observation or an official guide before they belong in a location or numbers page.
