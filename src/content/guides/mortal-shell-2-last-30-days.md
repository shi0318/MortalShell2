The last 30 days on the US Steam page, checked **October 25, 2026**, were **84%** positive out of **1,243** English reviews. The label was Very Positive. The price was **$49.99**, with no discount shown. The all-time English count on the same page was **9,890**, also Very Positive.

That month overlaps Rotten Autumn, which went live on October 20. It also overlaps the October 13 hotfix. A review dated sometime in the last 30 days might be about the new dungeons, the old combat, a crash, or a helmet. The percentage cannot sort those. Your install can.

## Do not credit the costume

The pumpkin helmet, the Rotting Christ score, the lute tracks, and photo mode shipped in the same list as 15 dungeons. If the month is Very Positive, the likely reason is the fights people already liked, plus free rooms, not the hat. Wear the hat after a room is clear. Take it off if it hides the swing you parry on.

Nightmode presets are a separate bullet. If you play with the sun off, test one preset on a road you know before you blame a new dungeon for being unreadable. Overkill is a graphics preset. Minimum spec still wants an SSD and an 8 GB card in the class the store listed. A stutter in a new room is more likely the preset or the stream than the seasonal album. Mute the album only after the frame is stable and the tell is still buried.

There is no sheep dungeon. A 30-day score will not create one. The sheep content is a Tarstone. Read its text. Equip it alone. Unequip it if it fights the weapon.

## What 84% of 1,243 should change

It should stop you treating the game as an unproven August launch. Two months of English reviews, and a recent month of 1,243, landed on Very Positive. That is enough to buy if the shell combat is what you want, at **$49.99**, without waiting for a sale the page does not show.

It should not stop you updating. An old build will not grow the 15 dungeons by itself. It should not stop you learning Harden. Very Positive players still die to swings they cannot read. The input has not been replaced by a helmet.

It should not start a completion grind you have not planned. **53** achievements exist. The recent reviews are not a route through the rare ones. Clear one new dungeon. Write down the entrance. Leave the other fourteen for a night when the first one has an exit.

The 30-day line is friendlier than a Mixed page and large enough to trust more than a 100-review week. It is not a walkthrough. The map is. One road, one new room, helmet off, old score until the tell is learned. Then the seasonal toys. The **$49.99** game underneath is what 84% of those reviews were willing to recommend.
1,243 reviews at 84% is large enough to trust more than a tiny week and not large enough to map 15 new dungeons for you. The month says the fights still convince English reviewers. It does not say Overkill is safe on a minimum GPU, and it does not say a darker Nightmode preset is fair until you test it on a road you already know. Leave the seasonal score off while you learn one tell. Leave the sheep hunt in the patch note that denied the pen. Then enter one dungeon. Write the entrance. The other fourteen can wait. $49.99 is the price of joining that month, not a coupon printed by the percentage.
