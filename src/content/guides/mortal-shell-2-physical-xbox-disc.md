If you are searching **“Will Mortal Shell 2 have a physical disc for Xbox?”**, the current official answer is: **no Xbox physical disc has been announced in the official sources checked on August 18, 2026**.

## What is confirmed for Xbox?

Playstack's release announcement says **Mortal Shell II launches digitally worldwide on August 20, 2026** for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Steam. The Xbox listing is therefore confirmed as a launch platform, but the announcement does not describe an Xbox retail disc edition.

That means an Xbox player should currently plan around a digital purchase. This page does not claim that a physical release is impossible forever; it records what Playstack and the official game site have announced so far.

## Is there a physical Mortal Shell II edition?

The official Mortal Shell website lists **The Revered Edition** as a physical collector edition for **PS5**. Its listed contents include:

- a physical artbook;
- a steelcase;
- fine art prints;
- digital extras.

The same official page currently says the edition is sold out in most territories. Availability can vary by region, so check the official purchase link before relying on a third-party stock page.
