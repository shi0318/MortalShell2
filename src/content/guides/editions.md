## Why this page ranks for buyers

Searchers looking for "Mortal Shell 2 Devout Edition", "Revered Edition", or "early access" need a clean comparison before pre-order. This page only uses official marketing language.

## Quick comparison

| Edition | Type | Confirmed perks (official wording) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (base game) | Digital / platform store base SKU | Full game on August 20, 2026 |
| **Devout Edition** | Digital deluxe | **Obsidian skin set** for all **eight playable Shells**; up to **72 hours advanced access** |
| **Revered Edition** | Physical collector (PS5) | Physical artbook, steelcase, fine art prints, digital extras |

Pre-order messaging on the official site also states that **two exclusive Harbinger skins** are rewarded to those who pre-order. Exact store SKU mapping can vary by platform storefront, so always verify the store page you buy from.

## Devout Edition

Official points:

- Transforms all **eight playable Shells** with the **Obsidian** skin set
- Pre-order / Devout buyers get up to **72 hours** advanced access before general release
- Aimed at players who want deluxe cosmetics + early start

If your main goal is simply to try the game now, use the free [Open Beta download](/download/) instead of waiting for early access.

For the Xbox physical-disc question, see [Will Mortal Shell 2 Have a Physical Disc for Xbox?](/mortal-shell-2-physical-xbox-disc/).

## Revered Edition

Official points:

- Collector physical package **coming to PS5**
- Includes physical artbook, steelcase, fine art prints, and digital extras
- Best fit for collectors, not required for gameplay access

## What editions do **not** change

Regardless of edition:

- Core story and open-world structure are the same game
- Open Beta inventory still **resets** at launch
- Platform availability for physical collector content can differ (Revered is called out for PS5)

## Release date reminder

Worldwide launch target: **August 20, 2026** on Steam, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.
