**Lazlo's Temperament** perk had an infinite glitch at launch: once triggered, it never expired. Cold Symmetry fixed it in the **14 September 2026 hotfix**. If your Lazlo build seemed to print resources forever, that was the bug, and it is gone.

![A Shell in heavy plate armour on a frosty castle road](/images/ms2-hot-7.webp)

## What Temperament is

Each Shell has a Temperament – a perk track that rewards a style of play. Lazlo's is built around his armour: he is the tank Shell with the highest base health in the game and a base 10% damage reduction (**Fortified Plate**) while his armour is on.

## Lazlo's kit in short

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| Retribution | Heats the armour for a Shockwave |
| Overheat | Repeated Retribution: stronger shockwave that inflicts Burn, but the armour is disabled for a while |
| Fortified Plate | 10% damage reduction while armour is on |

## After the fix

- Re-enter Lazlo and trigger the perk once. It should now end normally.
- If it still loops, you are on an older build – update the game.
- Builds that relied on the loop for permanent buffs no longer work; plan Overheat windows instead.

## Other Lazlo fixes

- **Week 1:** a softlock involving Lazlo's Rekindle and an armour loop.
- **14 September:** a bug that could trap players in the dungeon after claiming Lazlo.
- **24 September:** small parts of Lazlo's leg clipping through armour.

## The same hotfix also fixed

Proxima getting stuck in the Biosampler animation, Justiciar Stone durability, Gloombound Stone durability, the Egon's Stone Gloom exploit and dungeon loads, Hexapod's post-death dive, Starved Harridan getting stuck, and Zhirelle not talking in NG+.
